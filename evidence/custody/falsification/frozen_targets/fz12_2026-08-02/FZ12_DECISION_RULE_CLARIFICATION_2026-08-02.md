# FZ-12 decision-rule clarification

Created: `2026-08-02T12:37:49Z`

This append-only note corrects one quantifier in the frozen FZ-12 decision
rule. It changes no coefficient, angular template, physical premise,
admissibility condition, exposure exclusion, or failure threshold. The
original target, coefficient snapshot, source receipt, and registration
manifest remain byte-identical.

The original target classifies every null as inconclusive and then states
that a null cannot decide the branch without a source-derived lower bound on
the carrier scale. The intended combined rule is the following.

A null or non-detection is inconclusive unless a pre-exposure source theorem
for the same action and physical sector fixes `a_min > 0` in the comparison
units, and the preregistered sensitivity and remainder contract makes the
entire admissible linked manifold, including every `a >= a_min` in the
declared validity domain, excludable at five standard deviations or more by
the joint-likelihood rule. Without that lower bound and power calculation, a
null has no verdict. The mathematical premise `a > 0` alone is insufficient.

The positive-`C4` and lower-angular-rank failure rules do not require the
negative-`C4` trigger. The linked sixth-order rule does require a resolved
negative `C4` and adequate sixth-order sensitivity.

A qualifying failure rejects the seam-current edge propagation branch. It
reaches OPH as a whole only if a separate theorem proves that branch forced
and exclusive.
