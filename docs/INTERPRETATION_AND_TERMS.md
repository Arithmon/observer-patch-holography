# OPH interpretation and working terms

This reference distinguishes the proposed physical interpretation from the
objects and hypotheses of a particular theorem or executable model. The
[axiom reference](AXIOM_REFERENCE.md) remains the source for A1–A3; the
[agreement research foundations](AGREEMENT_RESEARCH_FOUNDATIONS.md) map the
established mathematical results used in model construction.

## One structure, two descriptions

OPH proposes that one complete structure is both a timeless solution of a
self-reference condition and a world that computes itself. Its proposed
realization is literal: observers reverse-engineer their reality's substrate
and construct hardware within it whose actual execution realizes that same
history, including its builders and executing hardware. Construction and
execution occur in emergent, mutually calibrated local times. No external computer or additional
time in which the whole history is played is supplied by this hypothesis.

The realization requires a specified map between the hardware's actual
execution and the represented history. Matching one actual history, preserving
a declared family of preparation and intervention responses, and encoding every
independently allowed microscopic state are different contracts. A passive
replay of one trajectory does not establish the stronger intervention-faithful
contract; this does not by itself reject an actual-history-only hypothesis.
Each candidate must state its comparison, observable scope and error budget.

The universe-level equation `T(U)=U` concerns a complete history and its
structural closure. This notation is schematic: a candidate must specify the
history domain and the action of `T`. It permits changing local states within
that history.
An actual record history or quantum process need not assign simultaneous
outcomes to every incompatible, unperformed measurement. Complete history
does not mean a noncontextual table of counterfactual classical outcomes.
Existence, uniqueness and physical realization of such a universe remain
hypotheses to investigate. The equation alone proves neither a final
instantaneous cosmic state nor that every mathematical fixed point is a world.
See [Essay B, §§6–7 and 9](../essays/B-why-does-anything-exist.tex) and the
[flagship's closure hypothesis](../flagship/from_observer_consensus_to_standard_physics.tex).

## Internal construction and execution

The live [observer implementation](../model/observer/README.md) constructs
finite read, retained-record and feedback functions. Its
[binary electromagnetic detector](../model/observer/p_response/README.md)
uses P as an accepted closure input and makes the detector outcome control
the next native repair. Apparatus and protocol are legitimate design choices
in this construction; self-consistency need not select one unique device.
Executable finite observer functions are therefore an achieved component,
while a physical realization containing the builders has the broader contract
below.

The builders' inference, device construction, program and data initialization,
controls, memory, read/write and record services, and clock comparisons must
be operations available within the candidate history. Its realization map must
account for their information access, execution delay and resources. Specifying a decoder
does not implement one, and initial data cannot conceal unaccounted access to
the desired answer. A mathematical correspondence also needs an explicit
physical identification with the history containing the builders and device.
These are open realization obligations, not new conclusions of A1–A3 or the
finite source models.

[Wolpert's self-simulation lemma, Lemma 2 in Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16050v5#S4)
is a relevant conditional possibility result. It assumes the Physical
Church–Turing thesis (deterministic computable universe dynamics) and the pristine reverse
Physical Church–Turing thesis. For a fixed requested finite model-time horizon
it supplies an initial computer program whose computation returns the
whole-system state at that horizon, including the computer; the program can
depend on the horizon and execution can finish later in model time. The model
allows arbitrarily extendible memory. It does not show that observers can
prepare the required initial state, that a finite device has sufficient
resources, or that the simulated and executing worlds are physically identical.
Nor does one program for each requested horizon establish one execution
realizing a whole ongoing history. The result therefore neither completes
OPH's construction nor supports a blanket impossibility claim about internal
self-simulation.

## Proposed primitives and emergent physics

The proposed starting objects are patches with local states, records,
interfaces and admissible operations. A1 supplies a particular mathematical
carrier and incidence structure; A2 constrains shared operational meaning;
A3 selects a state within its declared feasible set. Those supplied structures
and every further theorem premise must be counted.

Physical spatial position, metric geometry, durations, fields and matter are
intended to be readouts of a common realization. This is the programme's
emergent-physics thesis. It does not mean every current construction derives
those objects from the proposed primitives. A supplied graph, Euclidean field
domain, Lorentz chart, matter representation, action, layer time or control
clock is an explicit premise of that construction. A successful calculation
on such a background remains a conditional result until its source and
physical interpretation are supplied.

## Terms with different mathematical roles

| Term | Meaning and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Patch | A bounded local system with specified state, access boundary, records and operations. A graph vertex is a model representation; adjacency alone does not establish physical distance or causality. |
| Operational observer | A system or connected federation with the declared readback, record, feedback, prediction and continuation interfaces. Conscious experience is not part of this mathematical definition. |
| Conscious observer | An additional psychophysical interpretation or empirically supported instantiation of an operational system. Accessible records alone do not prove consciousness. |
| Meaning | The declared operational interpretation of data: probabilities, intervention effects and record values. It is not automatically Shannon information, subjective significance or an ethical ordering. |
| Compatibility | Independently specified agreement of the relevant restrictions or records. Its definition depends on whether the objects are assignments, relations, probabilities, operators or quantum states. |
| Repair residual | A discrepancy measured for a declared update or constraint. Zero update residual need not imply semantic compatibility; that correspondence requires proof. |
| Repair fixed point | A state `R(x)=x` of one declared map, on a specified full or reduced state space. It does not imply that hidden states, records or all other observables stop changing. |
| Public normal form | A terminal state or quotient record for a declared repair relation. Termination ensures every repair sequence ends; confluence gives the same endpoint from the same initial state. Identifying normal forms with independently specified compatible states requires an additional soundness/completeness correspondence. Uniqueness from one initial state differs from uniqueness across histories or boundary data. |
| Stationary distribution | A probability law preserved by an evolution. Individual realizations can continue to change. |
| Complete-history closure | A consistency or self-reference condition on an entire history. Its fixedness is distinct from fixedness of an instantaneous state or record. |
| Legitimate regime | An independently specified set preserved by the admitted dynamics. Recovery can enter a regime with continuing motion. Stability, convergence and termination are separate properties. |

Relational support compatibility, existence of a global assignment, exact
global extension of every local relation, and existence of a probability
extension are different tests. A classical counterfactual joint distribution
is also a different object from a quantum state with contextual measurements.
The [extension and lattice examples](AGREEMENT_RESEARCH_FOUNDATIONS.md#5-keep-relational-probabilistic-and-quantum-extension-separate)
make those distinctions explicit.

## Six uses of time

| Quantity | What it supplies |
| --- | --- |
| Host wall time | Runtime and resource cost of an implementation. |
| Solver/update counter | Position in a chosen search or execution schedule, including any rejected attempts or stutters. |
| Causal order or logical numbering | Precedence relations, such as authenticated read-from dependence; numbers respecting them add no physical duration. Physical causal identification is a separate map. |
| Model coordinate or lapse | A parameter used by a declared equation, chart, action or discretization. A shared layer parameter can be a legitimate restricted-model premise. |
| Modular parameter | The dimensionless parameter of an algebra-state pair's modular automorphisms. The geometric cap interpretation has its own branch hypotheses. |
| Operational clock or proper time | Observer-accessible readings tied to the model's observable evolution, with calibrated duration and, where claimed, a worldline/proper-time realization. |

Conversions between these quantities need explicit maps and error assumptions.
The target is compatible local clock readings and comparisons, without a
preferred universal clock, universal simultaneity or identical rates for all
observers. A common calibration within one experiment is legitimate. The
[scalar action clock](../paper/tex_fragments/SOURCE_SCALAR_CLOCK.tex) supplies
whole-layer durations in one benchmark; the
[count clock](../paper/tex_fragments/SOURCE_COUNT_CLOCK.tex) reconstructs
proper-time ratios under its order/count-limit and ancestry premises.
Experienced succession is a further interpretation, not a consequence of a
modular-flow or convergence theorem.

## Evidence and reuse

Self-stabilizing recovery, confluence and fixed sheaf diffusion provide
established tools with different hypotheses. Use the
[theorem mappings](AGREEMENT_RESEARCH_FOUNDATIONS.md) before asserting a new
convergence result. None selects a microscopic physical law just by sharing
its equilibria. Learning interfaces or changing a coarse description requires
checking the original meaning and preserved observables again.

An exact finite theorem establishes its mathematical conclusion under its
premises. A numerical check supplies bounded evidence about the tested model.
An interpretation identifies what that model is proposed to describe.
Physical support requires independently discriminating observations with
calibration, uncertainty and alternatives accounted for. OPH does not have
one common model established as physically correct.

Current summaries use these distinctions. Dated registrations, vendored
sources and hash-bound receipts retain their historical content; their
current scientific interpretation belongs in maintained guides and explicit
errata. Generated registers are refreshed from their source records.
