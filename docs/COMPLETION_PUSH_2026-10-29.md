## What is new about the field result

The retained computation uses 27, 125 and 343 spatial sites, respectively
54, 250 and 686 fermion modes. It evolves their exact invariant two-particle
sectors, with dimensions 1,431, 31,125 and 234,955; it does not replace
interacting evolution with Gaussian covariance propagation. The local
quartic term changes a Slater input into a non-Gaussian state. Complete
number/Ramsey settings reconstruct a Slater defect `sin²(1/4)` and a
connected four-point value `−sin²(1/4)/4`, with a free control.

At 343 sites, removing the interaction changes the full two-read law by
about 0.03265 in total variation. A sparse independent Hamiltonian provides
a numerical comparison. Separately, analytic splitting and finite-box
estimates control the ideal-source complete quantum output, including
untouched references and feedback, against the same fixed-spacing infinite
lattice. They do not certify floating-point or physical hardware error.
The larger-volume limit is distinct from a spatial continuum limit.

This supplies an interacting quantum-field benchmark in three spatial
dimensions with a declared evolution parameter. It does not yet satisfy
CP-06's same-process, chiral 3+1 physical-field contract. In particular,
retaining a single Weyl node while allowing local interactions would require
a justified removal/decoupling map for the other nodes; they are retained
throughout these executions.

## Actual joins and scoped exclusions

The [cross-lane tests](../model/completion/test_four_lane_joins.py) check that
the moving code, kinetic parent and residual-symmetry calculation use the
same endpoint weights, fixed scheduler and C/E mixture. The actual residual
port permutation preserves both the 66-state reference and kinetic parent.
That common symmetry does not make their generators identical.

An accepted finite-record point estimate has only the identity as its exact
port stabilizer in this retained sample, whereas the actual canonical law
has a two-element stabilizer. This is an uncertainty distinction, not
evidence that the true symmetry has disappeared. Exact equality of weights
cannot be promoted from a finite-sample clock fit. Another explicit test
shows that scalar clock compression on a moving code can coexist with
nonzero leakage out of its subspace.

Each exclusion retains its own quantifiers:

- The moving-memory stopping result covers the declared 60-code active
  service and fixed stochastic schedule, not arbitrary protected sectors.
- The kinetic obstruction covers the constructed stochastic-matrix parent
  and the fixed product reference, not other action/reference maps.
- The anomaly classification covers the chosen five representation blocks,
  with no extra cancellation fields or inflow. The family obstruction
  additionally requires a specified source-to-family attachment, matching
  left/right actions and a symmetry-preserving Higgs/vacuum and kinetic data.
- The fermion result retains the naive regulator's nodes and its supplied
  lattice/action. A clock obstruction under the stated occupation dictionary
  does not exclude nonlinear embeddings or additional interacting degrees
  of freedom.

The maintained completion registry records component progress, while
CP-12/CP-13 retain the scope and theorem-premise review. No integrated gate,
physical comparison or whole-program verdict is added.
