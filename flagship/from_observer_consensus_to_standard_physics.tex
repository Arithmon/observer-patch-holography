\begin{abstract}
Observer Patch Holography (OPH) proposes that physics is what bounded
observers can establish together: finite patches compare records on shared
boundaries, repair disagreements, and treat as objective whatever survives
every comparison. The universe, on this view, is a timeless self-referential
structure whose observers recover its specification from their records and
realize it, so that it reads itself into existence. Nothing lies outside to
impose premises, so the recovered specification owes no a-priori
justification; it has to run and reproduce what observers measure.

That specification is a candidate, an executable observer: a twelve-port carrier, a native
repair law, retained records and record-controlled feedback, joined in one
interacting quantum process of electric flux, charged matter and a scalar
field whose feedback response is certified nonzero in the infinite-occupation
limit. Finite theorems turn the specification's readouts into familiar
structure: schedule-independent public records, the Born rule and Tsirelson
bound on completed records, an intrinsic three-dimensional carrier, the
Standard Model gauge Lie algebra with an anomaly-free generation, a
source-derived $(1+3)$ causal precursor with proper-time ratios from record
counts, and the Einstein equation from a realized null balance. We state the acceptance criteria of the construction, which premises it
discharges, which it takes as declared inputs, and the frozen tests that can
reject its branches.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Physics usually begins with a world and asks how observers fit into it.
Observer Patch Holography (OPH) begins with observers and asks what world
they can establish together. Its observers are finite. Each holds a patch
of local state, reads a boundary it shares with its neighbors, keeps
durable records, and repairs its state when records disagree; it accepts
only repairs that preserve protected data. OPH identifies an objective
fact with a record that survives every authorized comparison. Quantum
probability, Lorentzian kinematics, gravitational dynamics and gauge
structure enter standard physics through distinct mathematical inputs
\cite{hardy2001,chiribella2011,masanes2011,jacobson95,dr90}; OPH asks
whether they are different public readouts of one finite observer
architecture. Space, time, matter and classical objects are effective
descriptions of what observers can measure and jointly interpret; no
observer-independent continuum is assumed beneath them.

The proposal about the whole is closure. The universe is a timeless,
self-referential structure whose observers recover the specification of
their own world from completed records and build hardware whose execution
realizes that same history, including its builders. The structure reads
itself into existence. This changes what a premise has to do. A theory set
against an external world must justify its postulates in advance or by fit;
a structure with no outside has no place from which a postulate could be
imposed. Whatever observers recover and implement therefore needs no
further justification: the carrier, the observer algebra, the repair law
and two closure coordinates are justified by being recovered, by running,
and by reproducing what observers measure. The same rule cuts the other way.
Nothing may be assumed that observers have not recovered, and every change
of description they can make must be implemented by the structure's own
operations. Agreement fixes what counts as shared, so ``our universe'' is
the world described by the public record. The executable specification is
where this account enters the loop; the loop itself has no first step.
Section~\ref{sec:strange-loop} develops the hypothesis and its consequences.

The programme's target follows from this reading. An executable
construction should contain observers who make the measurements we make and
who can use the effective continuum descriptions we use: quantum field
theory, classical mechanics and dynamical gravity, each in its regime. The
specification exists and runs. Its finite observer executes repair, writes
and retains records and chooses its next interaction from those records; an
electromagnetic detector evaluates the coupling from a closure coordinate
and feeds a single bit back; and one interacting quantum process joins
electric flux, charged matter and a scalar field with complete measurements
and a feedback response certified nonzero in the infinite-occupation limit.
Section~\ref{sec:selected} turns six readouts of the specification into familiar structure: schedule-independent public records, the quantum identities of completed records, three spatial directions, the gauge algebra with one anomaly-free generation, a causal precursor with record-count clocks, and the Einstein equation up to a cosmological constant.

\section{The observer}
\label{sec:architecture}

\subsection{Patches, records and repair}

\begin{definition}[Observer patch]
An observer patch is a finite object with local state, an observable boundary
map, durable records, readback, and a set of authorized repair moves. A
patch sees a fragment of the world: its own state and the boundary data of
its authorized overlaps.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Overlap and repair]
For patches $x_i$, $x_j$ with an authorized overlap $e=(i,j)$, both induced
boundary records must agree for the pair to hold public data. A repair move
changes local state while preserving protected readout. A configuration is a
normal form when no authorized repair applies.
\end{definition}

An observer in this sense is defined by its access and record interfaces.
It reads, writes and retains records, and it uses retained records to choose
its next operation. Nothing in the definition refers to a background space,
a global clock or a pre-assigned list of particles. Those enter later as
readouts that observers can share.

\subsection{The twelve-port carrier}

Each local carrier has twelve primitive boundary ports with oriented
icosahedral incidence: thirty seams and twenty faces, five seams at each
port, every seam on two oppositely oriented faces. Carriers federate through
typed seam algebras with coherent triple overlaps into a nerve carrying a
degree-one bridge to an oriented spherical support. The theorem library
calls this carrier, federation and response layer A1. Within the closure
hypothesis of Section~\ref{sec:strange-loop} it is the part of the
specification that observers recover from their records; every result below
displays its dependence on it.

Two exact features of the carrier enter later constructions. First, the
proper rotation group of the icosahedral packet is the alternating group
$A_{5}$, with sixty rotations acting on the twelve ports, and the real
port-coefficient space decomposes without multiplicity as
\begin{equation}
P_{12}\;\cong_{A_5}\;\mathbf 1\oplus\mathbf 3\oplus\mathbf 3'\oplus\mathbf 5 .
\label{eq:bands}
\end{equation}
On the six-dimensional golden sector the spectral projectors have entries in
$\tfrac1{20}\mathbb Z[\varphi]$, $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, each is the
entrywise Galois conjugate of the other, and both triplets are irreducible
real representations of real type; the projectors, characters and
irreducibility certificates are machine-checked
\cite{serre1977,ophrepo}. Second, record keeping is integer valued: a write
appends $+1$, a retraction appends $-1$, and readback sums atomic events per
port. A conservative repair transfers one unit across a seam whenever the
oriented mismatch has magnitude at least two. With $V(x)=\sum_ix_i^{2}$, each such repair strictly decreases $V$ by $2(d-1)$ for mismatch $d\ge 2$,
so repair terminates.

\subsection{The native repair law}
\label{sec:native-repair}

The executable observer runs on one carrier with twelve nonnegative integer
loads of total two. There are $78$ such configurations, and the $66$ binary
configurations form an invariant shell. For an oriented seam $(a,b)$ and a
fair placement coin $c\in\{0,1\}$, the native repair law, called C, moves
\begin{equation}
d=x_a-x_b,\qquad
j=\tfrac12\bigl[d-(2c-1)(|d|\bmod 2)\bigr],\qquad
y=x+j\,(e_b-e_a),
\label{eq:native-repair}
\end{equation}
so load is conserved and the signed current $j$ is the native observation.
Its quantum current instrument has Kraus operators
$L_{(c,j)}=2^{-1/2}\sum_{x:\,j(x,c)=j}|y(x,c)\rangle\langle x|$. A second
law, E, is C with one additional seam-parity record. Both laws have the same
diagonal classical repair statistics, and they differ in the coherence they
preserve: Section~\ref{sec:empirical} turns that difference into a test.
The quantum relaxation of the channels obtained by discarding the source record, the forgotten-source channels, is known exactly.
Both laws have quantum spectral gap $(5-\sqrt5)/60$ on the binary shell and
on the full carrier, their slowest binary coherences decay by the sharp factors $5/6$ per attempt
for C and $7/10$ for E, the binary-shell channel becomes entanglement breaking
after finitely many attempts ($134$ attempts suffice by an explicit
certificate), and on the full carrier it never does: an explicit partial
transpose stays negative at every attempt count \cite{ophmodel,ophmixing}.

\subsection{What runs}
\label{sec:observer-runs}

The specification is executable, and its components run with retained
receipts \cite{ophmodel}. The companion construction paper states each
component, its declared services and its certificates \cite{ophspine}; the
following summary gives the parts used in this paper.

\paragraph{An observer that remembers and acts.} The native observer
executes two repair events with a finite tape. It records the first current,
issues a forecast from that record, chooses the next seam from the record
alone, records the result and halts. Exhaustive checks retain all $264$
binary-source paths, and a $20{,}000$-trial seeded execution reproduces the
exact predictions. Record-controlled feedback gives final port-zero
occupation $35/264$, against $1/6$ when the selector is disconnected or
shuffled with the same action frequencies, so the response is caused by the
retained record and not by the action statistics. Feedback preserves the
committed classical record while it can reduce coherence between different
record values. Complete records of these short histories recover the source optimally, with entanglement fidelity $D^{-2}\sum_hw_h\operatorname{rank}(P_h)^2$ for the maximally mixed input, where $D$ is the number of carrier configurations, $h$ runs over the complete admitted histories, $w_h$ is the product of their seam-selection and coin probabilities and $P_h$ projects onto the inputs compatible with $h$; it equals $1301/2028$ for the full-carrier two-event C observer and $3329/6084$
for E under the same current-sign selector
\cite{ophrecovery}.

\paragraph{An electromagnetic detector driven by the closure coordinate.}
A second observer evaluates the coupling $\alpha(P)$ from the screen grain
$P$ and sets $g(P)=\sqrt{4\pi\alpha(P)}$. A prepared path qubit reads the
first current through a calibrated loop exposure $\chi$; the detector bit
$y=\pm1$ has probability $[1+y\sin(g(P)j_1\chi)]/2$ and alone chooses the
second seam, giving mean transferred charge
$\mathbb E[g(P)j_2]=(5g(P)/264)\sin(g(P)\chi)$. All
$528$ weighted histories are retained and a $200{,}000$-trial execution
agrees with the analytic response. The closure coordinate thereby enters an
actual response magnitude; the charge assignment, probe, exposure and
controller are declared apparatus.

\paragraph{Fields measured by the observer.} A scalar detector prepares a
signed field intervention from the first native current, reads the field with
a binary Weyl instrument and feeds the bit back, with inherited continuum
bounds transferred to the complete history law. A source-compiled two-site
field apparatus builds its entangled preparation from blank registers,
evolves the field and reads it with a quantum pointer, all from emitted
twelve-port control words; at the executed sixteen-level cutoff an exact
rational interval places the ideal plus probability in
$[0.51495,0.51789]$, excluding a fair detector.

\paragraph{One interacting process.} A common execution puts
quantum electric flux, a charged fermion pair and two neutral scalar sites on
one work register. Native source words implement their noncommuting
evolution, the matter and scalar pointer instruments, and feedback
controlled by a committed matter bit; the final scalar bit controls the next
native repair. A smooth extension retains four quantum reads on the same
disturbed state, compiles both native record laws around it, and actively
protects a logical qubit with record-controlled recovery. Its complete
conditional native/work/reference outputs are retained. In the original sharp
protocol, for zero native preparation current the scalar feedback
contrast of the same declared action has the certified infinite-occupation
enclosure $[-0.024045670143,-0.024043450955]$, including the original free
vacuum, unbounded interacting evolution and the actual coordinate half-line
detector \cite{ophinfinite}. Section~\ref{sec:measurement-process} develops
what this process says about measurement.

These executions are finite and their apparatus, services, field action and
preparations are declared. They discharge finite execution and continuation
under those contracts; autonomous unbounded observers, spatial refinement
and responsive gravity are not constructed (Section~\ref{sec:scoreboard}).

\section{What observers agree on}
\label{sec:consensus}

Two observers who share a boundary can disagree about what it shows. If
repair stopped in different places depending on who acted first, the
boundary would carry no fact of the matter. If it always stops in the same
place, the final record is a public fact that no single observer owns. This
section states when the second case holds, and then shows that completed
records carry the familiar quantum rules.

A completed finite configuration $x$ is a fixed point of the named
repair-and-agreement map,
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cons}}(x)=x .
\]
This map is
distinct from the universe-level closure operator of
Section~\ref{sec:strange-loop}. Fixed-point existence, observable
determination and schedule independence are separate questions, and the
following results isolate them \cite{ophnormalforms,ophconsensus}.

\subsection{Observable normal forms}

\begin{definition}[Observable quotient system]
An observable quotient system is a tuple $\mathfrak S=(Q,C,\mathcal B,B)$,
where $Q$ is a set of configurations, $C\subseteq Q$ is the consistent
subset, $\mathcal B$ is a set of protected records, and $B:Q\to\mathcal B$
is the record map. For $b\in\mathcal B$, write $C_b=C\cap B^{-1}(b)$.
\end{definition}

The fiber $C_b$ is unrealizable, reconstructing, or ambiguous according as
its cardinality is zero, one, or greater than one. This trichotomy avoids
assigning a state to a record that has either no consistent extension or
several observationally indistinguishable extensions.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical observable normalizer]
\label{thm:observable-normalizer}
The following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=1.6em]
\item $B|_C$ is injective;
\item there is a unique map $\mathcal N:Q\to C\sqcup\{\bot\}$ that preserves $B$ on
its $C$-valued outputs, fixes every point of $C$, is constant on each
$B$-fiber, and returns $\bot$ exactly on fibers with no consistent extension;
\end{enumerate}
If $B(Q)\subseteq B(C)$, the map is a total idempotent retraction $Q\to C$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Injectivity makes every nonempty $C_b$ a singleton, which defines $\mathcal N$ and forces all its properties fiber by fiber. Conversely, if $c,c'\in C$ have $B(c)=B(c')$, the fixed-point and fiber clauses give $c=\mathcal N(c)=\mathcal N(c')=c'$.
\end{proof}

The normalizer is defined by observable fibers, independently of any repair
schedule; a repair relation implements it when the relation preserves $B$,
reaches the consistent set and satisfies a liveness condition. Approximate
versions control the normalizer where exactness fails, through an optimal
two-output distance bound and summable naturality defects along refinement
towers. Canonicity carries no promise of cheapness: for succinct Boolean
boundary systems, deciding whether a protected record has a consistent
extension is NP-complete, and deciding injectivity of the record map is
coNP-complete \cite{ophnormalforms}.

For the canonical single-site repair, every adaptive run is eventually
constant because each nonstuttering attempt strictly lowers the mismatch
count; pathwise weak fairness makes the stable state a normal form, and
completeness with confluence sends every weak-fair run to the same public
fixed point. These results select no scheduler and provide no stochastic hitting law, rate, physical clock, or refinement theorem.
Attempts obey a sharp capacity law. A scheduler can delay the first genuine repair for any prescribed number of attempts. When every reducible record sees a genuine change among the next $q+1$ attempts, a bounded-waste upper horizon of $(q+1)$ times the initial mismatch count holds, and an independent-defect family attains the work-conserving case exactly at $q=0$.
The input-independent compiler produces a fixed federation with immutable input ports;
genuine node repairs decrease a dependency rank, and pathwise weak fairness
gives stable consensus and the correct output.
Emission-order round robin finishes within its first $n$ attempts, and the single-consumer structure bounds every accepted path by $n(n+1)/2$, with an emitted family attaining $\Theta(n^2)$.
General well-formed circuits instead admit $2^n-1$ accepted steps, attained by a fanout chain, where $n$ counts the emitted nodes.
These statements concern canonical node repairs: the weak patch-frame relation can stutter fairly forever, and no physical scheduler, clock, energy or hardware cost follows from these operation counts \cite{ophnormalforms,ophconsensus}.

\subsection{Public records from local repair}

For a prepared batch of support-closed transactions, join $\tau$ and $\sigma$
in the conflict graph when
$W_\tau\cap(R_\sigma\cup W_\sigma)\ne\varnothing$ or
$W_\sigma\cap(R_\tau\cup W_\tau)\ne\varnothing$, and iterate to a fixed
point: compute semantic support inside every aggregate, rebuild the conflict
graph on the expanded read and write sets, merge every connected component,
and repeat until the partition and all aggregate supports are unchanged.

\begin{proposition}[Transactional local diamond]
\label{prop:transactional-diamond}
Let an accepted repair be an aggregate transaction with read set $R_\tau$,
write set $W_\tau$, a read snapshot, and a snapshot-determined payload.
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=1.6em]
\item every read set contains the semantic dependency closure of its write
set, and every affected acceptance functional is revalidated at commit;
\item the terminal closure above exists, and each of its connected conflict
components has one coherent canonical aggregate payload;
\item a prepared component whose snapshot remains valid survives commits of
independent components; and
\item accepted commits preserve the protected record and strictly decrease
the integer descent functional.
\end{enumerate}
Then every one-step quotient peak $t\longleftarrow s\longrightarrow u$ has a
quotient state $v$ with $t\longrightarrow v\longleftarrow u$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Two different first steps cannot originate in one conflict component, since
that component has a unique aggregate payload, so they originate in distinct
components of the terminal closure. Rebuilding the graph after every
aggregate support expansion makes their final read and write sets disjoint,
so neither commit changes the other's read snapshot, and semantic dependency
closure ensures neither changes an acceptance functional consumed by the
other. Revalidation and the surviving component rule make both second
commits legal, and with snapshot-determined payloads and disjoint writes the
two application orders agree; protected-record preservation carries the
equality to the physical quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Consensus normal form]
\label{thm:confluence}
Suppose the accepted repair relation satisfies the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:transactional-diamond}, so that it has the one-step diamond property, strictly decreases the quadratic descent functional $V$ of Section~\ref{sec:architecture}, and is repair complete, meaning that its normal forms are exactly the consistent states. Every maximal asynchronous
repair schedule from one initial state then terminates at the same consistent
quotient normal form. The induced global repair map is idempotent, fixes
consistent states, and preserves the protected record.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Quadratic descent makes the accepted relation terminating.
Proposition~\ref{prop:transactional-diamond} makes it locally confluent, so
Newman's lemma \cite{newman42} makes it confluent. A terminating confluent
relation has a unique normal form below each source, and repair completeness
puts that normal form in the consistent set. Applying the normalizer a second
time does nothing, and protected-record preservation holds along every
accepted edge.
\end{proof}

Atomic commits, disjoint write sets, or termination alone do not imply the
local diamond; countermodels omitting semantic dependency closure, canonical
aggregation or the surviving component rule are retained with the finite
receipt. This is the precise sense in which a public fact is what survives
every authorized comparison: the public object is the protected quotient
record, independent of the order in which observers repaired.

The law runs at scale. The public run uses the level-six cell rung of the
geodesic icosahedral refinement tower: $81{,}920$ exact twelve-port carriers
with one declared gluing of ports to neighboring cells, $2{,}580{,}480$
seams in all, and the seam-mean retraction as repair primitive. Sixteen
shuffled schedules from one seeded load terminate at the balanced class with
no accepted move raising $V$, and all sixteen return one quotient hash equal to the component multiset fixed by the initial totals.
A standalone verifier that imports no simulator code rebuilds the component
multisets from the primitive arrays, replays one schedule from its seed and
recomputes every hash \cite{ophsim}. The level-ten rung, with $20{,}971{,}520$ carriers and $660{,}602{,}880$ seams,
terminates all sixteen schedules at the balanced class in $84$ to $100$ sweeps
with one quotient hash fixed by the loads. Across the rungs from five to nine, one initial load per rung gives an excess-energy contraction between $0.81$ and $0.83$ per sweep, and the sweep counts to the balanced class grow slowly with the carrier count, from $51$ to $79$ at level six to $84$ to $100$ at level ten; these finite readings prove no all-size mixing or termination-time bound. On the sixteen level-six schedules the number of unit transfers to the balanced class lies between $924{,}832$ and $925{,}708$, about $1.25$ times half the initial $\ell_1$ deviation of the loads from their mean. The first-settling configuration retains
spatial information that its public component multiset does not encode: at
level ten the initial-to-terminal amplitude responses of equal-count
ancestral groups of sizes $1{,}024$, $4{,}096$ and $65{,}536$ are $0.90758$,
$0.95232$ and $0.98983$, with conditional schedule-noise fractions
$1.241$, $0.594$ and $0.143$ percent. Stationary continuation adds a
constructive fluctuation result: for fair integer repair on a connected
weighted graph with independent edge clocks of total rate one the fixed-occupancy law is reversible, centered loads have lagged covariance $\kappa e^{-|t|L_G/2}\Pi$ in the edge-clock time $t$, with $L_G$ the weighted graph Laplacian,
$\kappa=r(p-r)/(p(p-1))$ for $r$ raised occupancies on $p$ ports and $\Pi$ the
projection off constants, and fixed linear readouts obey a martingale central
limit theorem \cite{ophMicrophysics}.

The same records let observers recover the specification itself.
At carrier scale, recovery from event logs under the declared probe and
operation grammar returns the twelve ports, thirty seams, inverse pairing,
automorphism and rotation groups of orders $120$ and $60$, mean rule,
schedule, rank-three Gram and Lie split $1+3+8$.
Re-instantiation preserves the invariant vector for two iterations, and

\section{Closure: a universe that reads itself into existence}
\label{sec:strange-loop}

The previous sections built observers, their agreement and the structures
they can read off, and they stated the executable specification as a
candidate with a scoreboard. This section returns to the proposal stated in
the introduction and develops it in full. It explains why the specification
is exempt from a-priori justification and why the theory has no first
premise, although its exposition has an entry point.

\subsection{The closure hypothesis}

The universe is modeled as a timeless, closed, self-referential structure in which the description and the described system are one object.
Let $\mathfrak U$ be a complete inhabited history, let $D(\mathfrak U)$ be an
account of its architecture recoverable through completed records within it,
and let $R$ be an admissible realization of that account constructed inside
$\mathfrak U$. The closure condition is
\begin{equation}
R\bigl(D(\mathfrak U)\bigr)\;\cong\;\mathfrak U ,
\label{eq:closure-condition}
\end{equation}
where $\cong$ is agreement in the observable and interventional structure:
the records used to recover the description, and the responses of the
realization to every admitted intervention, fit the world from which the
account was recovered \cite{ophessayB}. Written as a fixed point of a
universe-level closure operator, the condition reads
$T(\mathfrak U)=\mathfrak U$. Observers infer the specification of their
world, build hardware within it, and run that hardware; its execution
realizes the same history, including its builders and their hardware. The
identification of the construction-side and inhabited-side readings is the
hypothesis. In this sense the structure reads itself into existence.

No external computer runs the history and no additional time exists in which
the whole is played. Repair order is a partial order on commits, an observer
history is a chain of completed records, and that order is invariant under
strictly increasing regradings, so affine time and proper time require their
own calibration. Fixedness of the complete history permits changing states
within it: the equation concerns the whole history and identifies no last
instantaneous state with the universe.

Self-reference earns content only when the return condition restricts the
possibilities. On the real line $x\mapsto x+1$ has no fixed point,
$x\mapsto x$ fixes every point and $x\mapsto(x+2)/2$ fixes exactly one. A
candidate therefore has to specify the history domain and the action of $T$;
existence, uniqueness and the identification of our universe with a fixed
point are questions the equation leaves to the programme. Two finite results give the
condition exact content on declared branches. The packet-quotient closure
theorem proves that on the declared finite consensus branch the closure map
is an affine idempotent whose fixed points are exactly the packets supported
on consensus normal forms \cite{ophconsensus}. The universe-level
record-closure equation $N=\log M_0(\mathfrak U_N)$, with $\mathfrak U_N$
the regulated observer system at capacity budget $N$ and $M_0$ the
correctable-code capacity of its terminal public record, is posed exactly,
and the all-rung arithmetic proves that the bounded completion class defined
by base agreement, positivity and the carrier bound does not identify it
\cite{ophobservers}.

The executable observer gives the condition its finite meaning. A
realization differs from a description because it carries the described
dependencies under intervention: for a described update $\tau_a$, realized operation $F_a$ and fixed readout $p$, the one-step condition $p(F_a(s))=\tau_a(p(s))$ extends by induction to every finite admitted sequence
\cite{ophessayB}. A playback can match every frame of one history and fail
this test as soon as a retained state is changed. The controls of
Section~\ref{sec:observer-runs} perform exactly this test on the running
observer: disconnecting the detector or shuffling its bit with the same
frequencies removes the feedback response, while the record-controlled
execution keeps it.

\subsection{No outside}
\label{sec:no-outside}

A theory of an external world must justify its postulates by an argument
given in advance or by fit to data gathered from outside the theory. A
self-contained structure offers no vantage point from which a premise could
be imposed. This changes what a premise has to do, and it gives one rule
with three applications.

\paragraph{Justification.} A premise is justified when observers recover it
and the specification that implements it runs and reproduces their
measurements. The carrier, the observer algebra, the repair law and the
closure coordinates are found by observers inside the structure and built
by them; asking for an additional a-priori reason would ask for a view from
outside, which the hypothesis denies. Construction choices therefore need no
uniqueness theorem. What they owe is execution and agreement with what
observers measure, which is the content of Section~\ref{sec:scoreboard}.

\paragraph{State selection.} The rule also forbids assuming what observers
have not recovered. After every observer-visible constraint has been
imposed, the selected state may carry no further information relative to the
recovered reference, so it minimizes the weighted relative entropy
$\sum_pw_pD(\rho_p\Vert\tau_p)$ over the compatible local state families of the patches $p$.
The theorem library calls this premise A3. Its finite objective has exact
consequences: every minimizing local density has the union of the feasible
supports, and a feasible witness with failure probability $b>0$ gives an
explicit floor on the selected probability \cite{ophEinstein}.

\paragraph{Transformations.} Every change of description that observers can
make must be implemented by the structure's own operations, since nothing
else could implement it. On a carrier this means that each proper recharting
is realized from inside the complete reversible response. The library calls
this the endogeneity clause of A2, and it carries real weight:
Theorem~\ref{thm:inner-action-closure} uses it to exclude the only other
twelve-dimensional compact type and to force
$\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)$.

\subsection{Agreement and the meaning of ``our''}

The rule says what needs no outside justification. Agreement says what
counts as shared. Observers assign the same operational meaning to accepted
data on shared boundaries, naturally under every restriction, recharting and
refinement, and a record is public when it survives every authorized

that this public record describes. Agreement in this sense is a definition
and an acceptance criterion: the shared-meaning row of
Table~\ref{tab:acceptance} requires interpretation maps fixed independently
of validation outcomes and preserving the declared joint and conditional
statistics, including quantum correlations. It does not require a
noncontextual table of outcomes for incompatible unperformed measurements.

The basis reference states the hypothesis, the rule, the condition and the
specification formally, with the library's premise statements and their

\subsection{An entry point without a starting point}

The exposition of this paper enters the loop at the executable
specification. That choice reflects where observers stand, holding records
and inferring, and it makes the account concrete. The order of
justification runs the other way: the hypothesis comes first, since it is
what exempts the specification from outside justification. The structure
itself has no first element in either sense. A pair of mutually determining
equations such as $x=(y+2)/2$, $y=(x+2)/2$ fixes $x=y=2$ with no first value
from which the other is manufactured; a calculator may approach the pair by
iteration and someone may find it on a particular day, and neither belongs
to the reason the answer is two \cite{ophessayB}.

No temporal paradox arises. The closure equation relates parts of one
timeless object, with no global time coordinate along which information
travels backward. Observers recover the specification and later construct
it in their own record order, and both stages belong to the same fixed
point. Repair descent is a well-founded order on authorized updates used to
prove termination and compare schedules; it is neither an observer's
experienced time nor a universe-level clock.

\subsection{Propagation anisotropy at angular rank six}
\label{sec:spin-six}

Angular rank means spherical-harmonic rank $j$, not a particle spin. The dimension of the invariant subspace of the sixty proper icosahedral rotations in angular rank $j$ is
\[
m_j=\frac1{60}\Bigl[(2j+1)+15\,U_{2j}(0)+20\,U_{2j}(\tfrac12)
+12\,U_{2j}(\tfrac\varphi2)+12\,U_{2j}(\tfrac{\varphi-1}2)\Bigr],
\]
with $U$ the Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind, and the exact table
gives $m_1=\cdots=m_5=0$ and $m_6=1$ \cite{cohan1958}.

\begin{theorem}[Icosahedral angular-rank universality]
\label{thm:spin-six}
Let the carrier kinetics be any operator
$\lambda_a(k)=a^{-2}\sum_d w(d)\,[1-\cos(a\,k\!\cdot\!d)]$ with a finite
direction multiset and weights invariant under the proper icosahedral
rotation group. Then the dispersion is exactly isotropic through angular rank
five at every order in $a$, every directional term below angular rank ten is
one multiple of the unique normalized invariant $\mathcal I_6$, and whenever
the weighted sixth-moment coefficient is nonzero the first directional
artifact appears at order $a^4k^6$, suppressed by exactly $1/16$ under one
binary refinement at that order. Rank six is the least symmetry-allowed
nonzero anisotropic rank in the class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The order-$a^{2m-2}k^{2m}$ angular content is an invariant polynomial of
degree $2m$ with harmonic components at even ranks $j\le2m$, and the
invariant table forces zero at $L=2,4,8$ and a one-dimensional space at
$L=6$. The table, the $62$-direction stationary census, the orbit
verification and the nonvanishing sixth-moment coefficients are certified in
exact arithmetic \cite{ophrepo}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Primitive twelve-port branch prediction]
\label{prop:spin-six-prediction}
Suppose the intrinsic spatial kinetic symbol lies in the cosine class of
Theorem~\ref{thm:spin-six} with scale-independent coefficients, the complete
orbit of the twelve port directions $u_i$ is its sole hop support through
order $k^6$, the carrier scale $a$ is finite and positive with the quadratic
term normalized to $k^2$, one carrier rest frame is transported coherently
over the experiment, and the tested scalar or polarization-independent sector
realizes the operator. Then
\begin{equation}
\omega^2
=\frac{1}{2a^2}\sum_{i=1}^{12}
 \bigl[1-\cos(ak\,u_i\!\cdot\!\widehat k)\bigr]
=k^2-\frac{a^2}{20}k^4+\frac{a^4}{840}k^6

\label{eq:primitive-port-expansion}
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal I_6(n)=\tfrac{25}{132}\sum_iP_6(u_i\!\cdot\!n)$, so in a
transported frame the branch predicts the scale-free relations
\begin{equation}

\frac{B_0}{C_4^2}=\frac{10}{21},\qquad

\section{Discussion and comparison}
\label{sec:discussion}

Operational reconstructions derive quantum structure from information and
composition principles \cite{hardy2001,chiribella2011,masanes2011}. OPH makes
a narrower statement at the quantum stage: it takes the finite algebra-state
representation as an input and contributes the completed-record surface on
which the event, conditioning and correlation identities operate, together
with the consensus theorem that defines completion. Quantum Darwinism
locates classical objectivity in redundant environmental records
\cite{zurek2003}, and the completed-consensus record plays the same public
role with redundancy replaced by protected overlap agreement and an explicit
repair dynamics. The cellular-automaton programme shares the finite substrate
\cite{thooft2016}, while OPH locates the quantum identities on the completed
record surface. Wigner's-friend extensions constrain agents reasoning about
one another's unfinished measurements \cite{fr2018}, whose premises concern
pre-consensus perspectives that the completed surface does not represent.
Relational quantum mechanics treats physical facts as relational
\cite{rovelli1996} and the thermal-time programme reads time from modular
structure \cite{connesrovelli1994}; OPH attaches public facts to protected
overlap records, and its executable observer reads modular flow from the
actual observation state of a running process. The local-diamond proof sits beside asynchronous agreement \cite{lamport1982,flp1985}, and
Dijkstra's self-stabilization separates recovery from continued legitimate
operation \cite{dijkstra1974}.

The gravitational branch belongs to the thermodynamic and
entanglement-based routes to geometry \cite{jacobson95,jacobson16,vanraamsdonk2010},
with Bisognano--Wichmann modular flow and half-sided modular inclusions as
established analytic ingredients \cite{bw75,bw76,wiesbrock93}. The route from
a null-projected balance and the Bianchi identity to the full equation, with
$\Lambda$ as an integration constant, is familiar from trace-free and
unimodular formulations \cite{vdbij1982,ellis2011}; what is added is the
finite nine-direction design with exact constants and the premise contract
(G1)--(G6) stated as one implication. The gauge section uses
Doplicher--Roberts reconstruction for the sector group \cite{dr89,dr90} and
contributes the forcing of the local Lie type from complete response and
internally implemented rechartings.

The closure hypothesis has precedents of a different kind. Wheeler pictured
a participatory universe as a self-excited circuit \cite{wheeler1983}.
Tegmark's mathematical-universe hypothesis identifies physical existence with
mathematical structure \cite{tegmark2008}, and Nozick proposed that a
fundamental principle might subsume itself \cite{nozick1981}. OPH adds two
demands: the self-subsumption must be inhabited, carried out through records
and agents inside the structure, and it must be testable in part, through an
executable specification whose observers can be run and whose instruments
make predictions. Lawvere's fixed-point theorem shows why unrestricted
self-representation fails \cite{lawvere1969}; the closure condition is
restricted to the observable and interventional structure that observers can
recover.

A useful comparison of unification proposals asks how much physical
structure follows from each declared input and which observations
distinguish the resulting models; OPH offers a concrete case with proofs and
executable checks attached to individual implications. Formal verification
measures deductive reliability, predictive success requires physical
realization and eligible measurements, and neither theorem counts nor
numerical coincidences define a probability that an architecture describes
nature.

\section{Conclusion}

OPH proposes that physics is what bounded observers can establish together,
and that the universe is the structure in which such observers recover their
own specification and realize it. Because nothing lies outside that
structure, the specification owes no a-priori justification; it has to run
and return the world it was recovered from. The specification runs. Its
observer repairs, records and acts on its records, its detector draws its
coupling from a closure coordinate, and one interacting quantum process
carries flux, charged matter and a scalar field through complete
measurements with a certified feedback response.

The finite theorems turn the specification's readouts into familiar
structure: public records independent of the repair schedule, the Born rule
and the Tsirelson bound on completed records, an intrinsic carrier with three
directions, the
Standard Model gauge Lie algebra with an anomaly-free generation, a
source-derived $(1+3)$ causal precursor with proper-time ratios from record
counts, and the Einstein equation from a realized null balance. The prospective content is the frozen dispersion band of Theorem~\ref{thm:carrier-class-band} and Proposition~\ref{prop:spin-six-prediction}: a resolved intrinsic dispersion with $C_4\ge0$, with $B_0/C_4^2<10/21$, with $B_6/B_0$ outside $[-16/135,16/75]$ or with a nonzero coefficient at angular ranks one through five excludes every member of the declared carrier class at once; and the C/E instrument test, whose decision rule is fixed before any independent count.

The acceptance criteria state what is missing: a spatially refining family
of interacting observer processes whose statistics converge to a
$3+1$-dimensional quantum field theory with the observed matter, and
responsive geometry with calibrated clocks on the same histories. The five
maps of Section~\ref{sec:standing-maps} are consumed rather than
constructed, so these results do not establish that the architecture
describes our universe. They specify what a realization must preserve, and
the executable observer is the construction that has to carry it.
