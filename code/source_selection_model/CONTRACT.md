# Source selection: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Derive M1's operational causal/count limit from A1--A3, or state one explicit
additional source law and prove that it suffices for the complete limit,
including finite preparations, source record histories, native causal order,
population, interval and pair counts. Exact Fibonacci sampling and a unique
microscopic radius are not required: M1 may be a regulator with a proved
operational comparison. A countermodel alone does not meet this objective.

## Deliverables

1. An explicit finite-algebra model, with primitive central ports, typed
   seam algebras, operational instruments and an all-level spherical tower.
2. A same-source twelve-dimensional response and endogenous proper chart
   implementers. Check observable response faithfulness, ordered response
   closure, all proper actions and composition, without a spectator or
   a named Lie type supplied to the source constructor.
3. A complete local constraint grammar, exact reference/cover/weights,
   unique finite Umegaki optimizer and refinement of instruments and records.
4. An all-program information theorem, including arbitrary local retained
   history and stopping, and an explicit comparison with M1's required
   intervention-preserving remote reads. Keep host counts distinct from
   logical population encodings.
5. Compact reproducible response and record tapes, independent strict
   verification, hostile-input tests, and a Lean standard-axiom audit.
6. A clause-by-clause account of the analytic model interpretation. Do not
   claim that finite receipts or typed Lean shadows formalize the full axioms.
7. The complete proposed RG source law, with its substantive storage,
   response-natural transport, clock, composability and native realization
   requirements explicit. Derive its read relation from primitive flights,
   then join it to the covering and count theorems; do not assume an M1 menu.
8. A source-to-native-order sandwich including controller and auxiliary
   events, uniform timing error, localized initial and intermediate record
   interventions, all resource costs
   and an explicit comparison of M1 with another regulator of the same law.
9. Reproducible reference processes with every declared initial and first-layer
   commit intervention retained,
   an independent semantic checker, hostile-input tests and proof audits.

## Exit

The scientific exit is either an unconditional derivation or the sufficiency
theorem for one fully stated source law. The result here takes the second
route in RECORD_GLUING.md. A negative model explains the need for additional
operational information but is not, by itself, the exit. Its full-clause
membership argument remains independently necessary for the non-entailment
claim: finite checks cannot repair a failed membership proof.

For the negative construction, the theorem must identify the absent
record-to-seam channel and demand-selection requirement. It must not claim
that M1 is impossible on every allowed source or that a positive physical
M1 derivation from A1--A3 has been completed. RG is proposed, not added to the
three core axioms, not proved minimal or uniquely necessary, and not verified
for the registered simulator. Its primitive reference execution is labelled
as such. It is not acceptable to present native realization as already
established or to hide another operational premise outside RG. The previous
W12 routing and amplitude results retain their source/preparation domains.
