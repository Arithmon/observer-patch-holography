# From the registered simulator operations to numerical records

## Source and objective

The object studied here is the registered all-port capture in
`oph_fpe/bulk/physical_h3_kms_source_capture.py`, together with
`oph_fpe/core/echosahedral_dynamics.py`. It is not every simulator engine.
The group-valued production repair and the shared-vertex W12 routing fixture
are different interfaces. Their updates must not be imported into this
capture's alphabet without an explicit composition theorem.

The deliverable is the operation-to-payload map of this executable, its exact
real arithmetic counterpart, and its information and propagation limits.
No successful-history constraint, metric neighbor list, golden population,
or chosen target radius enters the extraction. A fixed seed and configuration
give one executed history, not an A3 optimization problem.

The companion simulator exporter executes both `_source_dynamics` and the
complete `capture_physical_source` driver. Their repair commitments must agree.
It exports the complete repair-log commitment, post-propagation ledger,
seams, scheduler permutation, terminal ledger, and observer zero's actual
numeric RECORD_COMMIT payloads. The independent standard-library verifier
replays every attempt, including no-ops, reconstructs every committed event
and its hash, and derives the observer values and feedback-selected ports.
It imports no simulator producer or numerical library. Live reproduction
additionally checks the captured source bytes and the complete-driver binding.

## 1. Which operations are actually composed?

For N even, each carrier has twelve complex coordinates. The initial
normalized complex-Gaussian vectors undergo a configured number of local
unitaries U(t)=exp(-itL), where L is the icosahedral graph Laplacian. The
driver then sets x_(c,p)=|psi_(c,p)|^2. All of these unitary steps occur
before the repair loop. During that loop the complex recurrent state is
unchanged; scalar repair modifies a separate visible ledger.

For each port p, a seeded perfect matching pairs the N carriers. Ports zero
and one use alternating matchings of one carrier cycle; other ports use
separate permutations. Thus the carrier graph is connected, but the graph
on the 12N *port slots* is exactly 6N disjoint pairs. No repair operation
transfers a ledger value between two ports of the same carrier.

In each cycle the scheduler visits k=ceil(6N/16) consecutive entries of a
fixed cyclic seam permutation. A selected pair (a,b) is skipped when
|x_a-x_b|<=epsilon, where epsilon is the actual binary64 value of `1e-15`.
Otherwise both entries receive the same computed mean and their versions
increase. Snapshots copy selected carriers' full twelve-entry ledgers.
The later observer loop reads these retained snapshots, commits numeric
records, and uses those records to choose its next displayed port. It does
not apply that feedback to the earlier repair or complex-state evolution.

The terminal complex lift, sqrt(x)*exp(i arg psi), is an exported projection
of the joint hidden/visible state. The source itself explicitly withholds a
physical complex-repair lift receipt. It is not a derived quantum instrument.

## 2. Complete finite repair family

Let mu be the fixed-point-free involution pairing slots. For a union A of
complete pairs define the ideal real operator

    (P_A x)_i = (x_i+x_mu(i))/2 if i belongs to A; x_i otherwise.

Then

    P_A P_B = P_(A union B),    P_A^2=P_A,    P_A P_B=P_B P_A.       (1)

Proof: on a pair visited by neither operation both entries are unchanged;
on a pair visited by one they are averaged; a second average of two equal
entries changes nothing. `SourceOperationReads` kernel-checks this identity,
its idempotence and commutation corollaries, and the resulting all-word
formula. Traversal order and repetitions cannot add a new dependency.

The complete monoid generated by these primitive real operations has
exactly 2^(6N) elements. Every subset of pairs is realizable by visiting it.
Distinct subsets act differently on a vector with unequal values on a pair
in their symmetric difference. Such a witness can also be chosen among
positive per-carrier normalized preparations: change one selected coordinate
and compensate at another port of that carrier. The selected pair reads
only the first coordinate and its partner. After every pair has been visited,
the real terminal projection has rank 6N on the ambient 12N ledger space.
This rank is not a claim about the dimension of its normalized input image.

The actual thresholded floating-point maps also commute and are idempotent
on finite nonnegative bounded inputs: each pair is disjoint, a skipped pair
stays unchanged, and a committed pair has bit-identical endpoints thereafter.
Their maps need not be linear. The rational rank statements concern the
ideal real operators; the native binary64 maps are replayed separately.

The cyclic scheduler visits every seam within ceil(6N/k) cycles. There can
be at most 6N committed transactions, each reading version zero and writing
version one. All subsequent attempts are no-ops. This proves saturation for
every duration and preparation, rather than inferring it from the earlier
single-log antichain control. Counts include attempted no-ops.

The primitive alphabet permits the complete finite word family, while the
registered driver fixes one word by its configuration. Laws on arbitrary
words are an intervention extension; they are not the driver's A3 family.
Even without restricting to successful words, every member has form (1).

## 3. Exact read algebra, including normalization

Give a receiver carrier its complete initial twelve-entry ledger and every
later local value. This is an upper bound on the information in its actual
snapshot payloads. If its pair is visited, an exact retained initial value
and the mean recover the remote partner:

    x_remote = 2*x_after - x_local_before.                         (2)

The resulting maximal read algebra is generated by the receiver's initial
coordinates and the initial partner coordinates of visited incident pairs.
There is no path through a second seam: (1) proves dependence only on the
original pair, for every finite word. The Lean dependency theorem includes
arbitrary words; it is not a bounded enumeration statement.

Normalization supplies additional information. At the post-unitary cut,
the ideal preparation domain is the product of N twelve-coordinate
probability simplexes. If eleven coordinates of another carrier are known,
its twelfth is known from their sum. If two or more coordinates remain
unobserved, none of them is uniquely determined in the interior: transfer
delta between two such coordinates while keeping all observed ones fixed.
This gives the exact coordinate-identifiability criterion, not just a
nonzero-dependency graph.

For one carrier after full coverage, the observation rank on the normalized
preparation tangent is exactly 23, independent of N>=4. The receiver provides
eleven independent initial values and twelve external partner values. The
alternating carrier matchings at ports zero and one give different partners,
so no external carrier supplies all twelve values; any at most eleven
coordinates of its simplex are independent. The hidden dimension is
11N-23: respectively 21, 65 and 153 for N=4,8,16. Independent rational
elimination verifies these ranks and the full coordinate closure. A separate
control checks the easily missed eleven-known-coordinates case.

For the *actual* observer zero, the source chooses two support carriers and
emits six full-C12 records. Its record cycles and carrier choices determine
rows of the same operator (1). The verifier reconstructs those rows, their
rank on the normalized preparation space, and the actual committed
repair-to-record edges. A visited no-op supplies no committed writer; its
ideal operator row and actual version provenance are kept distinct.
Feedback's next-port function is recomputed from the actual rounded values;
it adds no new numerical source input. The larger maximal read algebra of
both support carriers provides a conservative impossibility test even if
extra initial samples were made available. Actual snapshot times depend on
the total run duration; they are not falsely identified with prefix samples.

Equation (2) is an ideal real result. For a committed native mean on inputs
in [0,1], a conservative binary64 absolute mean error is 2^-52, hence the
inverse using exact representations of the stored floats has error at most
2^-51. The observer also rounds each record to fifteen decimal places;
including initial and final decimal rounding and their binary64 storage
gives the conservative bound 2^-50+3/(2*10^15). Each stored rounded value
contributes at most 1/(2*10^15)+2^-53; the three storage-error terms plus
the mean inverse bound fit within 2^-50. A skipped pair instead yields only
|x_remote-x_local|<=epsilon, not an exact equality or a committed read.
No fixed physical precision or robust arbitrary-depth decoder follows.

## 4. A positive failure effect and its exact scope

For a receiver support, choose two unobserved slots p,q on one remote
carrier. The controls explicitly find such a pair. Starting from every
coordinate 1/12, set

    x^+_p=5/48, x^+_q=1/16;
    x^-_p=1/16, x^-_q=5/48.

All other coordinates agree. Both preparations are strictly positive and
normalized. Their maximal receiver payloads agree under every common repair
word, although the required p value differs by 1/24. These values can be
prepared before the ideal local unitary by U*sqrt(x); no false assertion that
a particular integer PRNG seed realizes an exact rational witness is needed.
The witness belongs to the stated open preparation interface of the native
operations, not necessarily to a fixed seeded run or the full A1/A2 family.

Encode a hidden bit by these two preparations, keep the executed schedule
independent of that bit, and assign each bit probability 1/2. Every receiver
decoder, including a randomized one, then has error probability exactly
1/2. On the finite classical algebra of the bit and decoded bit, the diagonal
effect E_bad=1_{decoded != bit} is positive. A certain read of both values
requires error zero; this explicit family violates it. With an arbitrary
prior giving both indistinguishable preparations positive mass, optimal
error is their smaller mass. A source-dependent schedule or public seed that
reveals the preparation is extra information, outside this observation map.

The finite classical support theorem in `code/source_read_selection/` applies
only after this effect has a positive pullback to an actually scored
algebra and this family is admitted by the complete A1/A2 constraints.
Neither identification is supplied by this simulator. We therefore prove
failure in the named operation/preparation interface, not a countermodel of
full A1--A3 or a failure floor for every possible source model. The fixed
seeded driver itself has one trajectory, not a faithful convex ensemble.

There is a further concrete boundary in the actual observer JSON:
`source_state_root` hashes global preparation and dynamics material and is
included in record identities. A global hash can distinguish the two worlds
even when every local numeric payload agrees. The controls explicitly check
this distinction. Our observation map concerns numerical payloads and their
locally computed feedback, not the complete archival JSON including global
custody metadata. A proof that such global metadata is locally obtained
would be a separate communication mechanism, not evidence for (2).

## 5. Can the intensity ledger support recurrent propagation?

Not by itself. Take adjacent ports a,b and the two normalized states
(|a>+i|b>)/sqrt(2) and (|a>-i|b>)/sqrt(2). They have identical intensities.
After the actual U(t), their a-port probabilities differ. Writing
U_aa=A+iB and U_ab=C+iD, their difference is

    ((A-D)^2+(B+C)^2)/2 - ((A+D)^2+(B-C)^2)/2
       = 2(BC-AD).                                               (3)

At zero, its derivative is -2 because L_aa=5 and L_ab=-1. Since
||L||<=10 and ||rho_+-rho_-||_1=2, the absolute second derivative is at
most 2*(2||L||)^2=800. Taylor's theorem therefore gives a probability-gap
lower bound 2t-400t^2. At the exactly representable configured step
t=1/131072 this is 16359/1073741824>0. The numerical source execution and
an independent rational matrix Taylor enclosure both check the witness.
The polynomial and positivity arithmetic are kernel checked; the matrix
norm/Taylor argument is analytic.

Thus no deterministic map, nor a Markov kernel determined only by current
intensities, reproduces the source's next intensity statistics for every
allowed complex preparation. Restoring propagation requires the retained
phase/coherence information or an additional instrument specification.
Simply alternating scalar ledger repair with a guessed intensity transition
would change the model. This result does not claim that a full quantum
instrument cannot be constructed.

There is also an exact distinction between generator adjacency and a finite
unitary step. If d is graph distance, (L^k)_ij=0 for k<d and
|(L^d)_ij| counts shortest paths. The first nonzero exponential term has
modulus |(L^d)_ij|t^d/d!. For 10t<1/2, the remaining tail is bounded by
2(10t)^(d+1)/(d+1)!. The icosahedron has d<=3; at the declared step the
first term exceeds this tail for all 144 entries. Every finite-step port
amplitude depends on every initial port amplitude of that carrier. A
five-neighbor generator is not an exact five-neighbor finite-step read law.
The verifier also encloses the degree-eight exponential and checks the
executed matrix with relative error bounds, so tiny far-port entries cannot
be forged to zero under a loose absolute numerical tolerance.

## 6. The observer feedback is not a protected equivariant port selector

The actual observer function chooses

    next(p,x) = (p+1+abs(round(10^6 sum_j (j+1)x_j)) mod 11) mod 12.

For the uniform full-port snapshot and p=0, the weighted sum is 13/2,
so the next port is 2. A source-generated order-five incidence automorphism
fixes port zero but moves port two. It leaves the whole uniform input and
current port unchanged. An equivariant function would therefore have to
return an output fixed by that automorphism; this one does not.

The exporter executes the native observer loop on a controlled uniform
snapshot and obtains the actual next port. This is an admitted input of that
function, not a claimed output of a particular PRNG seed. The independent
checker verifies the permutation, its order five, all incidence relations,
and the unequal transformed output. An order-five automorphism of the
icosahedron is orientation preserving, so reflection is not the cause.
The violation concerns this feedback selection rule; it does not contradict
the covariance of the different local Laplacian unitary.

Consequently this next-port field cannot be promoted to an A1-protected,
presentation-natural selector on the stated input without additional
structure or a different instrument. A5 covariance of one propagation
operator does not establish covariance of the complete record process.

## 7. Population, propagation, and refinement

The source population N is an input (even, for the matchings); it is not a
derived golden q^3 population. There are 12N ledger slots, 6N seam pairs,
at most 6N committed repair events and no committed repair-to-repair edges.
The complete primitive propagation stage is local to each carrier and
precedes repair. Joining its output to repair and the actual snapshot reads
does not create recurrent cross-carrier transport after that cut.

The topology and repair schedule do not use `support_refinement_level`.
Holding N and seed fixed while changing that level from one to two gives
the same ledger operations and payloads. Extending repair time beyond the
coverage threshold gives only no-ops. Neither operation refines a source
spatial read graph. Increasing N rebuilds the seeded matchings; no carrier
coarsening or commuting operator square is supplied by that construction.
These are statements about the registered driver, not about every possible
refinement map or the separate shared-vertex W12 family.

No source radius is extracted here: the actual read relation is specified
by paired port identity and visit history, and the local unitary has dense
finite-step support. Identifying either relation with Euclidean ball reads
would require additional structure. Larger runs of this driver cannot
silently discharge that missing identification.

## 8. Axiom and exit audit

| Interface | What this source supplies | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| A1 local carrier | Twelve amplitudes, icosahedral incidence, local unitary, numerical readback and repairs | A C12 amplitude vector is not the canonical central-record algebra; coordinate projectors are not central in the full matrix algebra; the next-port rule fails the tested presentation covariance |
| A1 complete response | An executable Laplacian unitary; a separate ordered-port diagnostic | Completeness/closure of the canonical faithful twelve-dimensional response tangent; the obvious phase-kick extension generates u(12), not twelve dimensions |
| A1 federation/refinement | Explicit all-port seams, support descriptors and sampled snapshots | A commuting refinement of this operational read map and the required degree-one, record-preserving source attachment |
| A2 | Concrete record recomputation and selected interface consistency controls | Every canonical accepted-record constraint, or an endogenous holonomy identification for these operations |
| A3 | A configured initialization and deterministic scheduler | The complete convex feasible family, positive scored failure pullback, exact canonical reference/cover/weights, or a source-selected temporal instrument |

The finite operation-to-read task exits with a complete algebraic transition
classification, native and observer replay, normalized recovery criterion,
explicit failure witnesses, phase-closure obstruction, and measured source
population/refinement behavior. Full M1 would additionally require canonical
source-family membership and instrument/refinement selection. This work
neither assumes them nor transfers them into an alleged completed theorem.
The new simulator contribution adds extraction and verification; it does not
alter frozen dynamics to manufacture the target geometry.
