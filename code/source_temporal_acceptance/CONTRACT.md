# Temporal acceptance from source observations

## Objective

Derive the complete linear acceptance and continuation grammar of a finite
scalar source interface from its native observations. Construct complete
native recovery from connected topology, with termination and work bounds.
Determine exactly when a constrained A3-selected classical history law
admits that recovery. Derive minimal direct-read requirements from public
meanings and examine whether these source constraints select M1's metric
neighbourhood and particular record meaning.

The input is a linear preparation family, supported scalar pair-mean
histories, local sample ports and a fixed public record meaning. Observation
forms are generated without that meaning or an expected payload value.
The full A1 architecture, its accepted data domain and its source-selected
A3 cover/reference are not instantiated by this finite interface.

## Deliverables

1. Kernel proofs that agreement on every finite observation fiber is
   equivalent to kernel containment, linear-span membership and existence
   of a linear decoder. Derive the possible-observation relation from the
   preparation, rather than assuming an identification certificate.
2. A kernel proof identifying these observation forms with actual native
   pair-mean execution, including the common baseline. Derive prefix
   monotonicity, earliest sound publication, forced abstention before that
   prefix, presentation covariance and a coefficient-based sample-error bound.
3. An exact A3 criterion: under attained convex finite classical KL
   minimization with positive weights and faithful reference, certain
   publication is equivalent to observability on every history admitted by
   any feasible law. Produce a source perturbation witnessing each failed
   linear identification. Full-history atoms are scored; a merely
   state-determining coarse cover does not inherit this statement.
4. Derive the available individual-record menu from observation kernels.
   Prove that direct reads determine a specified linear sum exactly when
   every nonzero coefficient's record is included. Retain a kernel-checked
   aggregate observation that determines a sum but neither summand.
5. Exact independent replay on captured W12 seams and exhaustive finite
   attempt-word controls. Check both decoder and null-witness certificates,
   every prefix, native basis responses, signed interventions, complete
   stopped-law normalization and unconditional work. No postselection.
6. A kernel theorem that a full-state erasure before any discriminating
   receiver sample prevents recovery under every common continuation,
   including infinite words. Check the concrete initial erasure on all
   15,360 captured ports and distinguish individual records from their sum.
7. A kernel construction of a native calibration plan on every finite
   connected graph. Prove that one receiver's samples identify all initial
   scalar states, with N samples and at most N(N-1)/2 means. Derive the exact
   continuation criterion from the joint kernel of past samples and current
   physical state, and compose it with constrained KL selection.
8. A complete topology-only calibration plan for all 15,360 captured ports,
   independently validated for supported simple paths, fresh sources,
   calibrated relays, one receiver, complete coverage and native work.
   Replay all five basis states and three additional states on a captured
   five-port path; decode using receiver samples alone.
9. A kernel theorem for arbitrary nonlinear meanings on finite independent
   product records: isolated coordinate interventions determine the unique
   minimal sufficient direct-read set. Audit radius-selection arguments
   against the canonical source obligations without substituting a cost
   objective, an imposed public meaning or successful-word postselection.
10. A maximal preservation guard derived from the current record/state
    kernel, with a proof that the topology-derived completing word is
    admitted from every protected checkpoint whose retained record determines
    its current receiver scalar (`HasRoot`). Initially sample the receiver;
    each admitted mean records its updated sample. Prove completion after an
    occurrence among arbitrary other proposals and the exact finite
    full-proposal failure count. Independently replay the guarded attempt
    census, charging rejected proposals and retained samples explicitly.

## Exit

Every added theorem passes the transitive standard-axiom gate. Source pins
cover the complete local Lean import closure and producer/verifier contract.
Verifiers reject forged decoders, zero or visible ambiguity witnesses,
omitted prefixes, future-sample substitutions, wrong temporal order,
altered source samples/writers, understated error amplification, missing
abort costs and scope promotion. The retained evidence is compact; long
attempt-word enumerations are regenerated from commitments.
Continuation verifiers additionally reject unsupported/repeated paths,
uncalibrated relays, remote samples, missing ports, forged tree commitments,
altered receiver samples, incorrect work and promotion of topology evidence
to payload replay. Every theorem in this package is included in the transitive gate.
Guard tests reject an admitted erasing move, future-sample publication,
deleted rejections, forged response rows and omitted rejected-proposal costs.
Certificate helpers reject ragged matrices and inexact or boolean entries.
An explicit two-port control checks why the initial receiver sample is needed.

## Scientific boundary

The matrix grammar is complete for all real linear meanings on the stated
linear preparation space. The continuation factorization theorem separately
covers arbitrary meanings of a scalar state plus retained records. The
minimal direct-read theorem covers arbitrary meanings on finite independent
product records. These statements do not establish grammar completeness for
the full nonlinear/quantum A1 architecture. The finite replay preparations
are controls, not the domain of the general native completion theorem.

The available-record menu is derived. Its equality to a metric ball of
radius L/sqrt(q), the requirement to publish every such record, and the
one-plus-sum meaning are separate source-selection obligations. A required
meaning is a theorem argument; it is not obtained by optimizing over
unrelated menus. The direct-read theorem permits additional reads and does
not select a unique route, order, radius or memory representation.

The toy law is the uniform law on all finite attempt words, conditional on
the complete-alphabet/full-simplex/counting-reference premises of
RepairWordSchedule. Acceptance is a deterministic observation map of that
law. Failed attempts remain in its probability and work census. No altered
entropy problem restricted to successful words is called the original law.
The guarded experiment applies the proved deterministic guard to that full
proposal law. Rejected proposals stay in its attempt and controller census,
while executing no scalar mean. The uniform/IID proposal premises remain
explicit; canonical A3 alone does not supply them or the physical guard.

Preparation, allowed moves, sampling, retained observations, arithmetic,
controller realization and error limits are supplied. Exact rational
certificates prove information recovery; attainable precision and sampling
cost are not derived. No native implementation of the decoder arithmetic,
large-plan payload replay, physical clock, volume measure or complete A1--A3
derivation of M1 is asserted. PR-52 retains its classification.
