# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Decide whether a sparse read law preserving the leading crossing count of
fixed smooth sections also preserves area as a variational observable over
actual finite site partitions. If it does not, construct and prove a repair
without selecting edges by the desired outcome or fitting entropy weights.

The spaced-ball comparison is also introduced in `code/m1_necessity`; no file
or theorem from that package is required here.

## Deliverables

1. An explicit microscopic partition with the same finite read dictionary
   that defeats compactness and, in the clipped box, beats the continuum
   isoperimetric value at its limiting volume. Check whether connectivity
   alone removes the defect.
2. A fully specified sparse read family with one nat per unordered crossing
   pair and the original leading coefficient for regular interfaces.
3. A sharp threshold for compactness within the specified short-ball repair
   class, proved from translation estimates rather than assumed.
4. A full perimeter limit: compactness of bounded-entropy partitions, the
   lower bound for arbitrary convergent partitions and a binary recovery
   construction, including exact finite volume constraints.
5. Convergence of finite minimum values and near-minimizers. In a fixed
   interior cube with enough room for the prescribed volume, identify the
   limiting shape and its area constant.
6. Retain the explicit local causal/count construction on interior,
   non-wrapping diamonds and positive free-field
   limits, account for every added read, and distinguish fixed-population
   incidence savings from equal-accuracy or native resource savings.
   Analyze the uniform read action itself: identify the critical extra-mode
   limit and prove their suppression for the explicit repair, without an
   additional specially weighted nearest-neighbor action.
   Check the fixed-zero exterior field boundary potential separately from
   the internal reads; a clipped graph Laplacian is a different operator.
7. Independent finite cut replay, complete named catalogs, hostile controls,
   proof/source custody, claim registration and publication.

## Exit

Supply a counterexample and a constructive replacement theorem. The repair
must prove the compactness and perimeter conclusions for its defined graphs;
they may not be hypotheses or filters used to accept successful graphs.
The all-level variational proof is analytic. Executed finite comparisons
and kernel-checked finite reductions must be identified separately.

The comparison concerns a declared crossing statistic and coordinate volume.
It does not identify a physical entropy, select a horizon, derive black-hole
dynamics, implement a native source, or discharge a physical M1/RG premise.
The original fixed-section theorem remains valid within its stated scope.
