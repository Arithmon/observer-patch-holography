# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Remove the many-particle restriction of the coherent source clock by
constructing its fermionic operations from the existing proper-code source.
An assertion that a qubit swap is a fermion swap, or that an abstract CAR
lift is automatically a local source operation, is not a derivation.

The starting source is the explicit completion in COHERENT_SOURCE_CLOCKS.md,
including its stated CCG requirement. No extra reversible response or new
transport axiom may be inserted. Standard fermion encodings must be credited.

## Deliverables

1. Specify a finite local encoding of the even CAR observable algebra on
   every particle-number sector, including both parity blocks. Prove its
   algebra relations, dimension, locality and independence of ordering
   conventions. Check against an independent occupation-basis construction.
2. Prepare the constrained encoding deterministically, with all measurement
   branches and corrections retained. Supply explicit native circuits for
   its rotations, occupation instruments and a non-Gaussian density
   interaction. Do not grant an unexamined encoded gate set.
3. Compile the actual two-mass tetrahedral walk, including fermionic flight
   signs and blank-bank restoration, on a finite spatial workspace. Give a
   uniform finite bound on local degree, circuit depth, flight time, native
   pulses and code events. Respect one processor per private interface.
4. Transfer the named all-state CAR read bound and the clock detector to
   that construction. Charge preparation, measurement and reporting
   latency. Keep the fixed accounting observable distinct from physical
   energy and free evolution distinct from the interacting extension.
5. Decide which noise bounds survive encoding. Exhibit actual vacuum
   failures of independent physical dephasing and give a valid finite
   error budget. A one-particle noise bound cannot be copied to a constrained
   gauge encoding without proof.
6. Provide compact reproducible receipts, independent verification, hostile
   input tests and source/claim custody. Separate all-size arguments from
   small executions and reject false sign, parity, loop, timing and noise
   evidence.

## Exit

A complete source realization of the parity-preserving fermionic clock
process at arbitrary occupation, with executable interacting and measurement
controls, and a charged finite schedule. Neither code preparation, fermion
signs, physical locality nor the detector may remain a premise for a later PR.

CCG remains the existing additional transport requirement. The result does
not select empirical masses, an interaction coupling, a laboratory energy
standard or a unique microscopic source from A1--A3. It does not identify
odd CAR fields with commuting local qubit observables.
