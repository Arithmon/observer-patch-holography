# Scrutinizing record gluing through local clocks and classical information

## Result and logical status

RG, proposed in [RECORD_GLUING.md](../source_selection_model/RECORD_GLUING.md),
contains distinct assumptions about information, geometry, and implementation.
It is consistent as a classical process theory, and retained classical copying
is compatible with twelve-central-port matrix algebras. Neither statement is
a construction of a source satisfying every A1--A3 clause. In particular,
operational completeness of the response and an all-level source-support
completion require more than a circuit or a collection of completely positive
maps. This work does not declare a fourth core axiom.

There is a substantive reduction: **finite rotation covariance, a regular
local clock, and time-faithful refinement imply the same limiting cone**.
Exact scale covariance and a continuous orbit of primitive flights can be
removed. Classical finite-function programmability reduces to a fixed finite
gate basis with clean auxiliary registers. Independent preparation, cofinal
workspace, and localized record interventions remain separate obligations.
These are mathematical implications of stated premises, not observations of
the simulator. No numerical success filter defines the admissible family.

The target is the operational causal/count limit, with finite M1 populations
and radii serving as regulator choices. It is not uniqueness of a Fibonacci
cutoff, a preferred microscopic radius, or the claim that every execution
performs an exhaustive observation. This distinction follows the existing
source-net/count results on `main`.

## The reduced local-clock principle

Let E be the intrinsic three-dimensional simple response ideal, with its
positive trace inner product. Identifying affine E with operational positions
is an additional physical attachment. The following hypotheses define LC;
none is implicit in the phrase "a channel exists."

**LC1 (local information).** Arbitrarily large finite collections of independent
classical bit registers, blank auxiliary registers, NOT and Toffoli
gates, retained versions, preparation, discard, and waiting are available at
one position. Each finite program, including its independent concurrent
branches, has an exact finite realization. Auxiliary registers and all native
events are counted. Local processing has zero ideal time or cofinally
vanishing positive overhead uniformly over the whole finite program.

**LC2 (local clock).** In a neighborhood of zero, every sufficiently short
displacement v has a retained-record flight of time ell(v), independent of
origin and transported carrier chart. Here ell(0)=0, ell(v)>0 for v!=0,
and the squared latency has a nondegenerate quadratic expansion

    ell(v)^2 = Q(v) + o(||v||^2),   Q positive definite.

The latency, not merely an internal matrix, is invariant under the proper
carrier rotations. For the isotropy proof it suffices to use the tetrahedral
subgroup generated by the coordinate half-turns and the cyclic coordinate
permutation. The little-o is uniform in direction, as follows from a genuine
second-order expansion at zero. Directionwise limits alone do not suffice.
Local directional coverage is an independent premise: finite rotations of a
finite set of directions do not create flights in every direction. The
reduction removes continuous *rotation symmetry of the operations*, not this
coverage requirement. A finite directional stencil alone generally gives a
polyhedral effective norm.

**LC3 (complete, time-faithful refinement).** All native dependencies,
including payload, control, scheduler, timing and metadata, have the same
position and nonnegative elapsed-time grading. Local operations and waits
have zero displacement. Every native dependency path with endpoints
x,y and elapsed time T has refinements into small local flights/waits/operations
with maximum displacement tending to zero, the same endpoints, and total
path time tending to T. Each local primitive flight of displacement v costs at
least ell(v). A composite delivery may be faster than the one-step primitive
with the same net displacement; the path inequality retains all its steps.
Realizations preserve exact values and interventions at every designated
intermediate output before publication, with all other existing records and
caches fixed. Arbitrary finite concatenations and concurrent programs of local
flights have compatible finite realizations. Their additional overhead can be
made arbitrarily small **for the whole finite program**. This last quantifier
cannot be replaced by a fixed error per gate or per layer.

LC3 is a restriction on every admitted source influence, not just on flights
chosen by a compiler. It forbids a hidden fast coarse channel. Refinement is
required for old programs; a new fine program using a new independent input
need not descend to an old coarse program. Assuming the latter would contradict
LC1. The finite source cutoffs may change with the experiment; an infinite
translation group does not act faithfully on one finite address set.

### Theorem 1: finite symmetry fixes the quadratic clock

Choose an orthonormal icosahedral frame and write Q(v)=v^T H v with H symmetric.
The quadratic form inherits the latency's symmetry: for every carrier rotation
R and fixed u, Q(Ru)=lim_(s->0+) ell(s R u)^2/s^2
=lim_(s->0+) ell(s u)^2/s^2=Q(u).
Invariance under diag(1,-1,-1) kills H12,H13. Invariance under
diag(-1,1,-1) kills H23. The cyclic permutation of the three coordinates
equates the three diagonal entries. Thus H=tau^2 I for tau>0. These rotations
preserve the twelve vertices cyclically permuted from (0,+/-1,+/-phi), so
they belong to the proper carrier group. Only this finite subgroup is used.

It follows uniformly that ell(v)=tau||v||+o(||v||). Indeed divide the squared
expansion by ||v||^2 and use continuity of the positive square root at tau^2.
For every epsilon>0, sufficiently small v satisfy

    (tau-epsilon)||v|| <= ell(v) <= (tau+epsilon)||v||.

The exact symbolic certificate checks the invariant quadratic-form equations
and that the rotations preserve the port set. The universal argument above
does not depend on sampling directions.

### Theorem 2: time-faithful refinement supplies the global upper bound

Take any native dependency path of time T and net displacement v. Fix
0<epsilon<tau and refine until all flight displacements lie in the preceding
neighborhood. By the triangle inequality, its refined time T_n satisfies

    T_n >= sum_j ell(v_j) >= (tau-epsilon) sum_j ||v_j||
        >= (tau-epsilon)||v||.

Taking the refinement limit gives T>=(tau-epsilon)||v||; then epsilon->0
gives T>=tau||v||. This also excludes influences carried only in metadata or
timing, because they occur in LC3's complete dependency graph. Without that
completeness the theorem would bound only a selected subgraph.

Conversely split v into N equal displacements small enough for LC2. Their
total flight time N ell(v/N) tends to tau||v||. For a strict deadline
T>tau||v|| choose N large enough, lower this finite program with overhead
smaller than the remaining margin, and wait. LC1 and LC3 do this for any
finite collection of reads with independent payloads. Consequently all
strictly timelike accesses are achievable and all spacelike accesses are
excluded, with c=1/tau. Exact null access is not implied.

For a finite acyclic schedule, let K be its total primitive count after
subdivision. Choose each primitive overhead below epsilon/K (handle K=0 by
waiting). Every dependency path has at most K primitives. Induction through
addition and maximum bounds each event delay by epsilon. This explains the
whole-program quantifier; it does not supply a source compiler for free.

### Strict weakening and independence controls

* ell(v)=tau*r+kappa*r^2, r=||v||, kappa>0, satisfies LC2. A length-r
  straight path subdivided into N pieces costs tau*r+kappa*r^2/N.
  A flight may be refined and padded by a wait to preserve its original
  elapsed time. Thus the generated theory satisfies LC3. It violates exact
  dilation covariance, and no finite nonzero path attains the null boundary.
  This separates LC from the exact RG geometry, not from RG's approximate
  implementation variant. That variant can realize the same operational limit.
* The norm max_{u in V}|u dot v|, using the twelve icosahedral vertices V,
  is invariant under the full carrier group and is homogeneous and refinable.
  Its unit ball is polyhedral. Its squared norm has no quadratic expansion at
  zero. Finite symmetry alone therefore does not select a round cone.
* Adding a displacement-one primitive of time tau/2 leaves all sufficiently
  small flights unchanged and preserves local regularity. It violates LC3:
  refined small flights cost asymptotically at least tau. The local clock
  alone cannot exclude coarse shortcuts.
* Remove independent preparation and permit only equal copies of one hidden
  bit. Geometry survives but an independent input tuple cannot be observed.
  Bound workspace by B bits and arbitrary finite lossless tuples also fail.
* A compiler may replace a designated version a by a distinct cached version
  b which agrees on every initial input. Intervening on a alone distinguishes
  the programs. Initial-input equivalence does not prove writer ancestry.

### Corollary: the source-net and count limit

LC constructs the registers and the requested reads of any fixed finite
observation experiment. For a cubical window of side L take q^3 distinguished
registers with spacing L/q, layer duration delta_q->0, and q*delta_q->infinity.
Choose an inner menu radius a_q=(1-eta_q)c*delta_q with eta_q->0 and enough
slack for the finite realization. The existing `covering_constructs_path`
and `reachable_outer` theorems bound k-layer read reachability between radii
k(a_q-2h_q) and k*a_q, h_q=sqrt(3)L/q. Intermediate intervention faithfulness
places consumed-writer paths in complete native ancestry; Theorem 2 bounds
all native ancestry by the outer cone. Both orders converge away from the
null boundary. Cell assignment supplies distinguished-record count-volume
with weight (L/q)^3*delta_q. Auxiliary native events are accounted separately.

The choice delta_q=L/(c*sqrt(q)) is admissible, hence M1's mesoscopic radius
is one regulator. Other delta_q with the two limit conditions are admissible.
Neither RG nor LC singles out that radius or a unique finite population.
This corollary uses the published source-net/count proofs, not a new finite
fit. It does not identify distinguished records with all source events.

## Why a quadratic clock could be motivated, and what remains new

Local composition, reversible classical computation and independently
distinguishable records are general information-processing principles.
[Bennett's reversible-computation construction](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/fall04/cos576/papers/bennett73.html)
motivates computing, copying the classical answer, and reversing the work.
It does not establish a spacetime speed or an energy bound.
[Operational reconstructions of quantum theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6451)
likewise treat causality and composition as substantive premises. They do
not derive LC2 or RG from the OPH axioms.

There is a precise A3-adjacent candidate for the local quadratic form. On
M_d take the tracial reference rho0=I/d and a traceless Hermitian linear
perturbation X(v), injective on E. For small v, rho(v)=I/d+X(v) is positive.
Functional calculus and the scalar logarithm series give

    D(rho(v)||I/d) = (d/2) Tr(X(v)^2) + O(||v||^3).

The linear term vanishes because Tr X=0. An equivariant X makes this positive
quadratic form carrier invariant, hence proportional to the squared norm
induced by the trace inner product on E
by Theorem 1. This is an entropy susceptibility calculation, not an A3
minimizer or a clock theorem. The identification

    ell(v)^2 = alpha D(rho(v)||rho0) + o(||v||^2),  alpha>0

would supply LC2, but is an additional clock-calibration law. A3 by itself
neither fixes alpha nor identifies elapsed time with distinguishability.
At a nontracial reference a different positive Hessian appears; carrier
invariance of the reference and perturbation family must be checked.
The proposed physical content is testable: local latency ratios should tend
uniformly to one on displacements of equal norm for that inner product, and coarse/fine timings
should agree with LC3. A polyhedral limit or a persistent coarse speedup
falsifies this particular reduction. A positive quadratic susceptibility
alone is insufficient experimental evidence for it.

## Finite algebra coherence and its exact boundary

For a finite routing graph put a private port algebra C^12_i and private
response matrix algebra Q_i at each vertex, and a quantum buffer B_e=M_12
on each edge. The global algebra is

    A = (tensor_i C^12_i) tensor (tensor_i Q_i) tensor (tensor_e M_12_e).

The carrier algebra A_i uses its private factors and incident edge buffers.
Its center is exactly C^12_i: adjoining matrix factors creates no extra
central ports. Adjacent carriers share M_12_e, nonadjacent ones share only
scalars. Disjoint factor regions commute. Composite observers may use the
generated subalgebras; they need not have twelve central ports, since they
are not primitive carriers. These are literal finite-dimensional inclusions.

Source port p can be copied into an incident buffer with Kraus operators

    K_(p,b) = e_p tensor |p><b|,  p,b in {0,...,11}.

Their K* K sum is the identity. The port is retained and the buffer becomes
|p><p| on that classical branch, destroying its previous contents. Reading
the buffer in this basis and preparing a target central port copies p again.
Unknown quantum states are not cloned. Buffer effects with off-diagonal
matrix entries can be measured, so the shared matrix factor is usable and
is not merely a formal center-hiding device. All classical gate maps are
permutations on diagonal records, hence completely positive; discards and
preparations are channels. These maps commute with any response action
confined to the private Q_i factors.

Position the buffer at an intermediate point of a link. Charge both accesses
with their local-clock durations, including controller and measurement-result
transport. A shared algebra is not permission for instantaneous distant
control. This construction exhibits finite compatibility between retained
classical copying, quantum seams, and twelve central ports. A continuum
family of such channels does not constitute extra *reversible infinitesimal*
port responses on one carrier.

For state consistency one may explicitly include a joint observer with the
full algebra A and all marginal constraints. Its joint states and restrictions
form a nonempty compact convex feasible family; the joint tracial state is
faithful and compatible. Choose each local reference to be the restriction
of this same joint tracial state. With these compatible references, a positive-weight
Umegaki objective and an injective cover including that joint observer, it
is the unique unconstrained minimum: every term is nonnegative and the joint
term vanishes only at its reference. Independent port preparation belongs
to declared interventions or constrained problems, not to that unconstrained
minimum. Adding a blank factor, tracing it out, and lifting old operations
as Phi tensor id gives exact value-level refinement. This argument assumes
the joint observer in the stated finite model; local overlap compatibility
alone would not imply a joint extension.

Faithfulness and traciality alone are insufficient to identify the minimum.
On one shared classical bit, take joint reference (1/2,1/2) and a second
scored reference (3/4,1/4). Both are faithful and tracial, but the derivative
of their equal-weight summed relative entropy at probability 1/2 is -log(3).
Thus the joint reference is not the minimum. The exact control checks this
incompatible-reference counterexample; compatibility is a required premise.

**What this construction does not certify.** A complete native source must
also specify the source-bound spherical support and degree-one bridge at
every cutoff, natural response charts, the full admitted reversible response,
and the operational use/deletion tests for every load-bearing A1 field.
Putting a dormant spherical complex beside this circuit would not discharge
those obligations. Arbitrary coherent buffer gates or classically controlled
private response unitaries could enlarge the response beyond twelve dimensions;
the finite compatibility result does not admit them and then silently discard
their tangent. A grammar with only a prescribed private response is not a
proof that its response exhausts the native source. Consequently this is a
finite algebra and state-consistency witness, not a joint A1--A3+LC model.
It improves RG's internal coherence evidence without promoting that evidence
to a completed source-selection theorem.

## Classical compilation and intervention semantics

Encode finite alphabets as bit strings, declaring unused encodings explicitly.
Choose two port values for each bit. On the full twelve-symbol register,
each gate swaps the two target values under its declared controls and fixes
all other target values; this is a permutation extending the binary gate.
Carrier relabeling transports the chosen pair and the whole program. The
copy Kraus family is natural under simultaneous port/buffer relabeling:
K_(p,b) is carried to K_(pi(p),pi(b)). No label is a preferred physical bit.
For f:{0,1}^n->{0,1}^m, enumerate each minterm a. Temporarily negate each
input bit for which a_i=0. For every output bit j with f(a)_j=1, apply a
multi-controlled NOT from the n inputs to a fresh output j. Undo the temporary
negations. Exactly one minterm is enabled on each input, so the output is
f(x), while the input is retained.

For n>=3 compute successive conjunctions into n-2 clean auxiliary bits using
Toffoli gates, toggle the output using the last conjunction and input, and
reverse the conjunction gates. For n=2 use Toffoli, n=1 CNOT, n=0 NOT.
CNOT is a derived gate: prepare a clean control bit to one using NOT, apply
Toffoli with that extra control, and reset it with NOT. One such control can
be shared throughout a sequential circuit; setup, cleanup and storage are
counted. All
auxiliary bits return to zero. The worst-case construction is exponential;
no efficient native implementation is asserted. Replay retains every gate and
register; the receipt binds the full program by hash and records its resource
counts. Gates are self-inverse permutations, so running the
whole program backwards restores the initial output blanks as well.

This proves the universal finite-function reduction. The executable replay
enumerates all 256 three-input Boolean functions, selected multiple-output
functions, and n=0,1,2,4,5 cases; it is a check of that implementation, not
a replacement for the minterm proof. The verifier independently executes
the actual gates, checks every named case against a separately specified
target catalog, compares the full truth table, checks clean work,
tests inverse execution, and inserts isolated interventions at every gate
boundary for two diagnostic programs. It must reject invalid register types,
out-of-range or repeated operands, altered gates, and incomplete evidence.
Transport of each declared wire remains an LC premise, not a gate theorem.
The reference catalog rejects omitted, duplicate and substituted functions,
even if a producer changes its circuit and truth table consistently. Its
retained-copy diagnostic also compares the designated intermediate writer
against an independent boundary specification. Those paired comparisons
and their additional gate executions are included in the resource ledger.

## Finite entropy selection from actual transition constraints

There is a direct finite connection between the compiled operations and a
classical A3 state problem. Fix a program of g gates on R bits, n of them
independently preparable initial input bits. The ambient history alphabet contains all
2^D assignments of the R registers at g+1 times, D=R(g+1). Its classical
algebra is C^(2^D). Impose initial blank-work/output constraints and the
actual equation for each executed gate. These are linear zero-expectation
constraints on the indicators of invalid local transitions. They refer to
the program operations, not its target truth table or success at an M1 read.

Exactly one admissible history h(x) exists per input x, because the update
rule is deterministic and the full history retains the initial input. Give
the full input product domain any faithful prior p(x), which need not be a
product probability law, and all other ambient history coordinates
independent fair-bit references. Thus Q(h(x))=p(x)/2^(D-n), and the reference
mass of the admissible family is Z=2^(-(D-n)). For every admissible state P,

    D(P || Q) = D(P_input || p) + (D-n) log(2).

The unique minimizer is consequently P*(h(x))=p(x). This proves exact
agreement between the finite entropy minimizer and execution from the declared
input ensemble, using actual gate constraints. No histories are removed
because they fail a target read. A defective program's defective outputs
remain in the family and have positive probability for every affected input.
The exact control enumerates all 64 histories of one Toffoli step, obtains
four admissible histories, and recovers a nonuniform faithful input prior.
A hostile target table leaves feasibility unchanged and fails the separate
functional-correctness check.

The state problem has a faithful reference, a positive weight, a full joint
observer and its marginal restrictions; the joint observer alone is an
injective scored cover. The allowed global states are explicitly classical.
Additional marginal score terms would require separate compatibility with
this minimizer; their optimum is not silently inherited. This construction
does not derive the program, the clock, the record constraints or this score
cover from canonical A1--A3. It closes a finite process/state-consistency
obligation of the proposed implementation, not the native-source completion.

## Consequences beyond the M1 limit

### Theorem 3: lossless reads impose an information cut bound

Fix every cache and other preexisting variable, and independently intervene
on m fresh d-ary records. If the receiver reconstructs their tuple from a
transcript in a finite alphabet T, the transcript map is injective: applying
the decoder to equal transcripts yields equal input tuples. Thus |T|>=d^m.
If the complete cut transcript uses B binary symbols, B>=m log2(d).
The cut includes timing, control, metadata and side information changed by
the interventions. This is a zero-error combinatorial result; it assumes
neither a probabilistic prior nor thermodynamics. Arbitrarily precise real
timing is an infinite alphabet and must not be counted as one bit.

An additive accumulator fails this bound as a tuple decoder: (0,1) and
(1,0) have the same sum. Pre-shared copies do not evade fresh localized
interventions while all caches are fixed. A sufficient implementation is
to transport the tuple itself; neither RG nor LC promises bounded resources.
Disjoint-support classical operations commute, and the dependency bound
gives no signalling outside the cone. Common causes can still correlate
spacelike records; no statistical independence theorem follows.

### Theorem 4: dense M1 observation has a divergent resource bill

Take q=n^2 sites per axis, grid spacing L/q, radius L/sqrt(q), and layer
duration L/(c sqrt(q)). Let m=floor(n/2). Every offset in [-m,m]^3 has
squared length <=3m^2<=(3/4)n^2<n^2, hence is strictly inside the read
radius. For n>=2, at least (q-2m)^3 interior receivers each have
(2m+1)^3 incoming records, counting self. The explicit lower bound is

    reads/layer >= (n^2-2 floor(n/2))^3 (2 floor(n/2)+1)^3.

For n>=2, q-2m>=n^2/2 and 2m+1>=n. Therefore at least n^9/8=q^(9/2)/8
reads occur per layer. A physical horizon L/c contains n layers, giving
at least n^10/8=q^5/8 read incidences. These lower bounds use a strict inner
cube. For the inner-menu implementation, this same cube is included once
eta_q < 1-sqrt(3)/2, so the asymptotic bounds survive vanishing overhead;
the all-n constants above concern the stated full-radius experiment.
Nonself reads subtract one per receiver and have the same
growth. For an incoming communication cut, exclude the receiver's own record:
the fresh remote tuple needs (2m+1)^3-1 >= n^3-1 >= n^3/2 bits per layer.
Thus each interior receiver needs at least c*n^4/(2L)=c*q^2/(2L) incoming
bits per unit time. Its own locally available bit is not charged as transport.

The aggregate number is a read-incidence/output-size bound, not a network
unicast bound: multicast can share paths. The per-receiver information cut
bound applies if each layer's named outputs support independent fresh
interventions and complete local caches are fixed at that boundary. Correlated
unintervened histories alone do not justify a fresh entropy-rate bound.
A fixed bandwidth per receiver or fixed total output memory is consequently
incompatible with literal dense
lossless observation at every refinement under those intervention conditions.
Cofinal growth of hardware/workspace is permitted by RG and LC, but must
be a physical premise. Distinguished-record volume is not native work.
A bound on native event counts additionally needs a uniform bound on the
information carried per event; the duration grade and incidence count alone
do not provide one. The explicit compiler ledger counts its bit gates, but
does not identify them with a source's unbounded-arity elementary events.
A single primitive carrier's central algebra C^12 cannot hold a lossless
tuple with more than twelve alternatives in one central record. The compiled
receiver is a growing composite observer/register bank, not one unchanged
twelve-port carrier storing an unbounded tuple. Encoding into quantum buffers
does not change the dimension of that central record surface. This is a
concrete interpretation constraint on the population-to-native-carrier join.

Finally, identity, parity and other local update programs all coexist with
the same transport law. The law selects no matter equation, energy density,
Lorentz covariance of dynamics, gravitational field equation or thermodynamic
arrow. Its additional predictions are the access cone, the refinement timing
constraint, the intervention-sensitive communication requirement, and these
resource inequalities. Their failures can be investigated without fitting M1.
