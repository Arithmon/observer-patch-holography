# RG scrutiny: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Test the proposed record-gluing law on its own mathematical and operational
merits. Establish its consistency obligations, replace dispensable primitive
requirements by a proved weaker principle, and derive consequences that can
test the proposal independently of an M1 fit. RG is a proposed additional
source law; this work does not adopt it as a core axiom.

The reduction restates the relevant transport interface of the RG law in
`../source_selection_model/RECORD_GLUING.md` and uses the existing
source-net results. No unpublished simulator implementation is assumed.

## Deliverables

1. A finite-algebra construction with twelve central ports per carrier,
   usable quantum seams, retained classical copying, explicit completely
   positive maps, and a precise response-completeness boundary.
2. A constructive reduction of arbitrary finite classical maps to a finite
   reversible gate basis, with clean auxiliary registers and intermediate
   interventions. Every executed gate and auxiliary register is counted.
3. A local-clock/refinement theorem deriving a round limiting signal cone
   from finite carrier covariance and quadratic regularity. Neither exact
   scale covariance nor a continuously rotated flight family may enter its
   premises unnoticed.
4. Countercontrols separating isotropy, timing refinement, independent
   preparation, workspace, and faithful intervention semantics. State what
   is motivation, what is an additional physical premise, and what is proved.
5. Consequences beyond the M1 limit: a causal finite-program realization,
   distinguishability/cut-capacity bounds, and the resource growth required
   by the dense M1 observation protocol.
6. Independent strict replay, source-bound receipts, meaningful hostile
   inputs, and Lean proofs with explicit hypotheses and standard-axiom
   audits. Finite checks must not substitute for universal analytic proofs.

## Exit

Publish a substantial, self-contained PR whose claims match the proved
scope. A finite process model is not automatically a model of the complete
A1--A3 source and refinement clauses. A matrix compatibility calculation
does not certify that the registered simulator obeys RG. Any proposed
joint-source construction must expose its cover, state family, A3 problem,
refinement maps and complete admitted operation grammar.

A simplification must prove the resulting operational limit and exhibit
the removed requirements, rather than rename the full conclusion as one
axiom. Resource conclusions count native work separately from distinguished
record volume. Motivation from information processing is not a derivation
from the three core axioms or experimental confirmation.
