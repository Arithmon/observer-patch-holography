# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Connect a complete twelve-port source to a coherent, timed clock experiment.
Decide what classical record gluing and A3 actually select, derive the local
quantum controls from the existing source response, and isolate any additional
information-transport principle. A stipulated successful clock, an appended
unexamined quantum gate set, or a new name for the desired channel is not an exit.

## Deliverables

1. Give an explicit source construction with primitive central ports, complete
   response, same-response chart transport, spherical support, refinement,
   a complete constraint grammar and the actual finite A3 problems. Check
   destructive code transfers on the full accessible algebra as well as on
   their computational domains. Keep logical reversibility distinct from a
   reversible response on the entire carrier algebra.
2. Exhibit a classical transport completion that satisfies the stated source
   clauses but cannot transmit an unknown noncentral quantum state. Prove an
   all-program obstruction, including feedback, retained records and stopping.
   Do not infer full model membership from a stand-alone Choi example.
3. Derive a noncommuting read-agreement criterion for coherent code transfer;
   solve its finite maximum-randomness channel problem, including nonzero
   errors. Compare it with classical-only agreement in one common feasible
   family. State the additional physical content, rather than attribute it
   to A1--A3 or to a stationary ontic optimizer.
4. Extract single-qubit control and an entangling gate from the existing
   twelve source generators, without adding a reversible response direction.
   Compile the clock's gates and preserve entangled computational inputs.
   Account for moving data, overwritten receiver state, failure outputs,
   ancillary storage, primitive events and positive control durations.
5. Establish which operational-clock estimates survive this source
   implementation. Give a finite informative witness with its complete error
   and time budget. Test the classical/dephased alternative on the same
   diagnostic. Do not transfer a full Fock-space claim from agreement only
   on the vacuum and one-particle sector.
6. Supply reproducible compact evidence, an independent checker and hostile
   controls. Separate analytic all-size proofs from finite numerical checks.

## Exit

Produce a proved source-to-clock connection and decide the role of the new
transport requirement. If an additional principle is necessary, provide a
complete countermodel and a precise sufficient principle with independent
operational meaning. Do not leave the useful gate, selection, timing or
readout argument as an assumption for another PR.

This is research on explicit mathematical source realizations. A successful
construction is not a claim that the simulator, empirical physics, particle
masses or a unique source representation have been selected by A1--A3.
No additional principle is silently installed as a core axiom.
