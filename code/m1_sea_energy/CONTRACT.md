# Source vacuum and energy: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Connect the actual source walk's evolution to a positive excitation generator,
and determine which source preparations and reads remain informative over its
filled negative-energy vacuum. Extend the existing finite edge-sea example to
the complete massive walk. Preserve the previous empty-vacuum results as
separate experiments; do not silently replace their reference or detector.

## Deliverables

1. Derive the filled vacuum and positive particle/hole Hamiltonian from the
   same signed walk logarithm. Prove the full Fock dynamical identity, retain
   every band and valley, and distinguish this from replacing h by |h|.
2. Establish finite-boundary spectral control and quantitative local vacuum
   comparison. Prove a continuum covariance limit for the retained resolved
   read class, with explicit treatment of the high band.
3. Construct finite-energy local operations and a bounded, actually compiled
   interference read that resolves a clock above the sea. Keep all outcomes
   and prove an informative finite error bound; background subtraction alone
   is not a physical detector.
4. Supply deterministic source preparation, charged routing and protection
   costs, and a fixed-strength noisy realization of this new experiment.
   Do not inherit the old preparation-time bound without proving it.
5. Prove the surviving ambiguity of integer-time quasienergies, including a
   concrete countercontrol. Positivity alone must not be promoted to physical
   energy calibration or stability under unspecified interactions.
6. Independent finite operator/circuit replay, hostile and equivalence
   controls, compact source-pinned evidence, registered claims and platform CI.

## Exit

Provide an explicit positive-generator source experiment with a proved local
vacuum limit, finite-energy reads, a resolved clock signal and paid noisy
preparation. Its derivation must use the actual walk and complete instruments,
not a fitted continuum curve or selected successful runs. Standard CAR,
particle/hole, Slater-preparation and spectral-decay results must be credited.

The source/CCG capability, selected masses, native time unit and external
schedule remain declared. A principal Floquet branch is already specified by
the parent and remains a choice. No autonomous apparatus Hamiltonian, drive
work calibration, interacting vacuum or A1--A3 source selection is asserted.
