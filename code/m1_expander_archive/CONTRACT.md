# Fixed-strength public records: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Remove the vanishing elementary-error requirement from the public archive of
the constructed noisy source. Keep actual noisy central bits live, and join
them to protected quantum computation without a reliable bare export bit.

## Deliverables

1. An explicit graph family and native Boolean refresh schedule with bounded
   degree and depth independent of record size. Prove correction and live
   majority under a fixed positive fraction of arbitrary faulty locations.
2. A protected-to-public decoder whose error bound is uniform in the growing
   quantum concatenation depth. Retain the complete application instrument.
3. A single fixed-strength source theorem covering quantum computation,
   full-carrier faults, runtime control and maintained public histories.
   Derive the joint error, accounting, causal schedule and lifetime costs.
4. Independent exact graph and Boolean controls, full-channel export checks,
   counterexamples, strict receipt replay, hostile tests and platform CI.

## Exit

For a nonempty interval of fixed nonzero elementary error strengths, construct
a cofinal family with convergent joint decoded quantum/public history and
positive accounting. No elementary error is required to vanish with q. Prove
the archive invariant at every time in a refresh, not only at its final cut.

The proper-code source, CCG capability, local noise condition, external static
schedule and logical accounting reference are declared, as in the parents.
There is no numerical threshold claim for the entire native machine, trusted
physical majority gate, arbitrary unknown raw-input encoder, autonomous
clock, physical energy calibration or source selection from A1--A3. Imported
expander and computation-code results must be credited. The very large fixed
constants must be disclosed; finite controls must not be described as an
execution of the full asymptotic apparatus.
