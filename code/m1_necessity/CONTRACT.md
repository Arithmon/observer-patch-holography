# M1 necessity: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Determine which conclusions require M1's complete metric-neighbor read law.
Construct explicit alternative families and prove what they preserve and what
they change. A proposed physical premise or a finite fit is not the result.
The integrated base is main 7f588ea5444c87263e62dd8e30f0805f19d09b9d.
PRs 968 and 977 are included, with their retained information bounds.

## Deliverables

1. A checked inventory of the current direct M1-dependent claims, with the
   exact result being transferred distinguished from each larger claim.
2. An explicit sparse integer read family, a constructive finite path bound,
   and universal analytic proofs of its causal, interval-volume, strict-pair
   and count-clock limits. The construction must specify every edge, event,
   tick and weight; no assumption of successful cone convergence is allowed.
3. A second explicit family showing that even a round causal/count limit and
   cubic symmetry do not fix orientation-independent crossing-read entropy.
   Give exact finite crossing formulas, quantitative error bounds, and a
   nonvanishing limiting discrepancy that one scalar calibration cannot fix.
4. A positive sparse scalar action with an explicit free-field/detector error
   bound at the square-root scaling, a proved centered-leapfrog obstruction
   at the M1 tick, and explicit different radius schedules preserving the
   named limits. Keep the action and layered read program distinct.
5. A fully specified sparse family preserving the original leading crossing
   coefficient for every regular planar orientation with unchanged one-nat
   weights; a complementary fixed-radius sub-stencil lower bound; and a
   full free quantum and thermal comparison on specified positive actions.
   Check the whole spectral tail: smooth probes alone must not conceal extra
   low-energy species. Retain the same one-nat crossing statistic after any
   necessary correction of the defined action.
6. Actual finite graph execution, independently checked routes, complete cut
   counts and hostile controls. Keep compact reproducible commitments rather
   than committing large raw event archives.
7. Proof/source custody, kernel-checked reductions with standard-axiom audits,
   consistent claim registration and publication, and local validation.

## Exit

The exit includes both a proved separation and a constructive replacement.
S and A show that round causal/count limits alone do not fix crossing
entropy. T preserves the named limits and the original leading planar
crossing coefficient without recalibration. The fixed-radius sub-stencil
bound explains why its radius change has a role. No convergence success
condition or new physical transport axiom is assumed to obtain these results.

Do not delete the existing golden-family theorems, reclassify their physical
premises, or claim to preserve all of M1's finite outputs. Do not equate
logical read incidences with bit traffic, energy or native simulator events.
The new graphs are explicit mathematical experiments, not full A1--A3 models,
exact finite Lorentz-covariant histories, or an implementation of RG. A full
source realization, physical clock, state/process law and interacting matter
join remain separate obligations, not hypotheses secretly discharged here.
