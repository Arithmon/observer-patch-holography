# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Decide whether the explicit optimal tetrahedral quantum transport in
`code/m1_quantum_transport` supports an observable moving clock and a common
effective matter/read speed, despite its faster complete microscopic cone.
Use the actual flight duration. Derive the clock signal from evolution and a
positive local readout; a declared proper-time accrual rule is not a solution.

## Deliverables

1. Construct the massive walk as explicit onsite unitaries and one parallel
   register flight. Derive the entire spectrum, all species, the mass gap,
   and a uniform full-channel dynamical error. Include odd ticks and both
   quasienergy bands.
   Prove the sharp speed bound for every band and the fixed-spacing
   ballistic limit. Keep it separate from the fixed-duration refinement limit.
2. Prove a spatial-resolution bound for actual sampled local CAR readouts.
   Derive suppression of observable signals outside the matter cone,
   uniformly over states, without an assumed low-energy state or a projected
   positive-energy field. Retain the faster microscopic record counterexample.
3. Construct a normalizable two-mass clock, a positive readout with finite
   spatial extent, and an explicit visibility/error estimate. Derive its
   inertial dilation and give finite parameters for an informative bound.
   Bound finite preparation error and charge retained high-band weight in
   one fixed positive Floquet accounting observable, without calling it a
   source-derived physical energy.
4. Execute finite periodic packets with every Fourier mode retained. Replay
   the spectrum, timing, reversed evolution, readout and failure controls by
   independent algorithms. Reject malformed or forged evidence under Python
   optimization as well as normal execution.
5. Register the analytic results and provide a reproducible compact receipt,
   tests and CI. Distinguish analytic proofs from finite numerical checks.

## Exit

Give a quantitative answer for this specified process, including the exact
class of local operations for which the effective cone is valid. Do not
discard species, charge two parallel flights sequentially, treat two ticks
as one tick, assume a desired clock law, or hide a failed packet experiment.
An informative clock bound must exist at finite parameters.

The construction is a mathematical quantum extension of the timed-flight
grammar. Its source admission, mass selection and conversion of Floquet
quasienergy into physical energy are not supplied by A1--A3. No coin or mass
index is identified with a twelve-port source index. The operational theorem
does not cover arbitrary cell-resolving encoders, interacting matter or
accelerated clocks.
