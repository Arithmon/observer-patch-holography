## Admissible source packet

The admissibility contract has the following fields.

### Port-indexed reversible perturbations

There must be twelve reversible perturbation families, one per primitive port,
bound to the same carrier and orientation receipt.

No gauge-group, particle, coupling, mass, or downstream target label may enter
their definition.

### Both composition orders

For each of the 66 unordered pairs of distinct ports, the packet must record
both orders of composition:

```text
p then q
q then p
```

for 132 ordered compositions total, all on the same source packet.

This is the first source object capable of exposing a mixed-order difference
rather than remaining inside a single commutative functional calculus.

### First-order gate

The twelve first derivatives must produce twelve skew-adjoint tangent
generators of exact real rank 12.

The pairing

```text
-Re tr(XY)
```

must be positive definite on that source-reconstructed span.

### Mixed-order gate

The commutator must be reconstructed from the two composition orders rather
than imported from the conditional fixture.

Required output:

- every reconstructed commutator closes in the twelve-dimensional source span;
- commutator span has exact real rank 11;
- the center has dimension one;
- the constant linear combination of the twelve port generators spans that
  center.

### Holonomy gate

Closed words from those same perturbations must cover all sixty proper
rechartings and reconstruct their projective implementers.

The implementers must lie in the response-generated identity component modulo
the pointwise centralizer.

### Refinement gate

Generators, bracket and implementers must all intertwine along the same
registered refinement tower.

## Accepted exits

Stage 1 intentionally permits three outcomes.

### Positive reconstruction

The source histories reconstruct one current equivalence class, modulo the
declared basis/gauge freedom. Only after that reconstruction may it be
compared with `PORT-CURRENT-INNER`.

### Non-identifiability

Two inequivalent current realizations survive the identical source packet.
The certificate must emit both witnesses and an invariant separating the two
equivalence classes.

This is a scientifically successful negative result.

### Insufficient data

One or more first-order, mixed-order, holonomy or refinement gates fail.
