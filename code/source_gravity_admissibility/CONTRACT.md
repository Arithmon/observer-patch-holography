# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Resolve whether the paired-gravity nonselection result can be strengthened
from a freely declared two-field family to admitted operations of an
existing A1--A3 source. Derive a selected relation, or construct actual
clock-equivalent source processes with different spatial reads. Do not
mistake a new simulator capability or an imposed Einstein metric for that
result. This is a follow-up to merged #1016, not a reopening of #1013 or a
claim to close its physical parents.

## Deliverables

1. A clause-by-clause membership argument using the inherited coherent
   source, its complete response, endogenous charts, full instruments,
   support/refinement and typed A3 problems. Additional proposed RG/CCG
   services remain explicit; no claim about the integer-repair simulator.
2. One fixed source law, two unchanged source-conditioned transport
   policies, the same clock channel and calibration at every strength.
   No separate optical fit, new response direction or uncharged flight.
3. An exact finite receiver-read separation with a source-equation proof,
   retained failure outcomes, physical time ledger and nonvacuous error
   tolerance. Every accepted numerical case and the zero-source control
   must be present.
4. The derived principal symbol and the resulting functional spatial
   freedom, with finite-word remainder bound. Distinguish this from the
   exact finite result, a uniform ray approximation and physical photons.
5. A same-algebra A3 selection test identifying the missing restriction,
   rather than entropy optimization over untyped theories or programs.
6. Compact primary evidence, independent replay, full-column compiler
   checks and hostile controls for ancestry, source, clock, pulse order,
   flight reflection, policy, detector, timing, coverage and schema.

## Exit

The negative exit requires an analytic membership proof and an actual
finite distinction inside the inherited source, not a promise that a
universal computer could simulate different metrics. The exact gap must
survive the declared error budgets. The independent verifier must reject
altered semantic evidence even with a rebuilt custody envelope, and the
producer must reproduce the retained evidence within numerical tolerance.

No physical parent is closed by this negative result. Axioms plus RG/CCG
do not select which source-conditioned transport law nature follows.
The additional necessary relation is identified and independently
falsifiable; it is not adopted or described as independently justified.
The proof may rule out entailment while leaving selection by additional
physics open. That is a completed negative answer, not a claimed gravity
derivation with its central premise deferred.
