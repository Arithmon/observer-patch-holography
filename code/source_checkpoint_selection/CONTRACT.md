# Finite checkpoint selection from native records

## Objective

Derive a complete temporal constraint grammar and an actual finite
information-projection transition law from a specified public record
interface and native scalar response. Prove feasibility from connected
topology and publication by a finite checkpoint without an IID proposal or
fairness assumption. Build on temporal recovery, rather than assume a
successful route, a supplied optimizer, or an unexplained acceptance label.

## Deliverables

1. Prove that equality of a public record map is the complete agreement
   grammar for all tests generated by that map. Prove coordinate
   completeness and a distinguishing-pair witness for any omitted constraint.
2. Prove that preservation of that meaning by the joint record/state map
   is equivalent to bounded native continuation on connected support.
   Permit loss of distinctions irrelevant to the public meaning.
3. Derive backward continuation masses from the full native move tree,
   prove their exhaustive word-sum identity, and prove positive mass iff
   some native continuation publishes. Construct a normalized causal
   kernel; prove its product law and exact support.
4. Prove existence and uniqueness of the finite KL optimizer by an explicit
   entropy-gap identity, including candidate laws with zero coordinates.
   Its reference is inherited from the full-word reference, not reset to a
   locally uniform distribution after deleting moves.
5. Compose native feasibility and the selected law. Require that the move
   alphabet covers the graph edges and contains only permitted moves.
   A positive law atom uses only that support and publishes every required
   record for every preparation intervention;
   positive continuation mass excludes irreversible loss. The controller
   works with response maps, not payload values or future samples.
6. Check exact uniform and tilted-reference controls on the captured
   five-port path and a four-port chain. Independently enumerate every word,
   including infeasible histories in the reference denominator. Replay all
   retained online decisions, all nine signed preparations, every prefix
   certificate and each consumed writer. Charge the full selected horizon.
7. Retain infeasible deadlines, aggregate-only semantics, nonuniform
   references and incompatible independently chosen deadlines as controls.
   Give a precise remaining-source-obligation analysis, including why
   imposing a deadline is a changed optimization problem, not a consequence
   of canonical A3's randomness clause alone.
8. Prove analytically the sharp shortest-word classification for two
   adjacent unknown records on an otherwise calibrated path: 2d-1 means,
   a two-wave precedence order and Catalan(d-1) histories. Derive exact
   receiver responses and sharp two-sample error gains. Kernel-check native
   disjoint commutation and the reconstruction/error algebra; distinguish
   these from the analytic classification. Independently check every word
   at the minimal horizon for d<=5 and every generated positive history
   for d<=10. Show why every positive time-homogeneous edge-product
   reference selects the same uniform law at this minimal deadline.

## Exit

All new Lean theorems pass the transitive standard-axiom gate. The producer
and verifier use independent continuation algorithms: memoized response
states versus exhaustive integer-scaled scalar experiments and determinant
tests. Compact controls carry full source pins and a recomputable complete
word-census commitment. Tests reject resealed semantic forgeries, false
success, malformed certificates, future samples, omitted records/histories,
false transition masses, duplicate JSON keys, floats, scope promotion,
deleted operations, wrong writers and understated preparation/read/write/
sample costs. Full-word normalization and all short-tree prefix masses are
independently checked. No expanded production tapes are committed.
Zero deadlines, an initial receiver that is a source port, and malformed
path/target inputs have explicit regression tests.

## Scientific boundary

The theorem constructs a law inside one specified finite checkpoint model.
The public record map, accepted preparation domain, complete move alphabet,
retained-sample interface, deadline, full-history entropy cover and faithful
reference remain supplied. Completeness is for all tests of this record
map, not all constraints of the complete A1 quantum/response architecture.
For arbitrary record maps the publication predicate is classical and need
not be decidable. The executable policy computes it by exact elimination
for the stated rational linear response and record maps.
The checkpoint requirement changes the feasible set from the prior IID
proposal experiment. It is not claimed to follow from canonical A1--A3.
No failed physical run is erased: the new law is implemented by continuation
weights before each mean, and every selected word's full horizon is charged.
Host planning, exact arithmetic, randomness, sampling and record storage
are explicit implementation interfaces; their physical costs are not known.

The path alphabet is an isolated subgrammar of the captured W12 support,
not a completeness certificate for all 46,050 seams or all A1 instruments.
The controls cover two real preparation directions, not every captured
port's payload. Exact decoder identities extend those finite signed tests
to all real values in those two directions. No chosen mean radius, golden
population, immutable-version semantic order, physical length/clock,
refinement-compatible optimizer family or full M1 derivation is asserted.
PR-52 remains open and unconsumed.
