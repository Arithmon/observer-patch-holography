# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Determine when local quantum gates approximate the original positive scalar
Hamiltonian on an entire growing population, rather than only on selected
smooth probes. Prove and repair any ultraviolet vacuum error, using the
original ground state and energy throughout. Count the actual spatial reads
and separate simulation duration from a physical transport clock.

The sparse stencil also used in `code/m1_interfaces` is defined again here;
no file or theorem from that package is imported.

## Deliverables

1. Exact mode formulas for vacuum particle production, original-Hamiltonian
   excess energy and global vacuum fidelity of second- and fourth-order
   symmetric kick/drift programs. Prove a common explicit stability domain.
2. Necessary and sufficient refinement thresholds for uniform global vacuum
   fidelity and energy fidelity on a fixed nonzero time interval. Use the
   full spectrum, with an explicit time-average lower bound preventing a
   favorable sampled phase from hiding failure.
3. A constructive fourth-order schedule on the specified sparse family that
   makes the total excess energy vanish, with a bound on all bounded-effect
   predictions for the vacuum and bounded-energy, fixed-band coherent inputs.
   Correct the ultraviolet scale using the actual degree and second moment.
4. A proved obstruction to exact original-vacuum preservation by a nontrivial
   fixed-degree scalar polynomial symplectic update, including its finite
   spectral-cardinality requirement and its scope.
5. Exact read-version accounting and precision-dependent cost comparisons
   against the dense family. Charge inverse gates and all intermediate
   writes. Prove whether the support bound is attained by an actual signal
   and whether the simulation tick can obey a fixed-speed complete native
   influence law. Do not turn numerical substeps into physical fast reads.
6. Independent, complete finite replay: gate matrices versus covariance and
   mode calculations; complete named graphs, modes and times; exact algebraic
   cancellation/stability certificates; source custody and hostile tests.
7. Analytic proofs, clearly delimited Lean reductions, claim registration,
   reproducible compact evidence and publication integration.

## Exit

The failure and repair are theorems about fully specified programs, not
assumptions of their success. Supply explicit schedules and a global error
bound; a small fit or a stable eigenvalue plot is insufficient. Do not change
the reference Hamiltonian, choose a modified vacuum, discard modes, subtract
produced particles, or retain only favorable observation times.

The fourth-order composition is a standard numerical method, attributed as
such. The contribution is the full-population quantum fidelity theorem and
its application to OPH read/cost comparisons. Finite Schrödinger oscillators
and signed potential/kinetic gates are declared mathematical operations.
This is not an all-level A1--A3 source, a finite twelve-port implementation,
an interacting theory, or a derivation of a physical clock. Existing source
and M1/RG premises remain untouched.
