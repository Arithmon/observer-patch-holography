# Local publication from native observations

## Objective

Derive the strongest sound publication rule from a specified source model and
receiver interface, then determine its safety, availability and complete cost
for a captured native read. The source model must generate the possible
observations before publication is defined. Correct-answer labels cannot
filter the schedule space or enter the receiver.

This is a finite conditional step toward M1. Source derivation of the complete
temporal grammar, the A3 reference/cover and the metric read menu is a separate
obligation. The canonical axioms are unchanged.

## Deliverables

1. Kernel proofs that a feasible observation permits sound publication
   exactly when its compatible payloads form a singleton; maximality,
   observational refinement and ambiguous-transcript abstention. Connect the
   latter to native cut locality, rather than assuming communication.
2. Kernel proofs of native affine transport, positivity, dyadic signal
   separation and receiver error thresholds, including boundary ambiguity.
   A receiver uses its local samples, public error budget and checkpoint
   number. It receives no payload, remote values, future word or success flag.
3. A paired-rail read on captured ports, preserving total ideal load across
   the two payload interventions. All four adjacent paired copies are enabled
   at every checkpoint, including repeats and operations making no progress.
   Retain the entire finite word space, aborts and first-publication times.
4. Independent exhaustive reconstruction through eight checkpoints, actual
   nearest-even scalar means, every consumed writer, interval error margins,
   prefix multiplicities and unconditional work. Independently derive the
   first-arrival law from source influence, and compare it to execution.
5. General finite-path availability and work formulas with their precise
   schedule hypotheses, kernel-checked full-history counting/work recurrences,
   and finite-precision guarantees restricted to their declared horizon. No
   discarded retry, renormalized success probability, free reset, fresh source
   copy or unbounded fixed-precision guarantee.
6. Strict source/proof custody, malformed-input and resealed semantic
   mutations, executable CLI failures, transitive standard-axiom audits and
   Linux/Windows CI. Evidence stays compact and fully recomputable.

## Exit

Every theorem compiles without admissions, compiler trust or project axioms.
Both interventions publish correctly or explicitly abort for every tested
word, including all boundary/noise controls. Producer and independent verifier
agree on complete first-hit counts and paid costs. Tampering with observations,
support, acceptance, schedules, omitted failures, costs, proofs or receipts
fails even after rehashing. Existing tests and evidence are preserved.

## Scientific boundary

A2 checks meaning on an independently typed accepted domain; it does not
generate that domain. The singleton theorem derives the greatest sound local
partial function for a supplied model. It does not force an observer to run
that function or supply a physical comparator/publication instrument.

The native experiment supplies preparation, a paired-rail subgraph, isolation,
paired completion checkpoints, an error budget and a two-symbol code. Under
the explicitly supplied full-word simplex and counting reference, the existing
finite classical KL theorem selects the uniform product word law. That cover
is complete-history entropy, not a claimed reconstruction of the actual A3
cover. No source certificate establishes temporal grammar completeness or
selects this subgraph, checkpoint interface, cover or reference from A1--A3.

The experiment establishes a single finite remote read, with raw attenuation
and explicit possible abort. It supplies no arbitrary record service, complete
native q=13/q=21 histories, physical clock, volume law or M1 derivation. The
finite-history reward and paid-work recurrences are kernel-checked together
with their actual native stopped-word interpretation. The binomial closed
form and infinite ideal expectation have analytic proofs and independent
finite integer checks; they are distinct from the listed Lean results.
