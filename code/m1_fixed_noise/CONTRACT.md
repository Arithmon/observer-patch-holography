# Fixed-rate source protection: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Decide whether the source realization in `m1_fermionic_source` really needs
its physical dephasing rate to vanish with refinement. Construct protection
for its actual prepared experiments and named reads at a fixed finite rate,
including gates, preparation, storage, flights, recovery and reporting.
An ideal decoder applied after otherwise uncontrolled noise is insufficient.

## Deliverables

1. Translate the declared full-interface dephasing process, including noise
   during native pulses, into a local stochastic circuit fault model. Check
   the native universal gate interface and account for every idle interval.
2. Give a finite recovery instrument with bounded ancilla attempts and all
   records retained. Independently enumerate its single-location Pauli
   faults, including idle, preparation, two-qubit and read faults. Exhibit
   actual counterexamples when verification or repeated syndrome extraction
   is removed. Check arbitrary coherent input errors using the code algebra.
3. Apply a precisely cited constructive fault-tolerance theorem, including
   its non-Clifford gadgets, to the existing complete source programs.
   Prove an all-size fixed-rate refinement result with charged local control
   and flight times. Do not pretend that small Clifford executions validate
   an entire universal fault-tolerant computer or a numerical threshold.
4. Define the complete operational comparison, including abort outcomes,
   syndrome records, encoded-input scope and positive accounting effects.
   Prove convergence of the named clock/read/accounting outputs and explain
   why raw microscopic fidelity can fail at the same time. No successful
   branch may be renormalized into the claimed unconditional result.
5. Provide a compact independently replayed certificate, adversarial tests,
   a proof, claim registration and a precise record of imported results.

## Exit

A fixed positive physical noise rate is allowed in a constructed refinement
of the same CCG source, with vanishing error for the specified prepared
experiments and encoded-input read channels, finite positive event times,
bounded propagation speed and an explicit paid control schedule. The proof
must establish the fault-tolerance hypotheses in this source rather than
add reliable recovery, error-free magic states or free remote gates as axioms.

The threshold theorem and finite universal synthesis are credited standard
mathematics. CCG, reliable central control records and the source's cofinal
control/inventory capability retain their previous status. No fixed-strength
laboratory device, arbitrary raw-input encoder, full microscopic transcript
agreement or physical Hamiltonian is claimed.
