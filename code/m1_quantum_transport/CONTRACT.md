# Objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

Test whether the proposed classical native transport law can be completed by
coherent register flights and local scattering while giving matter the same
isotropic light speed as the complete native causal cone. Charge actual
elapsed time and retain intermediate events. A formal Dirac limit with a
renamed simulation tick is not a successful completion.

This work builds intellectually on the sparse read-family, interface and
vacuum-fidelity results in `code/m1_necessity`, `code/m1_interfaces` and
`code/m1_vacuum_fidelity`; no source file is imported from them. Those results
motivate the stronger question; they are not premises of the new speed-budget proof.

## Deliverables

1. Characterize the complete attainable set of untilted linear Weyl velocity matrices
   for the declared timed flight/scattering class, including arbitrary finite
   coin dimensions and compression to a degenerate band. Prove necessity
   and provide constructions attaining the boundary.
   Derive all saturation geometries and their unique minimal number of directions.
2. Execute a fully specified three-dimensional unitary process with each
   flight charged at its actual speed. Establish its continuum dynamics and
   show exactly whether that speed equals the complete record-cone speed.
3. Account for its entire momentum spectrum, including every additional
   zero-energy species. Prove full free thermal limits and use the vacuum
   selected by its declared quasienergy observable throughout.
4. Realize finite many-body examples by actual local fermionic gates and
   moving modes. Check coherence, fermionic signs, all basis states, inverse
   execution and intermediate positions/times independently.
5. Prove why switching to a bounded autonomous Hamiltonian on fixed finite
   site factors cannot restore an exact positive propagation delay. State
   precisely why moving registers and continuum fields are different cases.
6. Identify and derive the simplest noncommuting-velocity escape at the
   continuum operator level, with its own current and causal bound. Separate
   that mathematical witness from an A1--A3 source-realization claim.
7. Supply analytic proofs, delimited Lean reductions and axiom audits,
   independent compact evidence, hostile controls, claim registration and
   consistent publication.
8. Remove the flight-decomposition premise for a second obstruction: prove
   the support-polytope bound for arbitrary translation-invariant finite-range
   unitaries and quantify the direction growth necessary to approach a shared
   round-cone speed. Do not promote that necessary condition to sufficiency.

## Exit

The result must decide this entire operation class, not merely fail to find
a working example. If a shared-speed completion is impossible, prove the
sharp obstruction and show the optimal attainable alternative. Do not hide
species, insert a faster uncharged flight, reset intermediate time, replace
quantum amplitudes by classical probabilities, or equate Floquet quasienergy
with a derived thermodynamic energy law.

The declared mathematical operations are quantum extensions of the previous
flight grammar. Their admission by a complete twelve-port A1--A3 source is
not assumed or certified. A coin index is not identified with a central port.
The source-response attachment, physical energy calibration and interacting
matter remain outside any claimed completion.
