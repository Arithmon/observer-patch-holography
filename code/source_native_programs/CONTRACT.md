# Native stored computation contract

## Objective

Construct finite layered integer computations whose computed results are
stored, read by subsequent layers, and retired only after their last admitted
read. Every dynamic physical write is a supported scalar pair mean. The
comparison with ideal records includes one global error budget covering
preparation, all routing and cleanup, arithmetic, and local publication.

The target recurrence is `v[t+1,i] = 1 + sum(v[t,j] for j in menu[t,i])`.
Menus are supplied finite lists; duplicates and empty lists have their literal
sum semantics. Public initial magnitude bounds are independent of payloads.

## Deliverables

1. Native retained-source transfer, retired-destination overwrite, approximate
   bus cleaning, arithmetic restart, and composition theorems. A typed
   construction admits only those proved primitives, scalar-port permutations
   and concatenation; it has a whole-word noisy publication theorem.
2. A two-bank compiler and independent schedule/lifetime verifier on captured
   twelve-port supports. Core and record placement, both route polarities,
   paired path disjointness and every used seam are checked explicitly.
3. Nine complete signed three-layer histories with bank reuse, and two
   complete 27-site, two-layer golden-menu histories: the indexed initial
   payload and the centre-site `+1` intervention. Later layers read the stored
   outputs. The latter evidence is regenerated from compact commitments.
4. Complete paired-route witnesses for both banks at populations 27, 2,197
   and 9,261, independently checked against the captured seams. Exact metric
   menus and explicit sufficient compilation/precision bounds are checked for
   all three populations. The two large native program words are not replayed.
5. Standard-axiom gates, serious semantic mutations, compact evidence,
   deterministic regeneration and CI gates for both finite controls and the
   complete family route/metric certificates.

## Exit

The finite construction is accepted only if the independent verifiers reject
missing or extra histories, unsupported or aliased routes, clobbered live
versions, incorrect temporal words, omitted cleanup, undercharged work,
reset precision clocks, altered samples/publications, and scope promotion.
Every added Lean theorem is enumerated by the transitive standard-axiom gate.
Family receipts distinguish complete native q=3 evaluation from q=13/q=21
topology certificates and analytic sufficient bounds.

Deterministic repeated blocks may be accelerated only after executing a
whole block and checking that its complete touched state is unchanged.
Every omitted repetition stays in the charged native word and error clock.
This certifies its exact rounded state, not an event-by-event expansion of
every repetition. Raw route and execution witnesses are regenerated in a
work directory, not committed as long tapes.

## Scientific boundary

M1 remains a supplied structural rule under PR-52. The construction derives
the data-path operations conditional on a supplied word, placement, protected
lifetimes, preparation and publication instrument. It does not derive the
golden address population, metric menu, temporal controller, observer cover,
faithful source reference, physical isolation, physical clock or attainable
precision from A1--A3. The selection criterion and cover obstruction in
`code/source_read_selection` apply without modification.

The bank placement uses carrier zero as a core and carriers 1 through 2N as
payload banks. It is separate from the original Morton placement and protected
geometric address records. The experiment realizes the same supplied logical
menu on payloads; it does not preserve address loads in the routing ports or
derive the menu by reading them during execution.

The Lean construction and its composition are kernel checked. Identification
of the Python bank compiler with that construction is the explicit induction
in `DERIVATION.md`, supported by independent finite certificates; no generated
Lean proof term for the entire captured compiler is claimed. The analytic
large-family work bounds are sufficient and expensive. Native event count is
not assigned a spacetime volume or a physical clock. Host controller work,
metadata storage, evidence evaluation and the observation instrument remain
separate costs; the counted scalar word does not include them.
