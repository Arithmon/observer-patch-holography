# Paired weak-field gravity: objective, deliverables and exit

## Objective

On a named source class, determine whether the existing
source response, locality, positive action, shared source calibration and
universal probe dictionary determine both gravitational clock shift and light
deflection. A supplied Einstein equation is a comparison branch, not an OPH
source derivation.

## Deliverables

1. A local positive two-field source action on the existing source-addressed
   golden scalar support, with fixed boundary, source preparation, complete
   record transitions and finite residual certificates at two strengths.
2. A classification of its static response after a common calibration, including
   the complete clock-field Gaussian marginal, and an explicit pair of distinct
   source laws with identical calibrated clock predictions.
3. Derive the clock and optical reads from one fixed metric/matter dictionary;
   calculate their joint range, finite bounds, and the exact extra physical
   condition that selects the Einstein weak-field value.
4. A documented comparison to published static nuclear-resonance redshift and
   solar radio-deflection measurements. Preserve the distinction between
   published compressed measurement results and unavailable raw-data likelihoods;
   retain systematic bounds, shared-data alternatives and all model outcomes.
5. Independent source/operator and observable replay, strict receipts, semantic
   hostile controls, source/data custody, registered claims and Windows/Linux CI.

## Exit

The targeted exit is a constructive nonidentifiability result on the declared
positive local source-action class, with an evaluated, explicitly postdictive
measurement comparison. The proof must exhibit complete source actions, not
merely attach two metrics to an unspecified source or list missing maps.
Neither compatibility with a published interval nor choosing the Einstein
member after seeing data establishes OPH source selection.

Full physical source selection and geometry response, physical
curvature/stress and Einstein composition, calibration, and common-history and
outcome identification are open obligations (historical issues #779, #729,
#736, #740 and #730). This packet states them as retained obligations. It
discharges none of them and asserts no countermodel to their additional
physical Einstein premises.
