\begin{abstract}
Observer-Patch Holography describes finite patches that record shared
boundaries and repair disagreement. On a declared twelve-port branch,
normalized repair response gives a rank-three metric quotient. Signed records
and conservative seam currents are dense in its continuous Euclidean
completion. Conservative Fibonacci record populations, with complete reads
inside a shrinking metric radius and a declared model clock, have causal
orders and normalized counts converging to flat $1+3$ spacetime. Their
ordering fraction in interior causal diamonds tends to $1/10$.
Segment-covering estimates control the finite causal and volume errors.
For nonzero closed convex pointed displacement cones, operational boost
covariance selects Lorentz shape; accessible interval counts reconstruct
proper-time ratios.
A separate positive scalar action on source-metric populations has controlled
continuum detector readings. Modular covariance and a round celestial limit
give Lorentz-frame kinematics. Positive null translations and finite tomography
reconstruct stress tensors under locality, covariance and Ward hypotheses.
With same-family physical stress identification, fixed-cap entropy
stationarity, controlled small-ball and curvature limits, universal coupling,
a vacuum reference and an independent scale, the Einstein equation follows
up to a cosmological constant. Finite MaxEnt identities also determine the
fixed-screen capacity-transfer law and de Sitter shock sign.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Entropy stationarity from a continuing native record}
\label{sec:continuing-record-entropy}

The native population interface admits an entropy calculation after

Assume the tape resolves $q$, and that any retained flag has probability

\begin{proposition}[Counted native-record entropy envelope]
For an initial population $p$, let $p_B$ be its tape law conditional on that
flag. Define
\[
 F(p)=H(p_B)+\mathbb E_p\log d_B
       -\mathbb E_p T_B\log2-\log\beta.
\]
Writing $\sigma$ for the reference population, one has
\[
 F(p)-S(\sigma)
 =h_1\bigl(\langle q\rangle_p-\langle q\rangle_\sigma\bigr)
  +h_2\bigl(\langle c\rangle_p-\langle c\rangle_\sigma\bigr)
  -D(p_B\Vert\sigma_B),
\]
where $h_1=\log(w_0/w_1)$ and $h_2=\log(w_1^2/(w_0w_2))$.
Hence the fixed-moment first variation vanishes at $\sigma$, and its
Hessian is the negative semidefinite Fisher form of the tape response.

\begin{proof}
Injectivity and the retained fair coins give
$\sigma_B(b)=d_b w_{q(b)}2^{-T_b}/\beta$.
Substituting this law in $D(p_B\Vert\sigma_B)$ leaves
$-\sum_b p_B(b)\log w_{q(b)}$.

Finally $-\log w_q=-\log w_0+h_1q+h_2c$.
The second variation of relative entropy is the Fisher form; directions

\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite modular covariance and compressed responses}
\label{sec:finite-modular-covariance}

Geometric modular covariance requires a comparison beyond the existence
of a finite modular clock. The following finite identity states a necessary
boundary of that comparison, independently of the repair scheduler.

\begin{proposition}[Finite modular dilation and its compression boundary]
\label{prop:finite-modular-dilation}
Let $G,P$ be Hermitian matrices on a finite Hilbert space, let
$U(s)=e^{isG}$, and let $\kappa\in\mathbb R$. Then
\[
 \|i[G,P]-\kappa P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 =\|[G,P]\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2+\kappa^2\|P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2,
 \qquad
 \|U(s)PU(s)^*-e^{\kappa s}P\|
 \geq |e^{\kappa s}-1|\,\|P\|.
\]
For an orthogonal projection $Q$, write $G_Q=QGQ$, $P_Q=QPQ$ and
$B_Q=i(QG(1-Q)PQ-QP(1-Q)GQ)$. Then
\[
 Q\,i[G,P]Q=i[G_Q,P_Q]+B_Q,
 \qquad
 \|Q(i[G,P]-\kappa P)Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}+\|B_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \geq |\kappa|\,\|P_Q\|_{\mathrm{HS}}.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Cyclicity gives $\operatorname{Tr}(P\,i[G,P])=0$, so the cross term
in the squared Hilbert--Schmidt norm vanishes. Unitary invariance of
the operator norm and the reverse triangle inequality give the
finite-parameter bound. Inserting $Q+(1-Q)$ into each product gives
the compressed identity. Apply the first identity to the compressed
matrices, then the triangle inequality, for the last bound.
\end{proof}

For the native faithful reference with per-configuration core weights
$(2/225,1/36,2/45)$, take $G=\log\sigma$; thus $s$ is the dimensionless
modular parameter. Choose configurations $a,b$ with core occupation zero
