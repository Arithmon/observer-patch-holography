\subsection{A controlled causal limit with a refining direction menu}
\label{sec:refining-causal-cone}

The three-dimensional response completion supplies a local Euclidean
carrier under its stated repair and metric premises. Populating that carrier
with events is a separate operation. The following construction isolates
the role of the direction menu and shows why exact finite order reflection
is stronger than a controlled effective causal limit requires.

Choose an orthonormal frame in that carrier. For a spatial mesh $h>0$ and
an integer $R>3$, take events $(j,z)\in\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z^3$, placed at
$(Rhj,hz)$. A directed edge advances one layer and adds an integer vector
$v$ with $|v|\le R$, including $v=0$. Precedence is the generated path order.
The frame, mesh, direction menu and interpretation of a layer are supplied.
The order is generated by this local move law; a desired continuum pair
relation is not inserted as an edge table.

\begin{proposition}[Quantitative causal-cone approximation]
\label{prop:refining-causal-cone}
For an integer displacement $d$ and $k\ge1$ layers,
\[
 |d|\le k(R-3)\ \Longrightarrow\ (0,0)\prec(k,d)
 \ \Longrightarrow\ |d|\le kR.
\]
Consequently the placed order lies inside the unit-speed Lorentz cone and
contains its speed-$(1-3/R)$ inner cone. If $R\to\infty$ and $Rh\to0$,
every fixed future-directed strictly timelike pair, approximated on these grids, is ordered
for sufficiently fine meshes; spacelike pairs are excluded for sufficiently
fine meshes. On a bounded time window $T$, uncertain comparisons lie within
a radial cone margin $3T/R$, in addition to endpoint sampling error.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For the inner bound, use the integer path
$z_j=\lfloor jd/k\rfloor$ componentwise, $0\le j\le k$.
Its endpoints are $0,d$. Every coordinate of
$z_{j+1}-z_j-d/k$ has absolute value less than one, so its Euclidean norm
is less than $\sqrt3\le3$. Thus each step has norm at most
$|d|/k+3\le R$. For the outer bound, sum the step norms.
Multiplication by $h$ and division by the duration $kRh$ give the speed
bounds. Grid approximation changes a time coordinate by at most $Rh$ and
each spatial coordinate by at most $h$. A strictly positive timelike or
spacelike margin survives these errors and $3/R\to0$.
The Lean construction formalizes the rounded path and the two finite bounds;
the limit argument uses the displayed estimates.
\end{proof}

This family also has a declared count measure. An event cell has volume
$(Rh)h^3$, so $\rho=(Rh^4)^{-1}$. Weighted counts converge to Euclidean
four-volume for fixed bounded regions with boundary of measure zero by
Riemann summation. For a fixed timelike Alexandrov interval, the two cone
bounds sandwich its discrete order interval between inner and outer cone
intersections. The intervening layer approaches its null boundary, which
has four-dimensional measure zero. The same weighted counts therefore
converge to its Minkowski volume. Same-layer events are incomparable, and
their number in any fixed spatial box grows like $h^{-3}$. This supplies
growing width and an effective interval-volume control for the declared
family, with neither a width-two history nor a fitted dimension statistic.
Finite restrictions require the intervening paths to stay inside the
retained domain; a padded region or the unrestricted grid gives this clause.

At a finite cutoff this regular grid has a preferred frame, and its counts
are not a Poisson process. It is not a Lorentz-invariant sprinkling or a
causal-set faithful embedding in that stronger sense
\cite{bombellihensonsorkin2009}. Nor does it select a physical frame, clock,
density or source law. The growing menu replaces the fixed primitive move
set and is an explicit hypothesis. A fixed axial menu, for example, retains
an $\ell^1$ propagation cone: $(2,2,0)$ is inside the Euclidean cone at
three steps but requires four axial moves. Isotropy of a quadratic
long-wavelength response alone does not repair that path-order discrepancy.

The finite packet in \path{code/causal_refinement/} independently checks
rounded paths, an exhaustive axial control, and threaded read/write
histories on a separate finite layered box. Its patch records retain exact
values and writer stamps; a child explicitly reads the registers of all
allowed preceding-layer neighbors and writes their sum plus one. The
authenticated read relation is precisely the local move relation. Permuting
commits within a layer preserves the resulting state and order. This is a
supplied-law observer-like execution control. It establishes no OPH-native
selection of that law, physical manifold, matter dynamics or continuum QFT.
