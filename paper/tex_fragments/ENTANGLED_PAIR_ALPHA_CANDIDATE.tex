\label{sec:entangled-pair-alpha}

identification. For four slots of dimensions \((3,2,3,2)\), traceless
letters on unordered slot pairs give 169 product records. Three additional
exchange-odd directions give a 172-dimensional nonzero feature space.

\[

\]
the joint projectors have gains \(1,2,3,6\) and ranks \(79,48,42,3\).
The complete declared feature normalization is
\((3^2-1)(2^2-1)(3\cdot2)^4=31104\). Thus
\[

 =\frac{79+48\sqrt2+42\sqrt3+3\sqrt6}{31104},\qquad
 \alpha_{\rm pair}^{-1}=137.035999189566155\ldots.
\]
The executable reconstruction checks the involutions, integer projector
identities, ranks and gains. It fits no continuous coefficient. These are
properties of the stated finite operator and readout normalization.

The six-dimensional matrix algebra and a 24-dimensional mixed traceless
space are compatible finite ingredients for comparison with OPH. Equal
dimensions alone do not identify a state, a current representation or a
photon two-point function. Matching invariance and reading the identity as
one also do not uniquely select normalized trace: the normalized Bell
projector state and the tracial state share those properties and evaluate

tested pair of flat factors gives reduced eigenvalues \(1/5,4/5\) after

construction without changing its canonical scalar arithmetic.
