\subsection{Protected records with decaying repair memory}
\label{sec:protected-record-memory}

A conditional expectation describing equilibrium need not be the transition
operator. This distinction admits conserved records and dissipative memory
on one finite state space. The following construction supplies a local
stochastic law and its faithful reference; neither is identified with a
particular physical source.

Let $X=\coprod_c(\{c\}\times U_c\times V_c)$ be finite, with nonempty
coordinate sets and a strictly positive probability law $\pi$.
On the real space $L^2(\pi)$, let $P$ be conditional expectation given $c$,
$E_u$ conditional expectation given $(c,v)$, and $E_v$ conditional
expectation given $(c,u)$. Their stochastic interpretations resample only
the omitted coordinate from the same reference. Define
\begin{equation}
 T=\tfrac12 I+\tfrac14E_u+\tfrac14E_v.
 \label{eq:protected-memory-lazy-law}
\end{equation}
Thus bounded patches carry local state $(c,u,v)$, a protected record $c$,
coordinate readback ports and conditional repair moves. The step index is
a declared update count, with no laboratory time calibration.

\begin{proposition}[A protected-record dynamics with finite correlation tails]
\label{prop:protected-record-memory}
The kernel $T$ is stochastic, reversible with respect to $\pi$, and fixes
every function of $c$. It satisfies $TP=PT=P$. There is
$0<\varepsilon<1$ such that the entrywise inequality
$T^2\ge\varepsilon P$ holds. Consequently, on $\ker P$,
\begin{equation}
 \|T^n\|\le(1-\varepsilon)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor},\qquad
 \operatorname{gap}(I-T)\ge\varepsilon/2.
 \label{eq:protected-memory-decay}
\end{equation}
The unique fibre-centred Poisson solver is
$R=(I-T+P)^{-1}-P$. For $Pf=Pg=0$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal G(f,g)=\langle f,Rg\rangle_\pi
   =\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\langle f,T^ng\rangle_\pi
 \label{eq:protected-memory-green-kubo}
\end{equation}
is symmetric and positive semidefinite. After retaining lags $0$ through
$N$, its exact remainder and a uniform bound are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal G(f,g)-\sum_{n=0}^{N}\langle f,T^ng\rangle_\pi
   &=\langle f,T^{N+1}Rg\rangle_\pi,\\
 \left|\mathcal G(f,g)-\sum_{n=0}^{N}\langle f,T^ng\rangle_\pi\right|
   &\le\frac{\|f\|_\pi^2+\|g\|_\pi^2}{\varepsilon}
      (1-\varepsilon)^{\lfloor(N+1)/2\rfloor}.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:protected-memory-tail}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Conditional expectations are orthogonal projections in $L^2(\pi)$;
their kernels satisfy detailed balance and preserve $c$. The tower property
gives $TP=PT=P$, and $T\ge I/2$ as an operator. Two consecutive repairs,
first one coordinate and then the other, can reach every state in the same
fibre with positive probability. Finiteness and faithfulness therefore give
the displayed minorization. The remainder
$Q=(T^2-\varepsilon P)/(1-\varepsilon)$ is stochastic and stationary for
$\pi$. Jensen's inequality gives $\|Q\|\le1$. On $\ker P$ this implies
\eqref{eq:protected-memory-decay}, using
$1-\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}\ge\varepsilon/2$ for the gap.
The Neumann series on that subspace defines $R$, while $R$ vanishes on
$\operatorname{ran}P$. Reversibility makes $R$ self-adjoint, and its
eigenvalues there are $(1-\lambda)^{-1}>0$. The finite geometric-series
identity gives the exact remainder. Sum
$|\langle f,T^ng\rangle|\le\|f\|\|g\|
(1-\varepsilon)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ from $N+1$ onwards, grouping
successive pairs and using $2\|f\|\|g\|\le\|f\|^2+\|g\|^2$.
\end{proof}

An exact eight-state instance takes $c,u,v\in\{0,1\}$, fibre masses
$(1/3,2/3)$, and conditional reference weights
$(1,2,3,4)/10$ and $(4,1,2,3)/10$ in lexicographic $(u,v)$ order.
Both references are nonproduct laws. Its sharp two-step minorization is
$\varepsilon=7/96$, giving a certified gap lower bound $7/192$.
For $f=u-Pu$ and $g=v-Pv$, the one-sided coefficient matrix is exactly
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}11417/9375&1832/3125\\
                 1832/3125&3816/3125\end{pmatrix},
\]
and the positive-lag autocorrelation $\langle f,Tf\rangle=443/2400$
is nonzero. An independent rational verifier reconstructs the coordinate
kernels, checks both Poisson inverse identities, and replays 65 correlation
lags, the exact cutoff remainder and eight distribution readouts.

Fibre centering is essential: $c-2/3$ has global mean zero but is fixed by
$T$, with persistent correlation $2/9$ and no centred Poisson solution.
The limiting distribution retains its initial fibre masses. This is not
global ergodicity, an identification of $T$ with the full-fibre projector,
or a dynamic intertwiner recovering a recurrent mode from that projector.
It leaves the obstruction for the distinct source-counted transition
artifact intact. The local stochastic law and reference are supplied;
physical source attachment, transport normalization, clock calibration
and a continuum limit require additional arguments.
