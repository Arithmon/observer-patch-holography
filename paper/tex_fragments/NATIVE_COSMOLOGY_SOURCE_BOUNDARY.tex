\subsection{What the native observer records determine}
\label{subsec:native-cosmology-source-boundary}
The fair balanced integer repair law supplies an absolute spectrum for a
specified load readout, without fitting an observed sky. On a connected
weighted port graph with \(p\) ports and \(r\) raised loads, stationary
continued repair, in the continuous native attempt parameter \(t\) at unit
attempt rate per seam, gives
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(z(t),z(0))=\kappa e^{-|t|L/2}\Pi,\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{r(p-r)}{p(p-1)},\quad
 \Pi=I-\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/p.
\]
For the ordinary local average \(f_T=T^{-1}\int_0^Tz(t)\,dt\),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(v_\lambda^{\mathsf T}f_T)
 =\kappa\frac{2(u-1+e^{-u})}{u^2},\qquad u=\lambda T/2.
\]
The fixed-graph fluctuation record
\(T^{-1/2}\int_0^Tz(t)\,dt\) instead converges to a Gaussian law with
covariance \(4\kappa L^+\). Incident pre-attempt records, including null
attempts and centered with the prepared filling, have the same leading
inverse-Laplacian term with operator correction at most
\(3\kappa/d_{\min}\), at a common native attempt parameter; \(t\) is not a
calibrated observer clock. These are
analytical consequences of actual local port interaction and readback,
checked by finite-state enumeration and independent moment evolution.
The detailed proofs and the distinct occupation and event normalizations
are given in the screen-microphysics companion~\cite{ophmicro}.

The amplitude is in native load-record units. Occupancy, attempt clock,
duration, gain and stationary preparation are specified inputs.
For a centered geometric readout \(q=Bz+g\), orthogonal projection in
the stationary law gives at every finite duration
\[
 C_T(q)=B C_T(z)B^{\mathsf T}+C_T(g),\qquad C_T(g)\succeq0.
\]
Thus a measured collar-volume readout can test its density overlap;
the load record cannot be substituted for that geometric observable.
Along one common subsequence of refining source screens with
\(240\cdot4^l\) ports, one-third filling from the declared uniform
fixed-number preparation, independent uniform seam selection and fair
repair, normalized native occupation snapshots read at native attempt count
\(\lfloor1260\cdot16^l\tau\rfloor\) converge jointly to a stationary
Gaussian family with covariance \(\langle f,e^{-|\tau-u|A}g\rangle\). Here
\(A\) is one positive self-adjoint generator whose form is a conservative,
regular, strongly local Dirichlet form of heat-trace spectral dimension
two~\cite{ophcontinuum,ophtimefields}. These finite-dimensional laws assert
no path-space tightness or spatial convergence rate, and the density field
lives on the two-dimensional screen readout; it is not identified with a
primordial curvature field. With fixed-area
equal port weights and a common deterministic filter of bounded \(L^1\)
gain \(W\), the ensemble mean empirical harmonic pseudo-power satisfies
\(\widetilde C_\ell=(2\ell+1)^{-1}\sum_m\mathbb E|a_{\ell m}|^2
\le\pi W^2/(p-1)\), even without exact quadrature. This is not a
pointwise bound on every sky realization.
Ordinary averages have \(W=1\); amplified fluctuation records do not have
uniformly bounded gain as their duration grows.

There are two additional quantitative restrictions. A common deterministic
temporal filter of stationary density obeys
\(d\log V/d\log\lambda\ge-1\), so under a separately established
three-dimensional \(\lambda\propto k^2\) identification it cannot
produce the required \(k^{-3}\) curvature spectrum. Under the same operator
dictionary that identification fails for the native continuum generator:
its eigenvalues obey \(n/49920\le\lambda_n(A)\le3n/2\), which excludes a
unitary identification, up to a fixed scale and bounded mass shift, with a
second-order elliptic operator on a smooth compact three-dimensional
manifold, and excludes a boundedly invertible energy congruence with the
static boundary action of a smooth compact three-dimensional Dirichlet
bulk~\cite{ophcontinuum}. The native port
operator is also not proved round: at level four the seven-mode band
splitting is \(14.642\%\), compared with \(0.01561\%\) for a supplied
round-metric operator. An inverse graph covariance retains its precision's
anisotropy.

Absolute inherited/fresh/settled covariance is recovered for all 61
retained refinement chains and eight group sizes. For unshuffled
level-eight to level-nine chains at 65,536 carriers per group, the
inherited-only residual variance is \(2.55666955\times10^{-6}\);
removing the linear fresh-load response leaves
\(4.00020779\times10^{-8}\). The first variance times group size is
\(0.16755\), close to the supplied fresh variance \(1/6\).
Nested same-location residuals satisfy
\(\operatorname{Cov}(e_M,e_N)=\operatorname{Var}(e_{\max(M,N)})\);
this is a projection identity, not a separated-position covariance or
independence theorem. Group-dependent inherited responses do not supply
a common refinement generator. The retained sample shares initial fields,
and fresh reconstruction depends on pinned producer and random-number
semantics; it supplies neither \(A_s\) nor \(n_s\).
The specifications, original chain receipts, independent controls and
absolute-covariance extraction are archived~\cite{ophobsdyn}.
