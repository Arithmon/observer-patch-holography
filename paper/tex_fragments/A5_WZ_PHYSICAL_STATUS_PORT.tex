\paragraph{Scope of the gauge and \(W/Z\) results used here.}
The finite icosahedral package used by this paper contains a Lie-type theorem
under adopted premises and an exact conditional recognition result, both
proved in the gauge paper~\cite{ophGauge}. Complete reversible response and
endogenous transport imply the local Standard Model gauge Lie algebra.
Incidence determines the antipode \(J\), and under the explicit contract that
an admissible response is a signed central involutive graph automorphism
implementing inverse-port readback, the responses are exactly \(\pm J\).
Conditional also on the matrix current and the rank-15 matter contract, the
anomaly and tensor-descent certificates fix the \(3+2\) block structure,
hypercharge lattice, common \(\mathbb Z_6\) kernel and maximal faithful matter
image. The source does not select the matrix current, matter action or
physical global quotient. The finite local action, the parallel measure and
Hamiltonian branches, and the strict finite-order perturbative \(W/Z\) pole
theorem state what follows from typed action and quantization packets; they
do not show that the target-free source emits those packets, and no
source-native dimensionless or physical-unit \(W/Z\) pole follows from the
external validation packet.
