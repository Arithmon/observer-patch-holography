\subsection{Source selection and a sufficient record-transport law}
\label{sec:source-record-gluing}

The source-selection question has two different meanings. A unique finite
golden population and radius are stronger requirements than a common
operational causal/count limit. Here we prove a sufficient source law for
the latter, and compare M1 as one regulator of it. The proposed law is
\emph{additional} to A1--A3. It is neither adopted as a fourth core axiom
nor asserted to hold in the registered simulator. Its several substantive
requirements are stated together; no claim of minimality or unique
necessity is made.

\paragraph{Why the existing data-access clauses do not supply a channel.}
There is a scalar-seam construction with a complete port response, rather
than a response-free scalar toy. At each vertex of the normalized midpoint
sphere $S_n$ put
\[
 \mathcal A_i=\bigoplus_{p=1}^{12}M_6(\mathbb C),\qquad
 \mathcal R_i=Z(\mathcal A_i)=\mathbb C^{12}.
\]
Its twelve block units are primitive central ports. Distinct-carrier seams
are $\mathbb C$, with scalar inclusion and the unital normalized trace
$E_i(a)=\sum_p\operatorname{Tr}(a_p)/72$ as visible data restriction.
Triple restrictions are identities. Local chart overlaps are distinguished
from these inter-carrier seams. Their proper action groupoid implements
all sixty carrier rechartings; it does not transmit a private record to
another carrier.

An exact response on the same accessible internal $\mathbb C^6$ is
\[
 D_p=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(
 \widehat{v_p/2}+iQ_p,\widehat{\sigma(v_p)/2}\right),\qquad
 Q_p=\frac{v_pv_p^{\mathsf T}}2-\frac{r^2}6 I+\frac1{12}I,
\]
where $v_p$ is an icosahedral calculation frame, $r^2=\|v_p\|^2$,
$\sigma$ is its Galois conjugation and $\widehat v w=v\times w$.
Even and odd antipodal fields span respectively the six imaginary symmetric
directions and the two three-dimensional real skew spaces. Thus the
response is injective, commutator closed and has image
$\mathfrak u(3)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3)$. Full $M_6$ effects observe its
relative block phase; restricting to block-diagonal effects loses one
observable response direction. For each proper action $a$,
$U_a=\operatorname{diag}(R_a,\sigma(R_a))$ satisfies
$U_aD_pU_a^*=D_{ap}$. Executed Cayley factors in the original response span
realize these matrices, their covariance and their compositions.
The central permutation is the chart map; an inner automorphism of a
carrier algebra is not claimed to permute its center.

The admitted instruments are local: central readout, destructive internal
effect readout into two central ports, requested-block trace reset,
prediction from a supplied preparation and local conditioning, and in-place
checkpoint continuation. Only the declared response span generates
infinitesimal reversible port operations. Irreversible read/reset channels
have kernels and add no reversible direction. Independent ticks carry no
record-dependent controller information between carriers.

The open-star nerve is $S_n$, the bridge is the identity and its fundamental
cycle has degree one. Midpoints coarsen to declared endpoints, transported
under relabeling; fundamental chains push forward exactly and spherical
mesh contracts. Coarse local states average their descendant fibers.
The instrument lift runs independently on every descendant, with each
branch's own outcomes, then uses one fixed local tag through continuation.
Selective conditioning updates tag weights; longer refinements use the
composed weights. The tagged direct-sum experiment is not misidentified
with the descendants' joint tensor algebra or with a communication channel.

The feasible local-state family is unconstrained except for normalization
and chart covariance. Its complete grammar contains all $431$ independent
Hermitian state coordinates per carrier. The declared reference is
$I_{72}/72$, the cover contains every carrier, and the weight is $1/N_n$.
The weighted Umegaki objective has the unique tracial minimizer by
nonnegativity and its equality condition. Every omitted coordinate admits
two positive trace-one perturbations invisible to the remaining coordinates.
This is an explicit A3 problem, not invariance used to choose a reference
among all possible source ontologies.

Induction on every admitted local operation shows that changing an
independent private source port leaves another carrier's entire local
transcript unchanged. The induction permits arbitrary receiver-local
memory and stopping rules, so no decoder of that transcript reads both
interventions correctly. For a uniform unknown port its success probability
is at most $1/12$. Hence the stated typed data-access clauses, with local
chart holonomy distinct from inter-carrier record transport, do not entail
M1's remote read service. The detailed full-clause interpretation and
all-level refinement proof are in
\ophid{code/source_selection_model/DERIVATION.md}; finite receipts do not
replace that analytic model-membership argument. If reversible overlap
holonomy is strengthened to require inter-carrier record transport, this
particular countermodel is excluded by that additional requirement.

\paragraph{Proposed law RG: response-natural classical record transport.}
The complete-response theorem for A1--A2 gives
$\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$ at a complete
reference carrier. Its unique three-dimensional simple ideal $E$, with
the response trace form, has connected adjoint action $SO(E)$.
This is an internal Euclidean response space, not already physical space.
RG says that the central-record source admits a complete,
intervention-faithful, refinement-compatible realization of the following
generated process theory, graded by a source-observable duration:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Objects are arbitrary finite independent classical registers at
addresses in the affine space modeled on $E$. Finite tensor products,
local preparation, finite deterministic classical maps, nondestructive
read/copy, discard and retained versions are realizable at finite cutoff.
Their ideal displacement and local duration are zero. Their actual
implementations and storage costs are retained.
\item One retained-record flight has displacement $e$, $\|e\|=1$, and
duration $\tau>0$. Translation, connected response rotation and positive
scaling generate actual flights $(\lambda Re,\lambda\tau)$ for
$R\in SO(E)$ and $\lambda>0$. Waiting preserves the record. These are
inter-carrier record processes, not only relabelings of a response chart.
\item Sequential and finite parallel composition are realized. Time adds
on dependency paths; independent branches share elapsed time. Every native
dependency, including controller, timing and metadata channels, factors
through the graded local/flight/wait grammar. Conversely every finite
generated process has an admitted native realization preserving all
independent record interventions and immutable versions, with every
intermediate event and its resource bill retained. Realizations commute
with the declared source refinements.
\end{enumerate}
The exact version realizes these ideal grades. The vanishing-overhead
version requires, for every complete finite experiment and every
$\epsilon>0$, a finite realization with exact record values and endpoint
addresses, no faster native dependence, and each logical event time in
$[t,t+\epsilon]$ relative to its ideal schedule. This uniform whole-program
requirement is part of RG. Nonnegative primitive overheads summing to at
most $\epsilon$ on every complete path suffice, by induction on the
earliest-time recurrence using addition and maximum. A fixed error at
each layer is not a substitute.

RG supplies flat homogeneous operational addresses, record protection,
cofinal workspace, parallel composability and a physical connection from
the internal response to record motion. None follows just from internal
rotation covariance. Translations and scales act on a family of finite
experiments, possibly changing cutoff; they are not extra infinitesimal
port generators on one carrier. A proposed implementation must still obey
A1's full response constraint. A3 can select states inside such a source
but is not used to derive RG or compel a particular observer program.

\begin{proposition}[RG suffices for the operational M1 limit]
\label{prop:record-gluing-sufficiency}
Under the complete-response conclusion of A1--A2 and RG, there exist
cofinal finite independent central-record experiments whose native ancestry
on their distinguished records has the round $3+1$-dimensional causal
limit, continuity-set volume counts, Alexandrov interval counts and
four-dimensional ordering fraction. M1's golden population with
$q=F_n$ and observation duration $L/(c\sqrt q)$ is one regulator with
these limits, where $c=1/\tau$. Its finite radius is not a uniquely
selected microscopic length.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each primitive flight has norm $c$ times its duration. The triangle
inequality bounds every complete native dependency by
$\|y-x\|\leq c(s-t)$, including paths through auxiliary events.
Conversely, for $\|v\|\leq cT$, response transitivity rotates $e$ to
$v/\|v\|$. Scaling by $\|v\|$ and waiting for
$T-\tau\|v\|$ realizes the read. The zero displacement uses waiting.
The exact process has precisely the closed round cone; vanishing overhead
attains every strict timelike read and excludes every spacelike read.
Finite tensor composition realizes any finite list simultaneously, with
protected source versions.

In a calculation frame prepare records at
$x_b=Lb/q$, $b\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}^3$. RG supplies their joint finite
preparation, rather than a presumed native carrier census. Half-open cells
have equal volume $(L/q)^3$ and assignment/covering error
$h_q\leq\sqrt3L/q$. Choose $\delta_q\to0$ with
$q\delta_q\to\infty$. Exhaustively observe the preceding population
within duration $\delta_q$, then commit the consumed source versions.
The primitive latency calculation derives the complete read menu
$\|x_b-x_a\|\leq c\delta_q$; this menu is not input to the source law.
The covering-path theorem now constructs a $k$-layer read path whenever
\[
 \|y-x\|\leq k(c\delta_q-2h_q),
 \qquad
 \text{and every generated path obeys}\quad
 \|y-x\|\leq kc\delta_q.
\]
Independently variable records and retained versions make these actual
causal dependencies. Compiling a primitive correctly on every input state
extends to every finite word by induction; concatenating the native words
retains the sum of their event bills.

For each bounded horizon, lower the entire finite experiment with uniform
time error $\epsilon_q\leq\delta_q/q$. Commits need not be perfectly
simultaneous. Let $P_q$ be the consumed-writer order and $N_q$ full native
ancestry restricted to those commits. Faithful lowering and complete native
grading give
\[
 P_q\ \subseteq\ N_q\ \subseteq\ C_c
 \quad\text{at the actual native event assignments.}
\]
Every fixed strict timelike comparison eventually belongs to $P_q$, while
every fixed spacelike or backwards comparison is eventually excluded from
$N_q$. Moving two positions by at most $H$ and times by at most $\epsilon$
changes the causal margin by at most $2H+2c\epsilon$; this tends to zero.

Space and time cells give distinguished-event weight
$w_q=(L/q)^3\delta_q$. For any bounded continuity set $A$ in the window,
its pulled-back indicator converges almost everywhere and is bounded by
one, hence $w_q\#A_q\to\int_A dt\,d^3x$. A clipped terminal layer has
mass error at most $L^3\delta_q$. For intervals, use the order sandwich
and the null measure of the endpoint cone boundaries. For strict pairs,
apply the same argument on the product window to both interval indicators
and the precedence indicator; the time diagonal is also null. Dominated
convergence gives the limits for both $P_q$ and $N_q$, without excluding
additional native edges by assumption. An interior rest diamond of duration
$T$ has $V=\pi c^3T^4/24$ and oriented-pair measure $V^2/20$.
Consequently $2\,\#\mathrm{strictPairs}/[N(N-1)]\to1/10$.

For the golden regulator use the already proved equal-volume assignment
with $h_q\leq2\sqrt3L/q$ and $\delta_q=L/(c\sqrt q)$. RG supplies
the finite preparations and transmissions, and all previous bounds apply.
The radius $L/\sqrt q=c\delta_q$ is the reach of the chosen observation
clock. Both regulators thus converge to the same causal/count observables.
For a $K$-Lipschitz observable, assignment errors $(H,\epsilon)$ change
its weighted integral by at most $K(H+\epsilon)$ times the window volume,
plus the clipped endpoint term. Comparing regulators adds their bounds;
indicator comparisons use null boundary tubes instead.
\end{proof}

This conclusion concerns a realizable observer probe and its operational
limit. It neither selects spontaneous execution of that probe nor equates
its distinguished-event volume with a count of all microscopic repair
events. It proves no Lorentz covariance of matter, curved geometry or
laboratory clock calibration. Finite graphs need not be isomorphic, and
unrelated field dynamics do not inherit equivalence from count convergence.

\paragraph{Finite clearance, verification and deletion controls.}
The golden squared displacements in units of $L^2$ lie in
$\mathbb Z[\phi]$. For integer $q>1$ none equals $1/q$: irrationality
would first force the coefficient of $\phi$ to vanish, leaving an integer
equal to $1/q$. Thus every admitted nonself golden flight has positive
finite slack, although controller/commit costs remain charged.
The rational-grid reference uses $L=c=1$ and
$\delta_q^2=2/(2q+1)$; boundary equality would require
$(2q+1)m=2q^2$, impossible since $\gcd(2q+1,q)=1$.

The reference prepares, forks, flies, waits, adds locally and commits two
layers for $q=2,3,5$. Baselines and every single-site intervention retain
163 executions, totaling 2,683,568 primitive events. Compact commitments
are checked against live streams and independent semantic replay, including
all consumed writers and intervention path multiplicities. These are
executions of RG's reference grammar, not of an asserted native simulator
implementation. Source pins and hostile-input tests reject forged timing,
values, missing events and filtered intervention denominators.

The Lean proofs cover the local information bound, the primitive cone and
its covering paths, the lowering induction, event bills, population size
and margin stability. The full model interpretation and the join from RG
to native ancestry and counts are analytic. Detailed proofs appear in
\ophid{code/source_selection_model/RECORD_GLUING.md}.

Deleting transport gives the scalar-seam control; keeping only axis flights
gives an $\ell^1$ cone; an ungraded metadata channel defeats the upper
cone bound; destructive transfer or absent parallel workspace defeats the
finite experiment. These controls explain the content of the proposed law
instead of concealing it in a successful M1 schedule.
