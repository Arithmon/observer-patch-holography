\section{Recovery from finite federation logs}
\label{app:federation-log-recovery}

The local repair carrier and the complete glued federation have different
identification problems. Fix the supplied twelve-port register interface,
protected records, exact half-unit arithmetic and operation grammar of the
routed read program. A blind decoder receives only the eight integer fields
of each authenticated event. It checks the actual consumed writers and
operation values, reconstructs the observed port uses, initial address
records, logical reads and publication ancestry. The decoder cannot read the
generator, its input file, or the complete support graph. Archive decoding
and authentication take place outside this interface.

The retained level-three control contains $1{,}280$ prepared carriers,
$27$ populated hosts, $22{,}818$ events and $81$ logical publication or commit
records. Its $622$ logical reads induce $1{,}331$ strict ancestry pairs among
those records. Its $872$ mean events use only $86$ distinct seams. These are
finite observed quantities. The six recorded address coefficients, opcode
meaning, arithmetic convention and authenticated completeness of the supplied
log are premises; neither a general population law nor a physical clock is
inferred from them.

\paragraph{Indistinguishable complete gluings.}
Write a seam as $(a,p,b,q)$, connecting port $p$ of carrier $a$ to port $q$
of carrier $b$. In the supplied level-three support replace
\[
 (120,5,123,4),\quad (132,9,135,11)
 \quad\text{by}\quad
 (120,5,135,11),\quad (123,4,132,9).
\]
Both graphs are connected, have $7{,}650$ seams and preserve every carrier's
degree and occupied ports. All $27$ complete ascending-neighbour
breadth-first parent maps and queue orders agree. None of the changed seams
occurs in the retained executions. Running the same native producer with the
two input graphs gives byte-identical event histories for the baseline and
the source, branch and scratch interventions. The carrier graphs contain
$14{,}000$ and $13{,}990$ triangles respectively, so no graph isomorphism
identifies them. Consequently, no function of these histories alone can
recover the complete graph's triangle count on this candidate class. Full
gluing is a failed identification invariant even when carrier count, seam
count, degrees, occupied ports and the complete source traversals are given.

The candidate class here is the declared connected finite twelve-port
gluing interface. The second graph is not asserted to be a canonical
icospherical refinement. Restricting to that geometric family adds
information outside the log. The counterexample neither excludes recovery
under an explicit such restriction nor excludes a richer probing protocol.
The supplied feedback word includes export, reset, capture and protected
publication; its execution is not a derivation of that word from pair means.
Re-execution of the observed finite program cannot establish recovery of
unvisited topology or a universe-level fixed point. The executable evidence,
independent verifier and wrong-law and wrong-wiring controls are in
\texttt{evidence/federation\_recovery/}.
