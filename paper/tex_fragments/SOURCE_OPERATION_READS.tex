\subsection{From registered source operations to numerical reads}
\label{sec:source-operation-reads}

A finite source can be tested before assigning it a metric read menu.
We use the simulator's registered all-port capture, whose operations differ
from both the shared-vertex W12 routing interface and the group-valued
repair engine. Its local $\mathbb C^{12}$ unitary propagation precedes a
separate scalar intensity-ledger repair loop. Each of the twelve port
labels has a perfect carrier matching. On $N$ even carriers, the resulting
port-slot graph consists of $6N$ disjoint pairs, although the carrier graph
is connected. Snapshot records expose twelve numerical ledger entries;
the subsequent observer feedback chooses a displayed port without changing
the earlier source evolution.

Let $\mu$ pair the $12N$ slots and let $A$ be a union of pairs. Define
$(P_Ax)_i=(x_i+x_{\mu(i)})/2$ for $i\in A$ and $x_i$ otherwise. Then
\[
 P_AP_B=P_{A\cup B},\qquad P_A^2=P_A,\qquad P_AP_B=P_BP_A.
\]
These identities classify every finite ideal repair word, with exactly
$2^{6N}$ distinct maps. Full coverage gives an ambient projection of rank
$6N$. The native thresholded binary64 maps also commute and become
idempotent after their first visit, but are not linear. Each pair commits
at most once, reading version zero and writing version one. A cyclic
scheduler with $k=\lceil6N/16\rceil$ attempts per cycle covers all pairs
within $\lceil6N/k\rceil$ cycles; later attempts are no-ops. There are no
committed repair-to-repair dependencies at any duration.

\paragraph{Identifiable records and an explicit failure.}
Give one carrier all its initial coordinates and retained local samples,
an information upper bound for its actual snapshot records. A visited
pair reveals its initial partner through $x_b=2x_{\rm after}-x_a$ in exact
arithmetic. No further slot can influence this channel under any repair
word. On the product of normalized preparation simplexes, eleven known
coordinates determine the twelfth; two unobserved coordinates admit a
balanced perturbation. The registered alternating matchings ensure that
the single-carrier observation rank after full coverage is $23$ for
$N\geq4$, leaving $11N-23$ hidden dimensions. Actual observer zero has
two support carriers and six snapshots; their normalized operator rows,
numeric payloads, feedback ports and committed writer edges are separately
reconstructed.

Choose two unobserved coordinates $p,q$ on one remote carrier. Starting
from $1/12$ everywhere, exchanging the values $5/48$ and $1/16$ at these
coordinates gives two strictly positive normalized preparations with the
same receiver payload under every common word. Every binary decoder has
error $1/2$ under the balanced prior. The corresponding diagonal failure
effect is positive. This is a witness in the native operations' open
preparation interface, not a claim that a fixed integer seed realizes it.
Global seeds and custody hashes can distinguish preparations and are not
part of this local numeric channel. Applying finite A3 support preservation
additionally requires membership in the canonical feasible family and a
positive pullback to a scored algebra; the capture supplies neither.

\paragraph{Propagation and selector controls.}
The intensity ledger is insufficient for recurrent local propagation.
For adjacent ports $a,b$, the states $(|a\rangle\pm i|b\rangle)/\sqrt2$
have equal intensities but different intensities after the actual
$U(t)=\exp(-itL)$. Since $\|L\|\leq10$, the difference at port $a$ has
magnitude at least $2t-400t^2$. At $t=2^{-17}$ this lower bound is
$16359/1073741824>0$. No transition kernel depending only on current
intensities reproduces both outputs. Phases or a specified instrument
are required; this does not obstruct a full quantum instrument.
An independent rational Taylor enclosure also proves all $144$ entries
of $U(2^{-17})$ nonzero. Generator adjacency is therefore distinct from
exact finite-step dependence.

The native feedback provides a separate covariance control. At a uniform
snapshot and current port zero it returns port two. An order-five
icosahedral rotation fixes the input port and uniform state but moves the
output port. Thus this feedback function is not an equivariant protected
port selector. Covariance of the local Laplacian alone does not establish
covariance of the record process.

\paragraph{Evidence and scope.}
Seven complete captures vary $N=4,8,16$, duration and support level.
An independent standard-library checker replays every attempt and native
log commitment, reconstructs actual numeric observer records, and checks
the exact rank and phase controls. Live verification binds these results
to a pinned simulator commit and source bytes. Lean kernel-checks matching
composition, all-word dependency, recovery and decoder obstruction, and
the scalar phase-bound arithmetic. The normalized rank, matrix Taylor
argument and executable identification are analytic with independent
finite checks. The source population remains an input; changing its
support-level field leaves this operation family unchanged. Longer runs
past coverage cannot produce additional transport. No spatial coarsening,
canonical A1--A3 family or M1 radius follows from this driver. The result
identifies the exact read relation it does implement, without inserting
the target geometry or filtering histories for successful reads.
