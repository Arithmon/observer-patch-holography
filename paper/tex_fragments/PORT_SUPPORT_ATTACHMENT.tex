\paragraph{Local ports and the spherical support.}
A degree-one federation bridge and each carrier's local twelve-port boundary
are separate data. In the declared source construction, the local oriented
icosahedral boundary \(K\) and the level-zero support \(S_0\) have the same
ordered face table. Let \(s_0:S_0\to N_0\) be the designated section of the
nerve projection \(b_0:N_0\to S_0\), and set
\(z_0=(s_0)_*[S_0]\).

\begin{proposition}[Oriented seed attachments]
\label{prop:port-support-seed-attachments}
The maps \(h_0=s_0\circ p\), where \(p:K\to S_0\) ranges over
orientation-preserving simplicial isomorphisms, form a set of sixty elements
with a free transitive \(A_5\) action. Every such map satisfies
\[
 (h_0)_*[K]=z_0
\]
as an integer chain. For the fixed source row with fibre sizes
\((2,1,\ldots,1)\), the designated section and the process link within the
double fibre, the proper support relabelings preserving this decoration
form \(C_5\). Their action on the sixty maps has twelve orbits.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The shared oriented face table supplies an isomorphism, so every other one
is its composite with a proper automorphism of the icosahedron. These
automorphisms form \(A_5\), of order sixty. Composition acts freely and
transitively. Each automorphism permutes the twenty oriented triangles,
giving \(p_*[K]=[S_0]\) exactly; composing with the section proves the
chain identity. The double fibre singles out one support vertex. Its
stabilizer consists of the five rotations around that vertex and preserves
the section and process link. This subgroup acts freely on the seed maps,
so every orbit has five elements and there are \(60/5=12\) orbits.
\end{proof}

The twelve orbits refer to this fixed marked presentation. They do not
classify physical alternatives or equivalence after jointly transporting
the source decoration and local port charts. Exact enumeration and a
separate reconstruction check the sixty maps and their chain images in
\path{code/a5_closure/port_gram_support_attachment_certificate.py} and
\path{code/a5_closure/verify_port_gram_support_attachment_independent.py}.
The support checks also verify \((b_r)_*z_r=[S_r]\) and the section/coarsening
squares at four stages with \(12,42,162,642\) support vertices.

This seed classification does not supply a source-selected attachment.
The analyzed source definitions prescribe each local boundary independently
of the federation nerve. The local carrier construction scales barycentric
coordinates within a single face, whereas the support tower uses midpoint
subdivision and fibre coarsening. Those data do not specify a common local
incidence tower and a refinement-commuting family \(h_r\).

The two local Galois radial maps have degrees one and seven. A comparison
with the degree-one support bridge requires a source-bound attachment and
continuous realizations as maps between the same oriented \(S^2\), with
the same orientation convention. Under these hypotheses degree classifies
their homotopy classes: the degree-one radial map is homotopic to the
support composite, and the degree-seven map cannot. The equality of seed
tables alone supplies neither those hypotheses nor a physical direction,
metric or scale.
