\subsection{Causal order on a source-metric population}
\label{sec:source-net-causal-limit}

The continuous rank-three repair carrier admits finite populations chosen
by its own metric. A covering property of such a population also supplies a
constructive causal approximation, with an explicit local read law and
model clock. Let $\Omega$ be a convex subset of a real normed space, and let
$S\subset\Omega$ satisfy
\[
 \forall x\in\Omega\quad\exists s\in S:\quad\|s-x\|\leq h,
 \qquad h\geq0.
\]
For an edge radius $a>2h$, put an event at every $(j,s)$,
$j\in\{0,\ldots,K\}$, $s\in S$. An event at $(j+1,t)$ reads every
$(j,s)$ with $\|t-s\|\leq a$. This includes the same-site read, so waiting
is permitted. The event order is the reflexive transitive closure of these
reads. Reading every permitted neighbour, keeping the full population at
each layer, and assigning a duration $\Delta=a/c$ with $c>0$ are hypotheses
of this construction. They are substantive choices of dynamics and clock.

\begin{proposition}[Covering constructs causal paths]
\label{prop:source-net-causal-path}
For $s,t\in S$ and $k\geq1$, with both layers present,
\begin{equation}
 \|t-s\|\leq k(a-2h)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 (j,s)\preceq(j+k,t)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|t-s\|\leq ka.
 \label{eq:source-net-cone-sandwich}
\end{equation}
If $S$ is finite and nonempty, this layered poset has width exactly $|S|$.
Every event at layer $j$ has height $j$, counting edges from layer zero.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Divide the segment from $s$ to $t$ into $k$ equal pieces, with division
points $x_i=s+i(t-s)/k$. Convexity gives $x_i\in\Omega$. Keep the endpoints
$p_0=s$, $p_k=t$ and choose $p_i\in S$ with $\|p_i-x_i\|\leq h$ at every
interior division point. Then
\[
 \|p_{i+1}-p_i\|
 \leq \|t-s\|/k+2h\leq a.
\]
These are actual permitted reads. For any permitted path the triangle
inequality gives the opposite bound in
\eqref{eq:source-net-cone-sandwich}. A layer is an antichain, and the
same-site waiting chains partition all events into $|S|$ chains. This proves
the width statement. Every read increases the layer by one, and a waiting
chain reaches every layer, proving the height statement.
\end{proof}

The path construction and outer displacement bound hold in arbitrary real
normed spaces; \path{Lean/Geometry/SourceNetCausalCone.lean} formalizes them.
The partial order, its equality with the reflexive transitive closure of the
reads, the graph-distance characterization, and the width and height
statements are formalized in \path{Lean/Geometry/SourceNetLayeredOrder.lean}.
The volume estimates below are analytic results for the three-dimensional
Euclidean repair carrier.

For model time $\tau=k\Delta$, the two radii in
\eqref{eq:source-net-cone-sandwich} are
$c(1-2h/a)\tau$ and $c\tau$. Consider a family with
\begin{equation}
 a_n\longrightarrow0,\qquad h_n/a_n\longrightarrow0.
 \label{eq:source-net-causal-refinement}
\end{equation}
The read law agrees with the speed-$c$ continuum causal order away from its
null boundary. More explicitly, take two continuum events with positive
time separation whose positions lie in $\Omega$. Sample positions within
$h_n$ and times on the layer grid within $\Delta_n$. A strict timelike
margin eventually exceeds the endpoint errors, the time rounding errors,
and $2ch_n/a_n$ times the sampled duration; the inner implication then
applies. A strict spacelike margin eventually exceeds the endpoint and time
errors, and the outer implication forbids a path. This statement requires
the sampled layers to exist in the chosen time window. It makes no assertion
of exact order reflection for null-separated events at a finite resolution.

\paragraph{Volume from a controlled population measure.}
Suppose $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^3$ is bounded and convex, and $S$ is finite.
In addition to the $h$-covering population $S$, supply a measurable partition
$(C_s)_{s\in S}$ of $\Omega$, up to null sets, with positive masses
$v_s=\operatorname{vol}(C_s)$ and assignment error
\begin{equation}
 y\in C_s\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\|y-s\|\leq H
 \quad\hbox{for almost every }y.
 \label{eq:source-net-cell-assignment}
\end{equation}
Clipped Voronoi cells allow $H=h$. Equal-volume cells with a different
assignment bound are also admitted; no Voronoi hypothesis is required.
For $x\in S$ and $T=K\Delta>0$, let $I$ be the inclusive discrete interval
between $(0,x)$ and $(K,x)$, and assign it spacetime mass
\[
 V_{S,\Delta}(I)=\Delta\sum_{(j,s)\in I}v_s.
\]
The spatial assignment rule and the temporal factor are part of the measure.
The limiting volume convention is $dt\,d^3x$. For the metric
$c^2dt^2-|dx|^2$, its metric volume is $c$ times this coordinate volume.

\begin{proposition}[Weighted Alexandrov-volume limit]
\label{prop:source-net-alexandrov-volume}
Assume the preceding hypotheses and
$B(x,cT/2+H)\subset\Omega$. Then
\begin{align}
 \left|V_{S,\Delta}(I)-\frac{\pi c^3T^4}{24}\right|
 &\leq 4\pi(T+\Delta)(cT/2+H)^2
       \left(H+\frac{cTh}{a}\right)
       +\frac{\pi c^3T^3\Delta}{2}.
 \label{eq:source-net-volume-error}
\end{align}
Thus $H_n\to0$ and \eqref{eq:source-net-causal-refinement} imply the
continuum Alexandrov-volume limit on aligned, bounded interior time
windows. If all cells have a common mass $v$, this is the raw event-count
statement $v\Delta\,|I|\to\pi c^3T^4/24$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $u_j=\min(j\Delta,T-j\Delta)$ and $r_j=cu_j$. The causal sandwich
puts every population point within $c(1-2h/a)u_j$ of $x$ in the interval,
and puts every interval point within $r_j$. At the endpoint layers the
interval contains precisely the single endpoint. The partition assignment
therefore gives, at every layer,
\[
 \frac{4\pi}{3}\bigl(c(1-2h/a)u_j-H\bigr)_+^3
 \leq\sum_{(j,s)\in I}v_s
 \leq\frac{4\pi}{3}(r_j+H)^3.
\]
For the lower estimate, a point in the smaller ball is assigned to a
population point inside the inner causal radius. For the upper estimate,
the union of cells assigned to interval points lies in the larger ball.
The stated interior buffer keeps these balls in the spatial window.
The deviation of either radius from $r_j$ is at most
$H+cTh/a$. Since the derivative of $(4\pi/3)r^3$ is $4\pi r^2$, the
error per layer is bounded by
$4\pi(cT/2+H)^2(H+cTh/a)$.

Finally, $F(t)=(4\pi/3)[c\min(t,T-t)]^3$ has Lipschitz constant
$\pi c^3T^2$, vanishes at both endpoints, and integrates to
$\pi c^3T^4/24$. Its left Riemann sum differs from its integral by at most
$\pi c^3T^3\Delta/2$. The inclusive right endpoint contributes zero to
this reference sum. Summing the spatial errors over $K+1$ layers proves
\eqref{eq:source-net-volume-error}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Interior diamonds with moving tips]
\label{cor:source-net-general-diamond}
Let $x,y\in S$, $\|y-x\|<cT$, and $T=K\Delta>0$. Suppose the
continuum diamond between $(0,x)$ and $(T,y)$ has a positive spatial
buffer inside $\Omega$. Write
\[
 D(t)=B(x,ct)\cap B(y,c(T-t)),\qquad
 V(D)=\int_0^T\operatorname{vol}(D(t))\,dt.
\]
For the inclusive discrete interval $I$ between these tips,
\begin{align}
 |V_{S,\Delta}(I)-V(D)|
 &\leq 8\pi(T+\Delta)(cT+H)^2
       \left(H+\frac{2cTh}{a}\right)
       +4\pi c^3T^3\Delta.
 \label{eq:source-net-general-volume-error}
\end{align}
In particular, the same refinement conditions give weighted interval-volume
convergence for arbitrary fixed interior timelike diamonds.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At layer $j$, the union of assigned cells contains the intersection of
the two balls with radii
$(c(1-2h/a)j\Delta-H)_+$ and
$(c(1-2h/a)(T-j\Delta)-H)_+$, up to null sets. It is contained in the
intersection with radii $cj\Delta+H$ and $c(T-j\Delta)+H$.
Zero-radius balls contribute zero volume. Changing either reference
radius changes the intersection only within that ball's spherical shell.
Each radial error is at most $H+2cTh/a$, and each outer radius is at most
$cT+H$. The sum of the two shell bounds gives the spatial term in
\eqref{eq:source-net-general-volume-error}. The reference section volume
has Lipschitz constant at most $8\pi c^3T^2$: over a time increment,
each of the two radii changes at speed $c$, and each ball has surface area
at most $4\pi c^2T^2$. Both endpoint sections have zero volume. The same
Riemann-sum argument gives the temporal term.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A conservative-record family with a raw count law.}
The population and equal-mass hypotheses have an explicit realization in
conservative source records. In the exact source-axis presentation, write
$\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ and $L=2/\sqrt{\varphi+2}$. For consecutive Fibonacci
numbers $q=F_n$, $p=F_{n+1}$, with $n\geq4$, define
\[
 \xi_b=b\varphi-\lfloor b\varphi\rfloor,\qquad 0\leq b<q.
\]
For a triple $b=(b_1,b_2,b_3)$, put
$m_i=-\lfloor b_i\varphi\rfloor$ and take the integer record
\begin{equation}
 z(b)=(b_2-m_1,b_2+m_1,b_3-m_2,b_3+m_2,b_1-m_3,b_1+m_3).
 \label{eq:golden-source-record}
\end{equation}
Its coordinate sum is $2(b_1+b_2+b_3)$. It therefore lies in the exact
conservative seam-current image, whose integer section supplies a finite
source word. Direct contraction with the source axes gives the readback
\[
 s(b)=L(\xi_{b_1},\xi_{b_2},\xi_{b_3})\in\Omega=[0,L]^3.
\]
These are actual source-record images. A regular mesh is used below only
to prove a volume estimate; it does not replace the event positions.

\begin{proposition}[Source-record quadrature and four-dimensional counting]
\label{prop:golden-source-count-limit}
The population $S_q=\{s(b):0\leq b_i<q\}$ has exactly $q^3$ distinct
sites. It admits a partition of $\Omega$ into cells of common volume
$v_q=L^3/q^3$, assigned to the sites with error
\begin{equation}
 H_q=\frac{2\sqrt3 L}{q}.
 \label{eq:golden-source-assignment}
\end{equation}
Consequently, for every Lipschitz function $f$ on $\Omega$,
\[
 \left|v_q\sum_{s\in S_q}f(s)-\int_\Omega f(x)\,d^3x\right|
 \leq L^3 H_q\operatorname{Lip}(f).
\]
Apply the preceding complete-neighbour read law on $S_q$, with
$a_q=L/\sqrt q$ and $\Delta_q=a_q/c$. Its density in the declared
$dt\,d^3x$ convention is
\begin{equation}
 \rho_q=\frac{q^3}{L^3\Delta_q}.
 \label{eq:golden-source-event-density}
\end{equation}
Normalized raw event counts converge to volume on fixed bounded regions
with volume-null boundary. On intervals whose endpoints converge to a
strictly timelike pair and whose limiting diamond has an interior buffer
in the spatial and temporal windows, the generated order intervals
converge to that same diamond in count measure. In particular, for vertical
tips separated by model time $T$,
\[
 \frac{|I_q|}{\rho_q}\longrightarrow\frac{\pi c^3T^4}{24}.
\]
The local limiting order and measure are those of flat $1+3$ spacetime.
This conclusion concerns this declared source-record family and update law.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The Fibonacci recurrence gives $\gcd(p,q)=1$ and
$p-q\varphi=(-1)^n\varphi^{-n}$, hence
$|\varphi-p/q|<q^{-2}$. Let $k_b$ be the residue of $bp$ modulo $q$.
These residues permute $0,\ldots,q-1$. For $b=0$ the two floors below
are zero. For $0<b<q$, $bp/q$ is at least $1/q$ from either adjacent
integer, whereas $|b(\varphi-p/q)|<1/q$. Therefore
\[
 \lfloor b\varphi\rfloor=\lfloor bp/q\rfloor,\qquad
 \xi_b=\frac{k_b}{q}+b(\varphi-p/q),\qquad
 \left|\xi_b-\frac{k_b}{q}\right|<\frac1q.
\]
Irrationality of $\varphi$ makes the orbit points distinct; the source
readback then distinguishes all $q^3$ records.

Assign to $s(b)$ the rectangular cell
$\prod_i[Lk_{b_i}/q,L(k_{b_i}+1)/q]$, resolving cell-boundary ties
arbitrarily. This is a partition up to null sets. A site need not belong
to its assigned cell, but every coordinate displacement from a point of
that cell is at most $2L/q$. This proves
\eqref{eq:golden-source-assignment}, the covering property and the
Lipschitz estimate by integrating the displacement bound over each cell.
These spatial arithmetic, partition and quadrature statements have Lean
proofs in \texttt{GoldenSourceAssignment}. The module
\texttt{GoldenSourceCountLimit} also proves normalized actual event-count
convergence on each fixed measurable continuity set for the declared time
grid, with final-layer discrepancy at most $L^3\Delta_q$. The causal-boundary,
moving-interval and strict-pair specializations below are analytic.
Taking products with time cells $[j\Delta_q,(j+1)\Delta_q]$ proves
convergence of the normalized event measure: for a Lipschitz test function
on an aligned time window of length $T$, the error is at most
\[
 TL^3\bigl(H_q\operatorname{Lip}_x(f)
               +\Delta_q\operatorname{Lip}_t(f)\bigr).
\]
An extra endpoint layer contributes at most
$L^3\Delta_q\|f\|_\infty$. Continuous approximation then gives the
count statement for regions with volume-null boundary.

Use $h_q\leq H_q$ in the causal sandwich.
Here $a_q\to0$ and $H_q/a_q=2\sqrt3/\sqrt q\to0$; the sufficient
positive-inner-cone condition from this bound holds when $q>48$.
For converging timelike tips, the order and sampling discrepancies are
confined to shrinking neighbourhoods of the two limiting null boundaries.
Their volume is zero. The uniform cell displacement and temporal error
also tend to zero, so the normalized number of events in these shrinking
neighbourhoods tends to zero. This proves interval-count convergence;
for vertical tips it also follows directly from
\eqref{eq:source-net-volume-error}. The formula for the flat diamond is
its elementary ball-section integral.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The four-dimensional ordering fraction]
\label{cor:source-record-ordering-fraction}
For the contained timelike intervals in
Proposition~\ref{prop:golden-source-count-limit}, let $N_q=|I_q|$ and let
$C_q$ count their strict causally ordered pairs. Then
\[
 \frac{2C_q}{N_q(N_q-1)}\longrightarrow\frac1{10},
\]
the four-dimensional Myrheim--Meyer fraction~\cite{reid2003}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The cell assignment gives convergence also for the product event measure.
Interval-membership boundaries, the pairwise null-relation boundary and
the coincidence diagonal have zero limiting measure. The causal sandwich
confines disagreement to shrinking neighbourhoods of these boundaries.
Hence the normalized strict-pair count tends to its continuum double
integral; $N_q\to\infty$ removes the distinction between $N_q^2$ and
$N_q(N_q-1)$.

In units $c=1$, place the limiting tips at $(0,0)$ and $(T,0)$ and write
$V=\pi T^4/24$. The volume to the future of $(t,x)$ within the interval is
$\pi[(T-t)^2-|x|^2]^2/24$. Thus the strict oriented-pair integral is
\[
 \frac{\pi^2}{6}\int_0^T\!\int_0^{\min(t,T-t)}
       r^2\bigl((T-t)^2-r^2\bigr)^2\,dr\,dt
   =\frac{\pi^2T^8}{11520}=\frac{V^2}{20}.
\]
The ordering fraction counts both orientations of distinct comparable
pairs, giving $1/10$. Constant changes of time and volume units cancel in
the ratio. This is an interval statistic; the same value is not asserted
for a whole spatial box times a time window.
\end{proof}

A sharper finite covering constant is available without changing this
construction. Sort the one-dimensional orbit as
$0=\eta_0<\cdots<\eta_{q-1}<1$ and put
\[
 r_q=\max\left\{\frac12\max_{i<q-1}(\eta_{i+1}-\eta_i),
                     1-\eta_{q-1}\right\},\qquad
 h_q=\sqrt3 Lr_q.
\]
The product population has exactly this covering radius in the closed
cube. The path condition $2h_q<a_q$ is the exact quadratic-field inequality
$12qr_q^2<1$. This sharper $h_q$ concerns paths; the assignment estimate
$H_q$ in the count proof is separate.

The source-word section has coefficients growing at most linearly in $q$;
the finite implementation records their actual primitive lengths.
Counting one distinguished event per site and layer is part of the stated
law. Intermediate seam-word operations, read instructions and bookkeeping
records are not silently included in the same density. The spatial
populations are nested along increasing Fibonacci cutoffs. Their changing
layer duration and edge radius do not make the spacetime histories induced
prefixes; regulator convergence uses the common metric carrier and controlled
cell assignments.

\paragraph{Source populations and physical interpretation.}
The finite-word source construction supplies arbitrarily fine metric nets
in bounded convex windows with nonempty interior in the rank-three
completion. A maximal separated
subset retains a controlled fill distance and three-dimensional packing
bounds. These statements supply the spatial hypotheses of
Proposition~\ref{prop:source-net-causal-path} under a specified resolution
and population rule. Three-dimensional packing bounds alone do not supply
equal-volume sampling. The count conclusion requires a partition satisfying
\eqref{eq:source-net-cell-assignment} and, for unweighted counts, equal
cell masses or a separate counting approximation.

The construction gives a causal approximation on source-metric points
without inserting a Cartesian spatial table. The complete neighbour read
law, repeated population, cell measure, and model duration are supplied.
An implementation by accepted observer repairs must realize these inputs
with its actual records and operation costs. A scalar action on the same
population can have a different finite propagation relation; the causal
cone proved here is not an identification of that action's stencil cone
or its continuum response speed. Agreement with a field action and a
physical clock is an additional common-model condition.
