\section{M1 necessity: sparse reads and observable dependence}
\label{sec:m1-necessity}

The complete metric-neighbor menu is sufficient for the preceding causal/count
limit. Explicit sparse menus give the same named limits
and exhibit a downstream observable that changes. No new transport axiom or
assumption of successful convergence enters the construction. The finite
graphs and actions below are mathematical regulators; source selection,
physical clocks and a native operation grammar remain separate requirements.

\paragraph{Three explicit families.}
For $n\ge1$ put $R=2^n$, $q=R^2$, $h=L/q$ and
$\delta=hR/c=L/(cR)$. Place $q^3$ sites at $hz$ for
$z\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}^3$. Event $(j,z)$ has time $j\delta$ and
weight $\delta h^3$. A symmetric integer stencil $V$ containing zero specifies
all preceding-layer reads, clipped at the box boundary. Let $B$ contain every
integer vector of Euclidean norm at most $R$. Construct $S$ from the vectors
\[
 v(w)_i=\operatorname{sgn}(w_i)
 \left\lfloor\frac{R|w_i|}{\sqrt{\sum_j w_j^2}}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad w\in\mathbb Z^3,\quad\|w\|_\infty=n,
\]
all $\pm2^b e_i$ with $0\le b\le n$, and zero. Remove duplicates.
Finally let $A=S\cup\{\pm j e_i:1\le j\le R\}$. Then
\[
 S\subseteq A\subseteq B,\qquad
 |S|\le D_n:=24n^2+6n+9,\qquad |A|\le D_n+6R.
\]
All three menus have coordinate-permutation and sign-change symmetry, and
all use the same events, times and weights.

\paragraph{Constructive propagation bound.}
For a nonzero displacement $d\in\mathbb Z^3$, write $r=\|d\|_2$.
Round $nd/\|d\|_\infty$ to its nearest integer vector $w$, with half ties
away from zero. Normalizing and rounding towards zero gives
\[
 \|v(w)-Rd/r\|_1\le 3R/n+3.
\]
Take $k=\lfloor r/R\rfloor$ copies of $v(w)$. The residual $e=d-kv(w)$
satisfies $\|e\|_1\le3r/n+3r/R+2R$. Correct each coordinate by repeated
signed $R$-steps and the binary digits of its remainder. This takes at most
$\|e\|_1/R+3n$ additional steps and constructs an exact route with
\begin{equation}
 \frac rR\le N\le\left(1+\frac3n+\frac3R\right)\frac rR+3n+2.
 \label{eq:m1-sparse-route}
\end{equation}
The route remains within $3r/n+3r/R+2R$ of the straight segment: its first
part follows the chosen flight, and the coordinate corrections approach the
endpoint monotonically. Thus it fits in the box whenever this tube does.
Zero steps fill any extra integer duration. In physical units, the excess
route time is at most
\[
 \left(\frac3n+\frac3R\right)\frac{\|hd\|_2}{c}
       +\frac{(3n+2)L}{cR},
\]
and the tube thickness tends uniformly to zero on bounded displacements.

\paragraph{Preserved spacetime and count conclusions.}
Every fixed interior strictly timelike pair is eventually connected by the
constructed route. Every spacelike pair is eventually excluded because no
step travels farther than $R$. The same holds for $A$ and $B$ by inclusion.
Half-open space-time cells have weight $\delta h^3$ and vanishing diameter.
Their indicators give continuity-set counts by quadrature. For generated
intervals with converging interior tips, the path bound gives indicator
convergence off the limiting null boundaries; dominated convergence gives
the actual interval volume. Applying the argument to product cells gives
the strict-pair limit, since pair-null and diagonal sets have zero measure.
The flat-diamond integrals therefore give volume
$\pi c^3T^4/24$, ordering fraction $1/10$ and the fourth-root count-clock
ratio. A bounded continuous positive profile $\sigma(t)$ similarly gives
the weighted limit $\sigma(t)^4\,dt\,d^3x$ and the same finite FLRW mass
identities. The profile and its physical interpretation are inputs, not results of the comparison.

Over a horizon $L/c$, $S$ requires at most $q^3RD_n$ logical read incidences,
of order $q^{7/2}\log^2q$, while $B$ has order $q^5$. These are incidences,
not bit traffic, energy or native operation counts. Exact finite M1 outputs
are not preserved: a deleted adjacent-layer edge has no replacement path
within one layer, and an omitted independent input changes a lossless tuple
read. The proved replacement concerns the named limiting observables.

\paragraph{Crossing-read entropy is not fixed by those limits.}
Use the coordinate cut $x=(q-1)/2$ in index coordinates. A vector with $v_x>0$
contributes exactly
$v_x(q-|v_y|)(q-|v_z|)$ unordered crossing pairs. Consequently
\[
 X_S\le D_nRq^2,\qquad X_B\ge R^4q^2/32,
 \qquad \frac{X_S}{X_B}\le\frac{32D_n}{R^3}\longrightarrow0.
\]
For the lower bound retain $1\le v_x\le R/2$ and
$|v_y|,|v_z|\le R/2$, all strictly inside the ball. Thus the existing
one-nat-per-crossing-read dictionary changes by an unbounded factor despite
the common causal/count limit.

The $A$ family supplies a stronger counterexample. Its full axial subset has
coordinate-cut count $X_0$ and count $Y_0$ across $x+y=q-1/2$ given by
\[
 X_0=\frac{q^2R(R+1)}2,\qquad
 Y_0=q^2R(R+1)-\frac{qR(R+1)(2R+1)}3.
\]
Indeed a positive step $j$ along the $x$ or $y$ axis has $qj(q-j)$ allowed
diagonal-crossing pairs, including the finite box correction. The remaining
$S$ edges add at most $D_nRq^2$ and $2D_nRq^2$ respectively. Since
$D_n/R\to0$, the full ratio $Y_A/(2X_A)$ tends to one. The section areas
are $L^2$ and $\sqrt2(1-1/(2q))L^2$, hence
\begin{equation}
 \frac{Y_A/[\sqrt2(1-1/(2q))L^2]}{X_A/L^2}\longrightarrow\sqrt2.
 \label{eq:m1-cut-anisotropy}
\end{equation}
The complete-neighbor cap integral instead gives limit one. A common scalar
entropy conversion cancels in this ratio, so a single Planck-area calibration
cannot remove the orientation dependence. Cubic symmetry and a round limiting
causal cone therefore do not select an isotropic crossing-read entropy law.

\paragraph{A positive sparse scalar action.}
For $V=S,A,B$ omit zero and put $M_2=\sum_{v\in V}\|v\|^2$ and
$\alpha=6/(h^2M_2)$. Exact sign and permutation symmetries imply
$\sum v_iv_j=(M_2/3)\delta_{ij}$. Define
\[
 (A_V f)(x)=\alpha\sum_{v\in V}\bigl(f(x)-f(x+hv)\bigr),
\]
with zero extension at the boundary. The operator is symmetric positive
semidefinite, with the corresponding squared-difference energy including
the fixed-zero boundary terms. Taylor expansion cancels odd terms and yields
\[
 |A_V f+\Delta f|\le\frac{a^2M_4}{4},\qquad a=hR,
\]
where $M_4$ bounds unit-direction fourth derivatives and the reference is
smoothly zero near the boundary. This exact moment normalization removes the
$h/a^2\to0$ requirement of the earlier generic quadrature estimate for this
explicit action, at the unchanged square-root radius scaling.

For a smooth continuum Klein--Gordon reference supported away from the
boundary through time $T$, with $M_4$ uniform in time, the exact finite evolution
$F_{tt}+(c^2A_V+m^2I)F=0$ with sampled initial data satisfies
\[
 \|F(t)-f(t)\|_h\le
 \frac{c^2t^2a^2L^{3/2}M_4}{8},\qquad
 \|u\|_h^2=h^3\sum_x|u_x|^2.
\]
This follows from Duhamel and
$\|\sin(t\sqrt B)/\sqrt B\|\le t$ for $B\ge0$, including zero modes.
Bounded-norm smeared detectors inherit the bound. This is a free-field
limit of specified actions, not an exact finite causal-support statement
or a native quantum/matter implementation.

\paragraph{A concrete time-discretization obstruction.}
For nonnegative symmetric weights of range at most $a$, whose displacements
are nonzero modulo the periodic box, isotropic
normalization gives $\sum w_v|hv|^2=6$, so $\sum w_v\ge6/a^2$. On a
periodic box the largest Laplacian eigenvalue is at least its mean diagonal,
hence at least $6/a^2$. Centered leapfrog requires
$c^2dt^2\lambda_{\max}\le4$. At the M1 tick $dt=a/c$ the left side is at
least six, so that direct scheme is unstable, for the dense family as well.
The exact finite evolution above remains defined; alternative integrators
and substeps need their own physical operation and clock accounting. The
obstruction concerns this specified scheme, not all dynamics or M1 itself.

\paragraph{The radius exponent is not fixed by these limits.}
For any fixed integer $k\ge2$, keep the same stencils and $R=2^n$ but set
$q=R^k$, $h=L/q$ and $\delta=hR/c$. The route bound's additive time cost
is $(3n+2)LR/(cq)\to0$, and the boundary tube thickness is
$(3/n+3/R)\,\mathrm{distance}+2LR/q\to0$. The same cell and strict-margin
proofs give all the stated causal/count limits. The action error is
$O((LR/q)^2)\to0$; the cut anisotropy remains $\sqrt2$ because $R/q\to0$.
Thus the explicitly constructed radii $a=Lq^{-(k-1)/k}$, including
$L/\sqrt q$ and $Lq^{-2/3}$, work for these named limits. This does not
select a native radius or preserve finite counts between different schedules;
it proves that the named limits do not uniquely require the square-root
radius, Fibonacci population, or complete-neighbor law.

\paragraph{A sparse family preserving the crossing observable.}
The entropy separation has a constructive complement. For $t\ge1$ set
$q=2^{8t}$, $m=2^{4t}$, $K=2^{3t}$ and $R=mK$. Let $T$ contain every
$mu$ with $u\in\mathbb Z^3$, $\|u\|\le K$, together with the signed
axis steps $2^b e_i$ for $0\le b<4t$. Use the same grid, tick $hR/c$
and equal event cells. This has $\Theta(q^{9/8})$ parents, compared with
$\Theta(q^{3/2})$ for the original dense radius $R_0=\sqrt q$.

Round $d/m$ to $z$, connect zero to $z$ by rounded linear interpolation
with $\lceil\|z\|/(K-2)\rceil$ coarse steps, and correct the residual by
binary axis steps. Every step is legal and charged; the route satisfies
\[
 \frac{\|d\|}{mK}\le N\le
 \frac{\|d\|/m+2}{K-2}+1+12t
\]
and stays in a tube of radius $2m$. Its physical excess time and tube width
vanish. The same generated-interval, pair, count-clock and scalar proofs
therefore apply, with radius $a=Lq^{-1/8}$ and scalar error $O(q^{-1/4})$.

Put $C_K=\sum_{\|u\|\le K,\,u_x>0}u_x$. Exact finite enumeration gives
\[
 X_T=\sum_{\|u\|\le K,\,u_x>0}
       mu_x(q-m|u_y|)(q-m|u_z|)+q^2(m-1).
\]
Unions of unit cubes about the integer ball give
$\pi(K-1)^4/4-(2K+1)^2/8\le C_K\le\pi(K+1)^4/4$.
Since $mK^4=q^2$, it follows that $X_T/q^4\to\pi/4$, exactly the dense
leading coefficient. The receiver and displacement meshes are $h$ and $mh$;
their cap-count error is $O(K^{-1}+mK/q)$ for each fixed positive-area regular
plane section cutting the cube interior into two positive-volume parts;
supporting boundary faces are excluded. Hence, for every such section $\Sigma$,
\[
 X_T(\Sigma)=\frac{\pi q^4}{4L^2}|\Sigma|\,(1+o(1)).
\]
The added dyadic steps are lower order. Thus the original leading planar
crossing entropy and Planck-area dictionary are preserved with one nat per
unordered pair, without directional weights or scalar recalibration.
Over horizon $L/c$ the logical read count is $\Theta(q^{17/4})$ rather than
$\Theta(q^5)$. The larger radius converges more slowly: this comparison fixes
$q$, not accuracy, native cost or crossing traffic per unit model time.

The radius change has a proved role. For any symmetric translation-invariant
sub-stencil $V\subseteq B_{R_0}$ at the original radius, let $E=B_{R_0}\setminus V$.
The sum of the three coordinate-cut deficits is at least
$(q-R_0)^2\sum_{v\in E}\|v\|_1/2$. Retaining their leading counts implies
$\sum_E\|v\|_1=o(R_0^4)$. For every $\eta>0$,
\[
 \frac{|E|}{R_0^3}\le (2\eta+1/R_0)^3+
       \frac{\sum_E\|v\|_1}{\eta R_0^4},
\]
so $|E|=o(R_0^3)$ and $|V|/|B_{R_0}|\to1$. Substantial thinning at the
original radius cannot retain these counts within this stencil class.
The explicitly constructed $T$ family avoids that obstruction.

\paragraph{Completing the field comparison beyond smooth probes.}
The uniform-weight $T$ action has a concrete defect. On the periodic box,
all $m^3$ Fourier indices $(q/m)\ell$ make its coarse displacement phases
equal one. Only the $24t$ dyadic offsets act on them, giving eigenvalues at
most $48t\alpha$. A sub-box of the coarse ball gives
$M_2\ge m^2K^5/64$, so this upper bound is
$(18432/L^2)t2^{-7t}\to0$. For positive mass these modes approach the mass
frequency, making the fixed-temperature thermal energy diverge. Smooth-mode
agreement alone therefore misses a real failure.

The completed replacement action fixes it using existing edges:
\[
 \widehat A_V=\tfrac12(A_V+A_{\rm nn}),\qquad
 (A_{\rm nn}f)(x)=h^{-2}\sum_{i=1}^3
       [2f(x)-f(x+he_i)-f(x-he_i)].
\]
Every $V$ edge has positive weight and nearest neighbors receive the additional
$1/(2h^2)$. This specified one-half combination preserves exact second moments,
positivity and the spatial error bound, since its remainder is at most
$(a^2+h^2)M_4/8\le a^2M_4/4$. It changes no read, crossing entropy, radius or
degree. This is the scalar action used for the completed $T$ replacement.

For every principal periodic wavevector, elementary sine bounds give
\[
 \frac{2}{\pi^2}|p|^2\le\widehat\lambda_V(p)\le|p|^2.
\]
The nonzero alias modes have eigenvalue at least $8/(h^2m^2)\to\infty$.
Modewise convergence and this uniform whole-spectrum bound give convergence
of the full positive-mass thermal partition function, normal-ordered energy
and entropy, by summable exponential domination. No fitted vacuum-energy
subtraction is used.

The result also holds on the clipped, zero-exterior box of the read graph.
The form dominates half the nearest-neighbor energy, giving discrete-gradient
compactness in $L^2$ and limits in $H_0^1$. For a smooth compact test $f$,
positivity gives
\[
 \langle u_n,A_Vu_n\rangle_h\ge
  2\langle u_n,A_Vf_n\rangle_h-\langle f_n,A_Vf_n\rangle_h.
\]
Consistency and the supremum over such tests give the full continuum
Dirichlet energy as the lower limit. Smooth recovery supplies the upper
limit. Min-max with compactness proves convergence of each ordered eigenvalue
with multiplicity. Moreover the nearest-neighbor sine spectrum gives
\[
 \widehat\lambda_{n,j}\ge j^{2/3}/(2L^2).
\]
The resulting uniform thermal tails are summable, proving the full Dirichlet
partition, energy and entropy limits. The clipped-box result allows mass
$\mu\ge0$, since its frequency gap is at least
$\sqrt{\mu^2+c^2/(2L^2)}$; the periodic result requires $\mu>0$.

Canonical quantization also gives convergence of joint Weyl characteristic
functions for every fixed finite collection of smooth compact position and
momentum smearings, with sampled coherent means and bounded observation times.
The position covariance is bounded by the inverse gap; the smooth-test bound
on $\|\widehat A_Vf_n\|_h$ controls the unbounded momentum covariance tail.
The field commutator has coefficient
\[
 i\hbar\left\langle g_n,
 \frac{\sin((s-t)\sqrt{B_n})}{\sqrt{B_n}}f_n\right\rangle_h,
 \qquad B_n=c^2\widehat A_V+\mu^2I.
\]
It converges to the Dirichlet Klein--Gordon commutator and vanishes in the
limit before the supports can meet at speed $c$. Rounding each observation
time to a declared layer tick changes this coefficient by at most
$\hbar\delta_n\|g_n\|_h\|f_n\|_h$, which tends to zero. Thus the free
quantum response and read/count cones agree in the limit. Exact finite
support or native execution of the evolution operator is not asserted.
Thermal Gibbs entropy and the crossing statistic remain distinct observables;
preserving both does not derive their physical identification or a horizon.
