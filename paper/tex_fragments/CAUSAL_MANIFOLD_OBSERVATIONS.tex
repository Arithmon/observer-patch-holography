\subsection{Observations of the causal manifold}
\label{sec:causal-manifold-observations}

An observer inside the source-record family of
Section~\ref{sec:source-net-causal-limit} has access to the event order and
to event cardinalities. This section records what such an observer reads
from those two inputs at finite regulator, what the carriers produce
of the family itself, and which of the finite statements are theorems.

\paragraph{The finite poset.}
For a Fibonacci level $q$ the events are the pairs $(j,s)$ of a round $j\le
K_q=\lceil\sqrt q\,\rceil$ and a site $s\in S_q$, and $(j,s)\preceq(j',t)$
exactly when $j\le j'$ and the graph distance on the neighbour graph
satisfies $d(s,t)\le j'-j$. This relation is a partial order, it equals the
reflexive transitive closure of the one-round reads, every round is an
antichain, the width equals $|S_q|=q^3$, and every event at round $j$ has
height $j$
(\path{Lean/Geometry/SourceNetLayeredOrder.lean}, theorems
\texttt{layerPrec\_isPartialOrder}, \texttt{layerPrec\_iff\_reflTransGen},
\texttt{walk\_iff\_walkDist\_le}, \texttt{width\_eq\_card\_sites},
\texttt{height\_eq\_layer}; the concrete read law of the family is the
instance \texttt{precedes\_iff\_layerPrec}). The flat reference constants of
the ordering fraction are exact: the radial-time double integral of
Proposition~\ref{cor:source-record-ordering-fraction} equals $T^8/1920$, so
the fraction equals $1/10$, and the Myrheim--Meyer values in dimensions
$2$, $3$ and $4$ are $1/2$, $8/35$ and $1/10$~\cite{myrheim1978,meyer1988}
(\path{Lean/Geometry/OrderingFractionFourDimensional.lean}). The finite
clock enclosure of Proposition~\ref{prop:source-count-clock-error} is
formalized in \path{Lean/Time/SourceCountClockEnclosure.lean}.
The family limit, beyond these finite constants, is proved in
\path{Lean/Geometry/FlatDiamondNormalization.lean}: actual generated strict
pair counts have ordering fraction tending to $1/10$ for an interior
timelike limiting diamond, with the supplied clock, population and read
law kept explicit. The weighted count-volume limit and its finite error
are proved in \path{Lean/Geometry/GoldenSourceVolumeLimit.lean}.

\paragraph{What the observer reads.}
On the central vertical diamond of $K_q$ rounds the inverted Myrheim--Meyer
dimension reads $2.63$, $2.93$, $4.15$, $3.61$, $4.10$, $4.09$ and $4.07$ at
$q=5,8,13,21,34,55,89$, with ordering fractions $0.306$, $0.241$, $0.088$,
$0.138$, $0.092$, $0.093$ and $0.094$ against the flat value $1/10$; levels
with even $K_q$ approach the value from below, $0.088$, $0.092$, $0.093$,
$0.094$ at $K_q=4,6,8,10$, and levels with odd $K_q$ from above.
The outermost diamond of $K_q$ rounds extends past the sampling window at
every level, by $9.2\%$, $0.4\%$, $0.9\%$, $0.4\%$, $0.02\%$, $0.2\%$ and
$0.1\%$ of its continuum volume, so these values read clipped diamonds; the
largest interior diamonds, of $K_q-1$ rounds, read $4.04$, $5.02$, $2.83$,
$4.38$, $3.55$, $3.84$ and $3.92$.
The moving-tip diamonds of Proposition~\ref{cor:source-net-general-diamond}
read $0.250$, $0.135$, $0.121$, $0.115$, $0.108$ and $0.102$ at
$q=8,13,21,34,55,89$, dimension $2.89$ to $3.97$. The same read law on the
two- and one-dimensional golden populations reads dimensions $3.02$ and
$2.01$ at $q=89$ against the constants $8/35$ and $1/2$, so the readout
responds to the dimension of the population. The normalized count of the
vertical diamond against $\pi c^3T^4/24$ reads $0.193$ against $0.198$ at
$q=13$, $0.141$ against $0.147$ at $q=34$, $0.172$ against $0.177$ at $q=55$
and $0.163$ against $0.165$ at $q=89$. The count clock $(|I|/|J|)^{1/4}$ of
two nested vertical diamonds reads $1.705$, $2.088$, $1.907$ and $2.051$ at
$q=13,34,55,89$ against the model-time ratio $2$. Every pair count through
$q=89$ is an exact all-pairs count; the structural fields at $q=5,8,13$ agree
byte for byte with the replay of Section~\ref{sec:source-net-causal-limit}.
A sampled level at $q=144$, with $2{,}985{,}984$ sites and twelve rounds,
reads $0.0955\pm0.0002$ on the outermost diamond, so the even-round ladder
continues $0.0600$, $0.0803$, $0.0887$, $0.0932$, $0.0955$ at
$K=4,6,8,10,12$ with halving increments; its dimension reads $4.05$, the
largest interior diamond $3.95$, the moving tips $0.102$, the count clock
$1.959$ against $2$, and the two- and one-dimensional controls $3.02$ and
$2.00$. These are stratified estimates with standard errors, not exact
counts. The bounds of
Proposition~\ref{prop:source-net-alexandrov-volume} exceed the diamond
volume at these levels, and the finite values above are readings of the
supplied law, not a demonstration of its limit.

\paragraph{What the carriers produce.}
The family is produced by the carriers themselves. One exact twelve-port
carrier per site holds the record $z(b)$ as its port loads on the six
antipodal axes and reads its position through the rank-three response
$x=2P_{\mathrm{slow}}N$ of the rank-three source metric. The readback metric
equals the source-axis contraction $s(b)$ with scale exactly one, an identity
in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ checked on every pair through $q=21$ and on $10^6$
seeded pairs at $q=34,55,89$. Each carrier decides its neighbours from its
own readback; the resulting relation equals the family's neighbour graph
edge for edge at $q=5,8,13,21,34,55,89$. The carriers then read one another
for $K_q$ rounds, every read an authenticated read-after-write event, and the
precedence generated from those events with no declared parent lists equals
the layered order on the centre diamond at every level, with strict pair
counts $968$, $4{,}244$, $102{,}990$, $2{,}904{,}889$, $47{,}881{,}819$,
$2{,}083{,}062{,}313$ and $55{,}108{,}871{,}450$. A unit intervention at the
centre in round zero changes exactly the future cone in every round. The
operation costs are recorded: reads per round $2E+n$ for $E$ edges and $n$
carriers, at $q=34$ a total of $157{,}922{,}880$ reads and $275{,}128$
writes, at $q=89$ a total of $21{,}845{,}550{,}850$ reads and $7{,}754{,}659$
writes by $704{,}969$ carriers over ten rounds.

\paragraph{Inference and boundary.}
Order and count determine conformal geometry and scale under the continuum
causality hypotheses \cite{hawkingkingmccarthy1976,malament1977}; that is
the inference the observer makes from the readings above, and the finite
readings are consistent with flat $1+3$ spacetime at every level examined.
The population, the read law, the round duration and one counted event per
carrier and round are supplied. Their selection by accepted native repairs,
a physical clock, and the continuum limit are separate conditions rather than
results of this section. Two further receipts read the order with more than
one number. At $q=8$ to $34$ the Myrheim--Meyer dimension is the same at the
centre and at every admissible off-centre tip of the same size to within
$0.04$; the odd multipoles of the link directions vanish by the two-way reads
and the $l=4$ power falls from $0.036$ to $0.0016$ over the four levels; the
abundance of intervals of $64$ to $1024$ events matches a flat sprinkling
within $14$ percent while links and small intervals do not, as a layered order
requires. At $q=55$ and $89$ the chain counts $C_2$, $C_3$, $C_4$ of the
centre diamond, sampled with standard errors, invert to $4.09$, $4.03$ and
$4.02$ at $q=89$ (the two- and one-dimensional controls to $3.01$, $3.00$,
$3.01$ and $1.99$, $2.02$, $2.04$); the distribution of $|I(y,z)|/N$ over
related pairs lies closest to the flat $3+1$ law of $(\tau/T)^4$ among the
flat laws in two to six dimensions, with a maximum CDF difference of $0.025$;
and over fourteen six-layer diamonds displaced by $L/8$ the $C_3$ dimension
reads $4.078$ with standard deviation $0.016$ and the count-volume
coefficient $1.0627$ with standard deviation $0.0013$. Curvature estimators
are not evaluated. The receipts, the carrier event logs and a
standard-library generator that rebuilds the poset from the definitions are
published as the causal-poset evidence package \cite{ophfpe}.
