\subsection{Observations of the causal manifold}
\label{sec:causal-manifold-observations}

An observer inside the source-record family of
Section~\ref{sec:source-net-causal-limit} has access to the event order and
to event cardinalities. This section records what such an observer reads
from those two inputs at finite regulator, what the carrier stack produces
of the family itself, and which of the finite statements are theorems.

\paragraph{The finite poset.}
For a Fibonacci level $q$ the events are the pairs $(j,s)$ of a round $j\le
K_q=\lceil\sqrt q\,\rceil$ and a site $s\in S_q$, and $(j,s)\preceq(j',t)$
exactly when $j\le j'$ and the graph distance on the neighbour graph
satisfies $d(s,t)\le j'-j$. This relation is a partial order, it equals the
reflexive transitive closure of the one-round reads, every round is an
antichain, the width equals $|S_q|=q^3$, and every event at round $j$ has
height $j$
(\path{Lean/Geometry/SourceNetLayeredOrder.lean}, theorems
\texttt{layerPrec\_isPartialOrder}, \texttt{layerPrec\_iff\_reflTransGen},
\texttt{walk\_iff\_walkDist\_le}, \texttt{width\_eq\_card\_sites},
\texttt{height\_eq\_layer}; the concrete read law of the family is the
instance \texttt{precedes\_iff\_layerPrec}). The flat reference constants of
the ordering fraction are exact: the oriented-pair integral of
Proposition~\ref{cor:source-record-ordering-fraction} equals $T^8/1920$, so
the fraction equals $1/10$, and the Myrheim--Meyer values in dimensions
$2$, $3$ and $4$ are $1/2$, $8/35$ and $1/10$
(\path{Lean/Geometry/OrderingFractionFourDimensional.lean}). The finite
clock enclosure of Proposition~\ref{prop:source-count-clock-error} is
formalized in \path{Lean/Time/SourceCountClockEnclosure.lean}.

\paragraph{What the observer reads.}
On the interior vertical diamond of $K_q$ rounds the inverted Myrheim--Meyer
dimension reads $2.63$, $2.93$, $4.15$, $3.61$, $4.10$ and $4.09$ at
$q=5,8,13,21,34,55$, with ordering fractions $0.306$, $0.241$, $0.088$,
$0.138$, $0.092$ and $0.093$ against the flat value $1/10$; levels with even
$K_q$ approach the value from below and levels with odd $K_q$ from above.
The moving-tip diamonds of Proposition~\ref{cor:source-net-general-diamond}
read $0.250$, $0.135$, $0.121$, $0.115$ and $0.106$ at $q=8,13,21,34,55$,
dimension $2.89$ to $3.93$. The same read law on the two- and
one-dimensional golden populations reads dimensions $3.02$ and $2.01$ at
$q=55$ against the constants $8/35$ and $1/2$, so the readout responds to the
dimension of the population. The normalized count of the vertical diamond
against $\pi c^3T^4/24$ reads $0.193$ against $0.198$ at $q=13$, $0.141$
against $0.147$ at $q=34$ and $0.172$ against $0.177$ at $q=55$. The count
clock $(|I|/|J|)^{1/4}$ of two nested vertical diamonds reads $1.705$,
$2.088$ and $1.907$ at $q=13,34,55$ against the model-time ratio $2$. Pair
counts are exact through $q=34$ and stratified at $q=55$; the structural
fields at $q=5,8,13$ agree byte for byte with the replay of
Section~\ref{sec:source-net-causal-limit}. The bounds of
Proposition~\ref{prop:source-net-alexandrov-volume} exceed the diamond
volume at these levels, and the finite values above are readings of the
supplied law, not a demonstration of its limit.

\paragraph{What the carrier stack produces.}
The family is produced by the carriers themselves. One exact twelve-port
carrier per site holds the record $z(b)$ as its port loads on the six
antipodal axes and reads its position through the rank-three response
$x=2P_{\mathrm{slow}}N$ of the rank-three source metric. The readback metric
equals the source-axis contraction $s(b)$ with scale exactly one, an identity
in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ checked on every pair through $q=21$ and on $10^6$
seeded pairs at $q=34$. Each carrier decides its neighbours from its own
readback; the resulting relation equals the family's neighbour graph edge
for edge at $q=5,8,13,21,34$. The carriers then read one another for $K_q$
rounds, every read an authenticated read-after-write event, and the
precedence generated from those events with no declared parent lists equals
the layered order on the centre diamond at every level, with strict pair
counts $968$, $4{,}244$, $102{,}990$, $2{,}904{,}889$ and $47{,}881{,}819$. A
unit intervention at the centre in round zero changes exactly the future
cone in every round. The operation costs are recorded: reads per round
$2E+n$ for $E$ edges and $n$ carriers, at $q=34$ a total of $157{,}922{,}880$
reads and $275{,}128$ writes.

\paragraph{Inference and boundary.}
Order and count determine conformal geometry and scale under the continuum
causality hypotheses \cite{hawkingkingmccarthy1976,malament1977}; that is
the inference the observer makes from the readings above, and the finite
readings are consistent with flat $1+3$ spacetime at every level examined.
The population, the read law, the round duration and one counted event per
carrier and round are supplied. Their selection by accepted native repairs,
a physical clock, and the continuum limit are work in progress. Isotropy of
link directions, interval abundance profiles and curvature estimators on the
family are work in progress. The receipts, the carrier event logs and a
standard-library generator that rebuilds the poset from the definitions are
published as the causal-poset evidence package \cite{ophfpe}.
