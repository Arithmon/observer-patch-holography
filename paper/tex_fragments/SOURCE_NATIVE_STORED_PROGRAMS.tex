\subsection{Conditional stored computation by supported pair means}
\label{sec:native-stored-programs}

Pair means can store an intermediate answer, use it in subsequent layers and
reuse its record after the last admitted read. The construction assumes a
prepared unit and integer payloads, a supplied request list for each output,
two protected record banks and verified paired routes on a finite twelve-port
support. A temporal controller supplies the word and public scale metadata.
The reference loads encoding an integer $v$ are
$(b+gv/2^e,b-gv/2^e)$, with public exponent $e$ and gain $g>0$.
Attenuation changes $e$ while preserving the decoded integer. The logical
recurrence is
\[
 v_{t+1,i}=1+\sum_{j\in\mathcal M_{t,i}}v_{t,j},
\]
where the finite list $\mathcal M_{t,i}$ permits repetitions and empty sums.
A public cap follows the same recurrence with absolute-value bounds;
it uses no realized payload.

\paragraph{Transfers, retirement and composition.}
Each record occupies two ports on its bank carrier. A separate core contains
a unit, an accumulator, workspace and an operand. The placement requires
$2N+1\leq C$ for $N$ sites and $C$ carriers. A paired route has disjoint
scalar paths and avoids other live records; either admitted rail polarity is
checked against the captured seams. In the reference evolution, copying a
source into a blank export pair halves its amplitude and increments its
scale. A route with $n+2$ paired cells, $n\geq1$, delivers the pre-transfer
source amplitude divided by $2^{n+2}$. Actual deviations from the blank
reference loads enter the propagated error budget.
Repeated root clearing and forward sweeps clean the bus without touching
the destination. After $(n+1)^2k$ sweeps, its maximum residual is bounded by
\[
 A\frac{(n+1)^2}{2n+1}\,2^{-k},
\]
where $A$ bounds the reference amplitudes. Projection of this residual to
zero is used only for error comparison; no physical reset to a prescribed
value occurs. The read and store words contain, respectively,
\[
 4+2n+(1+2n)(n+1)^2k,
 \qquad 5+2n+(1+2n)(n+1)^2k
\]
scalar means.

Restart, aligned addition, operand retirement, read and store compose into a
finite word of supported scalar means. All records in the input bank remain
readable while the other bank receives the next layer. Banks switch roles
only after that layer finishes. Induction over the outputs and layers gives
the stated recurrence, including repeated requests and reuse of retired
records. The primitive composition and noisy-word publication theorem are
kernel checked. Identification of the Python compiler with this construction
uses an analytic induction and independent finite route and schedule checks;
a generated Lean proof term for the complete captured compiler is not claimed.

\paragraph{A single error budget.}
For initial integer bound $P$, put $A=\max(1,P)$ and choose normalized
baseline $b=A+1$. Normalized loads are represented in grid units by
multiplication by $Q$; in particular $p,m$ below are grid-unit samples.
Let $J$ count restart and transfer stages, let $D$ bound their bus size, and
let $E$ bound every observed exponent. For the complete scalar word length
$W$, sufficient choices are
\[
 \begin{split}
 k&=E+\lceil\log_2(64AJD^2)\rceil,\\
 R&=AJD^2\,2^{-k},\\
 B&=E+\lceil\log_2(64(W+1))\rceil,\\
 Q&=2^B,\qquad g=(Q-3)/Q.
 \end{split}
\]
Here $R$ bounds all reference cleanup discrepancies in normalized-load units.
The disturbance allowances below use those same units. With preparation and
sample allowances $1/(4Q)$ each and maximum-norm error at most $5/(8Q)$
per scalar mean, including disturbances on idle ports, the local samples
$p,m$ in grid units give the normalized observation and radius
\[
 z=\frac{(p-m)2^e}{2(Q-3)},\qquad
 r=\frac{\bigl((4+5W_{\mathrm{prefix}})/8+QR\bigr)2^e}{Q-3}.
\]
The error clock includes all preceding means and never restarts at a record
boundary. The true integer lies in the intersection of $[z-r,z+r]$ with
the public integer cap. The sufficient budget proves $r<1/2$, so that
intersection contains exactly one integer. The decoder receives the samples,
scale, cap and error allowances, rather than the expected answer.

\paragraph{Finite evidence and its scope.}
Nine signed three-layer histories exercise cancellation, repeated and empty
requests, stored intermediate values and bank reuse. Two complete $27$-site,
two-layer histories use the supplied golden metric menu, with a baseline
and a centre-site intervention. Together they charge $1{,}409{,}653{,}226$
means: $292{,}557{,}295$ are explicitly evaluated and $1{,}117{,}095{,}931$
have their exact rounded effect certified by deterministic stationary-block
checks. Acceleration requires an executed whole block to leave its complete
touched state unchanged; every omitted repetition remains charged in the
word length and error clock. This is not a retained event-by-event expansion
of those repetitions.

Independent checks validate all $45{,}940$ two-bank routes at populations
$27$, $2{,}197$ and $9{,}261$, together with every metric-menu decision.
For the two larger populations, sufficient bounds are
$27{,}466{,}293{,}578{,}568{,}192$ and
$11{,}563{,}547{,}398{,}990{,}878{,}570$ means, with $11{,}309$ and
$52{,}720$ grid bits. Their native words are not replayed. These bounds
establish finite compilability on checked routes under the supplied inputs;
they make no physical-efficiency claim. Route search has no all-level
completeness theorem.

The bank placement is separate from the geometric address layout. Runtime
payload computation does not prepare, preserve or read those geometric
addresses to select its menu. Agreement of the decoded recurrence does not
identify the raw write history with the separate protected-address routing
history. The controller, protected lifetimes,
observer cover, isolation, attainable precision and physical publication
instrument remain supplied. Scalar operation counts define neither a
spacetime volume nor a physical clock. Host control, metadata storage and
observation costs are additional. Reproduction is specified in
