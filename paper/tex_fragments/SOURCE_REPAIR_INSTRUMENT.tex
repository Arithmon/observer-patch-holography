\subsection{Repair instruments, recurrent influence, and erased records}
\label{sec:source-repair-instrument}

The current repair rule has explicit channel obstructions.
A quantum channel must preserve convex mixtures. For the exact
binary64 threshold $\epsilon=\texttt{1e-15}$ and $\delta=2^{-51}$, consider
the positive diagonal states
\[
 p_j=(1/2+j\delta,1/2-j\delta),\qquad j=0,1,2.
\]
The native rule leaves $p_0,p_1$ unchanged and averages $p_2$ to $p_0$,
since $2\delta\leq\epsilon<4\delta$. Its first output coordinate violates
affinity at $p_1=(p_0+p_2)/2$ by exactly $\delta$. Thus this thresholded
rule is not the exact diagonal restriction of a quantum channel on these
inputs. A numerical approximation to an ideal channel is a different claim.

\paragraph{The terminal lift also fails ensemble equivalence.}
Use the explicit density encoding
$\rho(\psi)=N^{-1}\bigoplus_c|\psi_c\rangle\langle\psi_c|$.
Keep other carriers in the uniform amplitude state and compare at carrier
zero the equally weighted ensembles $\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle\}$ and
$\{(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2,(|0\rangle-|1\rangle)/\sqrt2\}$.
Their average input densities agree. After full repair, the first ensemble
has local intensities $(13/24,1/24)$ or $(1/24,13/24)$ at these ports.
The native phase-preserving lift, including its zero-phase convention at
zero amplitude, gives coherence $\sqrt{13}/(24N)$ for both. The second
ensemble has intensities $(7/24,7/24)$ and opposite coherences
$\pm7/(24N)$, averaging to zero. The output ensemble gap exceeds
$1/(8N)$. The lift therefore has no affine extension on this density
encoding. Retaining an additional classical preparation label would change
the input state; it would not establish a channel on the stated densities.
Individual carrier normalization is also not preserved by a generic seam:
its sum becomes $1+(x_b-x_a)/2$, while the global sum is conserved.

\paragraph{Two valid extensions with different later reads.}
On the declared global mode space $\mathbb C^{12N}$, let $S_e$ exchange
the endpoints of seam $e$. The map
\[
 \Phi_e(\rho)=\tfrac12(\rho+S_e\rho S_e^*)
\]
is CPTP, with Kraus operators $I/\sqrt2,S_e/\sqrt2$, and its diagonal is
the ideal unthresholded pair mean. Disjoint seam twirls commute and are
idempotent. Let $E$ be their product and let
$D(\rho)=\sum_iP_i\rho P_i$ be coordinate dephasing. Both $E$ and $DE$
are CPTP and have identical immediate diagonal reads for every input.
Every convex mixture of them has the same property. These are covariant
under simultaneous relabeling of modes and seams; none is source-selected.

For a phase state $(|0\rangle+i|b\rangle)/\sqrt2$ at one carrier, with
$b$ adjacent to zero, and maximally mixed other blocks of weight $1/N$,
$E$ retains one quarter of the original local coherence while $DE$
erases it. The next native unitary read distinguishes them by at least
$(2t-400t^2)/(8N)$. At $t=2^{-17},N=4$ this is
$16359/34359738368>0$. Native execution and an independent rational
reconstruction of the full sparse matrices check the witness. Scalar
repair data therefore do not determine the recurrent instrument.

The coordinate probes also cannot simply be renamed A1's central records.
An accessible algebra in $\mathbb C^{12}$ containing all $P_i$ and the
native time translate $Q=U^*P_0U$ contains
\[
 P_iQP_j=\overline{U_{0i}}U_{0j}|i\rangle\langle j|.
\]
Every coefficient is nonzero by the preceding propagator bound, so the
algebra is $M_{12}(\mathbb C)$, whose center is scalar. A separate classical
record register can have central outcome labels, but those stored labels
are distinct from these coherent instantaneous probes.

\paragraph{A recurrence cone and its exact information loss.}
The explicit measured cycle $DE\operatorname{Ad}_U$, restricted to
diagonal inputs, has population matrix $T=RB_{\rm local}$, where $R$
averages matching partners and $B_{pq}=|U_{pq}|^2>0$. If $G$ is the
actual carrier graph and $m(c,p)$ the partner carrier at slot $(c,p)$,
then for $k\geq1$
\[
 (T^k)_{(c,p),(d,q)}>0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \min\{d_G(c,d),d_G(m(c,p),d)\}\leq k-1.
\]
The first step reads the two permitted input carriers at every port;
induction takes unions over neighboring carriers, with no cancellation
between nonnegative terms. Thus a carrier's outputs have exactly a
radius-$k$ graph \emph{influence} ball. Independently checked matrix powers
give carrier diameters $1,2,2$ at $N=4,8,16$. This is neither a Euclidean
radius nor a proof of exact record decoding.

Indeed, on the normalized preparation tangent $V$ of dimension $11N$,
the kernel of $R$ consists of opposite endpoint values whose oriented
seam weights have zero carrier divergence. The connected incidence matrix
has rank $N-1$, giving
\[
 \dim(\ker R\cap V)=5N+1,\qquad \operatorname{rank}(R|_V)=6N-1.
\]
At the stated step, put $\eta=20t$. The bounds
$\|U-I\|\leq\eta$ and $\|B-I\|_\infty\leq4\eta=5/8192<1/2$ make
$B_{\rm local}$ invertible on $V$, with inverse norm below two.
Consequently the same dimensions hold for $T$. A circulation $z$ around
the source's alternating port-zero/port-one carrier cycle satisfies $Rz=0$
and has zero sum on each carrier. The distinct inputs
$x_\pm=1/12\pm B_{\rm local}^{-1}z/48$ are normalized, componentwise
at least $1/24$, and obey $Tx_+=Tx_-$. No later global population trajectory
can recover that difference. Exact erasure is analytic; the executed
inverse and complete output vectors are checked to residual $2^{-40}$.
An archive of initial records or suitable retained instrument outcomes is
additional information and is not excluded by this obstruction.

Carrier totals do not close the measured dynamics either. A Markov map
on those totals exists exactly when total transition into every output
carrier is independent of the input port within each input carrier.
Choose $d=m(0,0)$ and a port $q$ with $m(0,q)\ne d$. Their probabilities
of reaching $d$ differ by at least
$(1-2\eta-10\eta^2)/2=536706947/1073741824>0$. This rejects that specific
coarsening, not every possible refinement map.

\paragraph{Constructive recovery and its record cost.}
For deterministic ideal means $y=Rx$, orient each seam $(a,b)$ and retain
$d_e=(x_a-x_b)/2$. Normalization gives $\partial d=\mathbf1-Cy$, where
$C$ sums a carrier's slots. It suffices to retain differences on the
$5N+1$ non-tree seams of any spanning tree: subtract their divergence,
then recover tree differences by deleting leaves. This gives
$x_a=y_a+d_e$, $x_b=y_b-d_e$ for every normalized input.
Any linear archive allowing recovery must be injective on
$\ker R\cap V$, so $5N+1$ real coordinates are also necessary.
This is a minimal linear archive, not a finite-bit capacity bound.
Exact rational execution and independent Gaussian elimination check it
at all three populations. The same archive after $B_{\rm local}$,
followed by its inverse, also reverses the measured cycle on its
normalized inputs. It requires global access to the means and extra
records, and supplies no local decoder or canonical spanning tree.

There is a different quantum completion. For all binary seam words $s$,
let $S_s$ be the indicated product of swaps and $p_s=2^{-6N}$. The
flagged channel
\[
 J(\rho)=\bigoplus_s p_s S_s\rho S_s^*
\]
has Kraus maps $\sqrt{p_s}|s\rangle\otimes S_s$ and is CPTP. Forgetting
the flag gives $E$; applying its conditional inverse before forgetting
gives $\sum_s S_s^*[p_sS_s\rho S_s^*]S_s=\rho$ for every input.
Known interleaved unitary steps can likewise be undone in reverse order.
This instrument retains $6N$ bits per sweep and its quantum state.
Its individual branch populations are swapped or unchanged: only their
expectation equals the deterministic mean. The flag marginal is uniform
and independent of the preparation, so the flags alone are not its public
read. These two constructions establish that extra records can prevent
erasure, while distinguishing their costs and operational meanings.

Lean kernel-checks permutation conjugation, twirling and dephasing as
channels, the diagonal and matrix-unit identities, labelled-branch
recovery, affinity obstruction and scalar gap reductions. The ensemble,
generated-algebra, all-time cone, incidence-rank, minimal-archive,
flagged-channel and inverse arguments are analytic, with independently
replayed finite controls. These results
identify concrete changes or additional state needed by a recurrent source.
They do not promote a constructed extension to canonical A1--A3 membership,
selected M1 records, population or radius.
