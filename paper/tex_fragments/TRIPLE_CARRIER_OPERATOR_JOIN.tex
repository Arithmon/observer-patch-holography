\subsection{A noncommutative join of the retained observer carriers}
\label{sec:triple-carrier-operator-join}

The retained observers 86, 88 and 247 have realized label spaces of
dimensions 13, 14 and 13. Their 32 aligned source rows define one triple
path \(q_t=(a_t,b_t,c_t)\), \(0\leq t\leq31\), with the two pair paths
as its coordinate projections. Use the declared tensor reading
\(H=(\mathbb C^{13}\otimes\mathbb C^{14})\otimes\mathbb C^{13}\),
and write \(M_n=\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C^n)\). The operator join
uses this 2366-dimensional carrier; it assigns no multiplication to the
338-dimensional linear pushout of diagonal records.

\begin{theorem}[Full operator join on the common carrier]
The maps \(\iota_{88}:M_{182}\to M_{2366}\) and
\(\iota_{247}:M_{169}\to M_{2366}\), defined by
\begin{align}
 \iota_{88}(X)&=X\otimes I_{13},\label{eq:triple-operator-88}\\
 \iota_{247}(Y)_{(a,b,c),(a',b',c')}
   &=Y_{(a,c),(a',c')}\delta_{bb'},\label{eq:triple-operator-247}
\end{align}
are injective unital star homomorphisms. Their images intersect in exactly
\(M_{13}\otimes I_{14}\otimes I_{13}\) and generate all of \(M_{2366}\).
The exclusive observer-88 and observer-247 factors commute. The two
overlapping pair algebras do not commute.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Identity-spectator inclusion and coordinate reindexing preserve products,
adjoints and the identity. Evaluating a fixed spectator diagonal entry proves
injectivity. If \(\iota_{88}(X)=\iota_{247}(Y)\), evaluation at
\(c=c'=0\) gives
\[
 X_{(a,b),(a',b')}=Z_{a,a'}\delta_{bb'},
 \qquad Z_{a,a'}=Y_{(a,0),(a',0)}.
\]
Thus \(X=Z\otimes I_{14}\), and injectivity forces
\(Y=Z\otimes I_{13}\). Conversely these two matrices have the same
triple image. This proves the full \(M_{13}\) intersection, whose complex
vector dimension is 169.

For any triple indices \(q=(a,b,c)\), \(r=(a',b',c')\), and any hinge
label \(s\), the pair matrix units obey
\[
 \iota_{88}(E_{(a,b),(s,b')})\,
 \iota_{247}(E_{(s,c),(a',c')})=E_{q,r}.
\]
Every triple matrix unit therefore belongs to the generated algebra.
Operators on the exclusive factors commute by the tensor-product rule.
The common hinge contains \(E_{01}\) and \(E_{10}\), whose two products
are the distinct \(E_{00}\) and \(E_{11}\), so the pair algebras cannot
commute elementwise.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Counted states and observable retractions.}
The empirical state and its pair marginals are
\[
 \rho_{ABC}=\frac1{32}\sum_{t=0}^{31}|q_t\rangle\langle q_t|,
 \qquad \rho_{AB}=\operatorname{Tr}_C\rho_{ABC},\qquad
 \rho_{AC}=\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_{ABC}.
\]
For arbitrary pair observables, these give
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{ABC}\iota_{88}(X))
   &=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{AB}X),&
 \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{ABC}\iota_{247}(Y))
   &=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_{AC}Y).
\end{align*}
Observable retractions instead use normalized partial traces:
\[
 \frac1{13}\operatorname{Tr}_C\iota_{88}(X)=X,
 \qquad \frac1{14}\operatorname{Tr}_B\iota_{247}(Y)=Y.
\]
These reverse maps are linear, not multiplicative. The density-state
marginals carry no spectator normalization. The empirical states are
distinct from the independently supplied uniform tower states; no
state-preserving tower/refinement morphism follows from these equalities.

\paragraph{Source generators and finite transport.}
Each observer's counted transition operator and diagonal field projectors
generate the full matrix algebra on its realized alphabet. Their pair lifts,
followed by \(\iota_{88}\) and \(\iota_{247}\), therefore generate the
full triple algebra. This is a family of operators constructed from source
data, not a history of source-executed quantum gates. A singleton path
projector does not replace this generating family.

For each \(0\leq t<31\), transpose the two adjacent path labels separately
in each factor, and let \(U_t\) be the tensor product of the resulting
permutation matrices. The triple conjugation \(\alpha_t\) and its pair
conjugations satisfy
\[
 \alpha_t\iota_{88}=\iota_{88}\alpha^{88}_t,
 \qquad \alpha_t\iota_{247}=\iota_{247}\alpha^{247}_t.
\]
These identities hold for arbitrary matrices, including off-diagonal
observables. The evolved source generators generate the full joint algebra
because \(\alpha_t\) is an automorphism. Set
\(P_0=I\), \(P_t=U_{t-1}\cdots U_0\), and
\(U_{s,t}=P_tP_s^{-1}\). Then
\(U_{r,t}U_{s,r}=U_{s,t}\) for \(s\leq r\leq t\), with exact pair
projections and path transport. The final-to-initial edge is absent from
all three transition-count tables and is excluded. The empirical state is
not invariant under these row-dependent permutations.
