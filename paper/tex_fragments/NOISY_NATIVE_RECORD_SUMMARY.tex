\paragraph{Maintained records in a noisy coherent source.}
A declared coherent-code source realizes bounded self-reading patches with
local state, ports, readback, noisy feedback and actual public record banks.
Complete transfer/reset services reduce carrier leakage, and a protected
quantum computation exports into a maintained classical archive. With a
fixed positive local service error below a derived library bound,
$O(\log\log q)$ quantum protection levels, staged decoding and expander
refresh of $O(\log q)$-bit words make the joint decoded quantum and live
record error tend to zero. The archive remains readable throughout each
refresh; no perfect bare decision bit or fault-free runtime controller is
assumed. The analytical argument imports computation-code fault tolerance
and an explicit expander bound; finite tests check its interfaces and fault
controls, without executing the enormous all-size apparatus.

control/inventory resources are hypotheses. They are not consequences of
