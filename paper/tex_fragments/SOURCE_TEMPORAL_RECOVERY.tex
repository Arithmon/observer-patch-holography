\subsection{Temporal acceptance, native continuation and preservation guards}
\label{sec:source-temporal-recovery}

The scalar seam interface permits a complete information-recovery theorem
without a supplied successful route. Its hypotheses are explicit: a finite
connected graph of scalar ports, the pair mean on each supported edge, exact
local samples at one receiver, retained classical observations and readout
arithmetic. This is an exact mathematical interface. It does not instantiate
the full A1 response and endogenous-transport architecture or its physical
instruments. The result complements the protected-bank compiler: tomography
changes the physical state and does not itself implement immutable archives.

\paragraph{Acceptance from native observations.}
Let $V$ be a real vector space of source preparations, $P$ a linear map to
the port scalars, $A_e$ the supported pair-mean map, and $r_i$ a local
coordinate readout. A sample after $k$ events has response
\[
 L_{k,i}=r_i A_{e_k}\cdots A_{e_1}P.
\]
These forms are generated independently of the requested linear meaning
$f:V\to\mathbb R$. Write $L$ for all retained sample rows. Equal observations
imply equal meanings exactly when
\[
 \ker L\subseteq\ker f
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f\in\operatorname{rowspan}(L)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f=cL\text{ for some }c.
\]
Indeed, agreement on observation fibers gives the kernel containment by
comparing $z$ to zero. Finite linear duality gives the row-span
factorization; the converse follows by evaluating $cL$. If factorization
fails, a vector $z$ has $Lz=0$ and $f(z)\ne0$. The source possibilities
$x$ and $x+z$ are indistinguishable, so every sound partial publication
rule abstains on their observation. This proves completeness for all linear
meanings on this domain, rather than for an enumerated output menu. Retaining
samples makes observability monotone and defines the earliest sound prefix.
For sample errors $|\eta_i|\leq\epsilon_i$, a decoder certificate gives
$|c\eta|\leq\sum_i|c_i|\epsilon_i$; attainable precision is a separate input.

\paragraph{Connected support constructs complete native recovery.}
Calibrate the receiver's own scalar first. Suppose a connected set $K$ has
its evolving values determined by the retained receiver samples. A proper
$K$ has an edge to its complement, since otherwise every graph walk from
the receiver remains in $K$, contradicting connectivity. Choose a fresh
vertex $u$ and a simple path through $K$ to the receiver. If the relay
pre-values are $b_1,\ldots,b_d$, a forward sweep has the nested endpoint
\[
 y_0=x_u,\qquad y_j=(y_{j-1}+b_j)/2.
\]
The receiver observes $y_d$ and reverses $y_{j-1}=2y_j-b_j$ to recover $x_u$.
It can then compute every changed relay value. Thus $K$ grows by one vertex;
no unintroduced vertex was touched earlier. Induction constructs a complete
supported plan, recovers every initial scalar and identifies the evolved
state on the calibrated region. No desired payload enters the path choice.
For $N$ ports there are exactly $N-1$ phases and $N$ receiver samples.
A simple phase has at most $|K|$ means, hence
\[
 B\leq\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}j=\frac{N(N-1)}2.
\]
The native endpoint identity, induction, lowering to pair means, sample count
and work bound are kernel checked in \texttt{SourceTemporalTomography} and
\texttt{SourceTemporalConnected}.

\paragraph{The exact continuation constraint.}
At a checkpoint let $Lx$ be the retained observations and $Mx$ the remaining
complete scalar state. A linear meaning can be recovered by a native
continuation exactly when
\[
 \ker\begin{bmatrix}L\\M\end{bmatrix}\subseteq\ker f.
\]
Necessity follows because equal records and equal states remain
indistinguishable under every common continuation. For sufficiency, the
connected protocol recovers $Mx$ from receiver-local samples, after which
the factorization evaluates $f$ from $(Lx,Mx)$. The corresponding
fiber-factorization statement holds for arbitrary meanings and preparation
domains, with equality of meanings in place of linear kernel notation.
In particular, an unobserved record may remain recoverable; an erased
record cannot be restored by waiting, even for an infinite common word.

\paragraph{A maximal guard and an unconditional proposal bound.}
For a specified source-record domain $X$, a checkpoint consists of functions
$s:X\to\mathbb R^{\mathrm{ports}}$ and $o:X\to\mathrm{List}(\mathbb R)$.
Call it protected when $(o,s)$ is injective. Admit a proposed mean exactly
when the proposed checkpoint remains protected, retaining the resulting
receiver sample. A rejected proposal leaves the scalars unchanged. On a
linear domain this is exactly a joint-kernel test on source response
matrices, independent of the realized payload. The additional receiver
sample is determined by the proposed scalar state.

The topology-derived completing word works from every protected checkpoint
whose record determines the receiver's initial value. Every prefix of that
word is admitted: an erasing prefix would give two equal records and states
which its common remaining suffix cannot separate, contradicting complete
recovery. Consequently an occurrence of the completing word among arbitrary
other proposals guarantees full receiver information. The kernel proof
identifies the actual guarded and unguarded native runs, rather than assuming
that a selected route succeeds.

For a separately specified uniform full proposal alphabet of $m$ event
identities, encode the completing word as a block of length $B$. At most
$(m^B-1)^k$ of the $m^{Bk}$ proposal words avoid that block at all $k$
disjoint positions. Thus the incomplete fraction obeys
\[
 \Pr(T>kB)\leq (1-m^{-B})^k.
\]
The finite count and its composition with guarded native execution are
kernel checked in \texttt{SourceTemporalAttempts}. Under the consistent
product proposal law, the geometric-tail argument additionally gives the
analytic bound $\mathbb E T\leq Bm^B$ for $B>0$. The empty word represents
immediate completion. Rejected proposals remain in the denominator and in
proposal work. This is not uniform sampling of successful or admitted
words. Computing response coefficients, the guard, proposal metadata and
decoder arithmetic are explicit interface resources outside the scalar-mean
count. Canonical A3 alone does not imply this uniform/IID proposal law.

\paragraph{Meaning requirements and A3.}
For an arbitrary function of finitely many independently variable records,
call coordinate $j$ essential when changing it alone can change the public
meaning. A direct-read set $S$ determines the meaning exactly when it
contains every essential coordinate. Necessity is the isolated-coordinate
intervention. For sufficiency, replace unobserved coordinates one at a time;
each replacement is inessential and preserves the meaning. Thus this set is
the unique minimal direct-read requirement, including nonlinear meanings.
For $b+\sum_j a_jx_j$ it is precisely the support of the coefficients.
Correlated source domains require a different fiber analysis.

In an attained convex finite full-history KL problem with positive weights
and a faithful reference, the support theorem for the minimizer retains
every history admitted by any feasible law. Therefore a linear meaning is
certainly completable under the selected law exactly when every such history
satisfies the joint-kernel condition. This is the composed theorem in
\texttt{SourceTemporalSelection}. A state-determining coarse cover alone
does not imply full-history support. The actual A1 grammar, A2-visible
constraints, A3 observer cover, reference and weights remain inputs to this
specialization; the result does not compare unrelated policy spaces.

\paragraph{Finite evidence and the M1 boundary.}
The captured 15,360-port, 46,050-edge support has a completely checked
topology plan with 15,359 phases, 425,979 means, 851,958 primitive reads and
as many primitive writes, plus 15,360 preparation writes and receiver
samples. The largest phase has 51 edges. This is a topology certificate,
not a large-plan payload replay or an attainable-precision claim. Eight
five-port histories, including all five basis states, independently replay
native execution and reconstruct original and evolved values from receiver
samples alone. Separate captured controls distinguish aggregate recovery,
individual recovery and wrong temporal order. Merging the two unprotected
source ports first erases their difference on the entire support, so no
common future repairs it. Under the stated uniform first-seam law this
cylinder has probability $1/46050$.

The guarded three-edge chain exhausts all 9,841 proposal words through
horizon eight. At that horizon 857 of 6,561 attempts complete both source
records; none loses their distinction irreversibly. The census retains
51,750 stopped proposals, 45,445 executed means, 6,305 rejected proposals
and 52,006 samples. Dual witnesses, decoder coefficients, supported paths,
complete coverage, costs and scope are independently checked. The
transitive trust gate uses only the standard Lean axioms.

This removes a finite recovery and temporal-feasibility gap without
selecting M1's metric menu or its particular meaning. Native recoverability
extends to the whole connected component. The causal-limit condition
$h_q/a_q\to0$, with $h_q$ proportional to $L/q$, also holds for every
$a_q=Lq^{-\alpha}$ with $0<\alpha<1$ when the declared layer clock is
$a_q/c$. That conclusion alone cannot select the square-root exponent.
Choosing an objective that balances these errors would be an additional
selection rule, not the canonical A3 optimization. The interpretation map,
record domain, physical guard, protection, precision and clock require
their source derivations. Full-state tomography also does not identify its
raw write history with M1's semantic read-from order or replace the separate
version-preserving bank construction. Physical spacetime identification is
a separate premise. These results do not constitute a countermodel to the
full three-axiom basis.
