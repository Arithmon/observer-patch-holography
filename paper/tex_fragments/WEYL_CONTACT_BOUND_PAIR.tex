\begin{proposition}[Isolated pair band on the declared lattice]
Let $b=2\sqrt3c$ and $U>b$. On a periodic box with $V$ sites, exactly $V$
eigenvalues of $H$ lie in the upper band, all at least $U$, while every
remaining eigenvalue is at most $b$. Thus the separation is at least $U-b$.
There is one upper-band state in each total-momentum fiber.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The symbol obeys $h(k)^2=c^2\sum_j\sin^2(k_j)I$, so $\|T\|\le b$.
A single hop separates an on-site pair, hence $PTP=0$. The quadratic form
of $H$ on $\operatorname{Ran}P$ is exactly $U$, while on its orthogonal
complement it is that of $T$ and is at most $b$. Min--max gives the two
bounds and the band count. In each momentum fiber $P$ has rank one.
\end{proof}

Writing $s=(0,1,-1,0)/\sqrt2$ and $d_K(k)=s/\sqrt V$, the free fiber is

\begin{align*}

 &Ug(E,K)&=1,\\

       \langle s,(E-B_K(k))^{-2}s\rangle\right]^{-1},
 &\psi_K&=U\sqrt{Z(K)}(E-T_K)^{-1}d_K.
\end{align*}
The resolvent is positive above the free spectrum and $g$ is strictly
decreasing there. The isolated pole is therefore unique. The receipt keeps
