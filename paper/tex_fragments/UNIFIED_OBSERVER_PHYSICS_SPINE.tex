\section*{Carrier signature, screens and observers}

The tower panel above orders the layers. Three conventions are shared by
every layer that uses the carrier.

\subsection*{Three meanings of screen}

The word ``screen'' is used for three related objects that are identified
only through an explicit map.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The \emph{local carrier boundary} is the twelve-port oriented interface
of one icosahedral carrier. Its incidence has \((V,E,F)=(12,30,20)\).
\item The \emph{federation screen} is the routed system of interfaces,
records, repairs and checkpoints of many carriers at finite cutoff.
\item The \emph{support screen} is the observer-facing geometric chart. On
the spherical branch it is the refined conformal \(S^2\) used for caps,
collars, modular flow and Lorentz reconstruction.
\end{enumerate}

Local icosahedral incidence does not determine the topology of the federation
nerve. Identical local carriers can be routed as a path, a cycle, a
higher-genus complex or a spherical complex. The map from routed carriers to
a support-visible spherical nerve is a physical bridge with its own premises.

\subsection*{Presentation invariance and architecture sensitivity}

OPH is invariant under changes of presentation that preserve the complete
observer-visible carrier signature. On the icosahedral branch that
signature is
\[
\mathcal C_{i,r}=
\bigl(
\mathcal A_{i,r},\rho_{i,r},P_{i,r},I_{i,r}^{\rm or},
\mathcal R_{i,r},\mathcal U_{i,r},\mathsf{Chk}_{i,r},
\mathsf{Resp}_{i,r},c_{sr}
\bigr),
\]
with accessible algebra, state, port set, oriented incidence, record
algebra, repair or feedback interface, checkpoints, visible response law and
refinement lineage. Hidden coordinates, port names, worker partitions,
materials and wiring presentations are silent when an isomorphism preserves
this tuple and its error model. A change of port number, incidence,
orientation, accessible algebra, response, repair law, clock or refinement
lineage can change the admitted physics. A cube and an icosahedron are
different carrier contracts even when built from the same material.

\subsection*{When a carrier is an observer}

A carrier body realizes an observer when it supplies bounded access,
self-readback, durable records, record-conditioned feedback, boundary
prediction against controls and checkpoint continuation. One carrier may
pass that test, or a connected subfederation may pass it instead; no theorem
fixes the primitive observer size by counting carrier bodies. Equal-frequency
phase locking can synchronize an interface; it makes the interface an
observer only together with these record and feedback clauses.

\subsection*{How the layers compose}

Consensus, geometry and gravity are composable branches of one carrier
tower: accepted repair produces the quotient-visible normal form, the exact
port response supplies the rank-three slow-response carrier, and the
support screen feeds the modular and Lorentz constructions of the spacetime
layer. The gauge layer uses the same carrier architecture, where the
complete twelve-port response and endogenous holonomy force the abstract
local Lie algebra \(\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus
\mathfrak{su}(3)\). The matrix current and matter action are conditional
realizations; a common source construction joining them to the geometric
tower is the matter layer's open join. Matching dimensions or symmetry
labels does not supply that join.
