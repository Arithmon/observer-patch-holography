# Exact source-charge obstruction for the first completion campaign

This is a bounded CP-03 / [#1139](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1139)
result. On both the full native 78-state total-two source and its invariant
66-state binary shell, the unital *-algebra generated by the complete C
branches is the full matrix algebra. The same holds separately for E.
Consequently a source-only Hermitian charge commuting with every admitted
branch is scalar, in every quantum basis. There is no nontrivial exactly
branch-preserved **reducing** source sector in this class.

This does not accept the full CP-03 gate or falsify the campaign. A localized
metastable object, a charge involving the record/controller, a growing-carrier
sector, and active correction have different mathematical contracts. They are
not excluded here. In particular the binary shell is a genuine invariant
subspace of the full process; it is not a reducing sector because collisions
can enter it. No lifetime or physical clock/energy identification is asserted.

## Exact model and proof

The source is the existing twelve-port, thirty-seam, total-load-two carrier.
Each complete raw branch has Kraus matrix `K=M/sqrt(2)` for a fixed seam,
where `M` is an integer partial injection. Both fair-coin outcomes and every
raw record, including zero-probability outcomes, are retained. The producer
reuses and checks the existing complete C/E maps in
[`native_defect_decision.py`](../common_source/native_defect_decision.py).

Let `A` be the unital *-algebra generated by those `M`. For each branch,
`D=M* M` is a diagonal projection onto its input support. The certificate
selects a small collection of such supports whose membership patterns are
distinct for all basis states. For the pattern of state `i`, the product of
`D` or `I-D` according to that pattern is exactly `P_i=|i><i|`. Thus every
basis projector belongs to `A`; this is an exact support calculation.

For every native transition `i -> j` in a branch,
`P_j M P_i=|j><i|`. Its adjoint also belongs to `A`. A retained spanning tree
of the undirected transition graph therefore constructs every matrix unit by
products along paths. The connected full and binary domains give respectively
`A=M78(C)` and `A=M66(C)`, of complex dimensions 6084 and 4356. Their
commutants are exactly the scalar operators. No numerical rank, eigenvalue
tolerance or sampling enters the certificate.

A Hermitian `Q` commuting with the branches also commutes with their adjoints,
so belongs to that scalar commutant. Equivalently, a projection reducing every
branch is zero or identity. This condition says that each source charge sector
and its complement are preserved by every branch; it is stronger than forward
invariance of one subspace. Arbitrary basis rotations do not evade it.

Commutation with every branch must also be distinguished from conservation
**in expectation**, `sum_h K_h* Q K_h=Q`. A general Hermitian fixed observable
of a channel need not commute with its Kraus operators when the stationary
state is not faithful. For example, an absorbing three-state process with
`1 -> 0` or `2` equally likely fixes the observable `diag(0,1/2,1)` in
expectation while its branch *-algebra is full. The certificate here classifies
commuting charges and reducing projections, not arbitrary Heisenberg fixed
observables. The existing uniform-source forgetting theorem can separately
constrain the latter on its own exogenous/discarded-record domain. In contrast,
a **projection** fixed by a trace-preserving channel must reduce every Kraus
branch: its zero and one eigenspaces each have zero probability to cross.

## Extension to the bounded family

At a classical controller state, changing a positive probability rescales a
Kraus generator without changing its algebra. C/E mixing is a classical tagged
choice, not a coherent superposition of instruments. The raw E branches obey
the checked integer identity

`M_C(raw)=M_E(raw)+M_E(raw+4)`.

Consequently E's generators contain every C generator as a linear combination.
This covers the pure C and pure E endpoints, every tagged convex mixture, and
even different supported C/E choices on different seams. Adding an identity
hold branch also leaves the algebra unchanged. A strictly positive repair
probability and exploration probability for every seam keep this support at
every nonhalted controller state.

The [frozen finite campaign](campaign.json), whose hash the receipt binds,
sets repair and uniform-exploration weights at least
`1/16`, rational parameters of denominator at most 256, bounded classical
FIFO/controller memory. These are stronger conditions than needed for this
exact obstruction.
The result needs only positivity and complete C/E support. It therefore applies
to a fixed charge `Q_source tensor I_memory` despite classical feedback, without
claiming that the adaptive process has the stationary law or mixing rate of
the exogenous uniform process. A controller-dependent or history-dependent
charge is outside this conclusion.

The campaign also admits an explicit full-buffer halt. Once permanently
halted, the source is unchanged and its arbitrary observables are preserved.
This theorem concerns the admitted **active repair** branches of a continuing
process; freezing the source by terminating observation is a failure/control,
not evidence of a defect protected during continuing repair. No uniform
all-attempt decay or lifetime bound is inferred across such halts.

## Controls, nearest results and what would have to change

The binary projector has 120 incoming collision-to-binary branch addresses
for each law and zero outgoing binary-to-collision addresses. Its nonzero
commutator explicitly prevents confusing an invariant face with a reducing
charge sector.

Deleting all five seams incident to port zero changes the support. Its
occupation becomes conserved; the full *-algebra decomposes into blocks of
sizes 1, 11 and 66, with commutant dimension three. This is an exact
changed-schedule control outside the positive-exploration campaign, not a
native defect or a proposed successful physical mechanism. With only identity
operations even the diagonal-separation premise fails. Distinguishing basis
states without connecting them also fails to generate the full algebra.

The nearest existing results are the classical and collision-subspace
exclusions in the native defect decision, and arbitrary-source quantum
forgetting in [`QUANTUM_MIXING.md`](../observer/QUANTUM_MIXING.md) and
[`record_laws`](../record_laws/README.md). The latter discard records and use
declared exogenous schedules. The addition here is an explicit full
branch-algebra certificate, including basis-independent charges and its
support-wise applicability to classical adaptive control. It is not a new
abstract commutant theorem or an adaptive mixing theorem.

Changing only mixture weights, positive holding, or the classical memory rule
cannot restore a fixed exactly conserved source-only charge while all complete
C/E branch supports remain. Evading this specific obstruction requires changing
at least one of those premises: remove or alter branch support; change the
state/observable domain (for example with a justified additional dynamical
degree of freedom); use a record/controller-dependent charge; or target
controlled lifetime rather than exact branch conservation. Coherent recovery
also changes the admitted operation grammar and must be constructed and costed.
None of these logical escape routes is an exhibited localized defect, energy
law or particle. An added memory label alone would not establish one.
