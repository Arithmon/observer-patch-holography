# Implementing the approach audit

This round connects the continuing observer to an unobserved environment,
adds controlled occupation removal, and turns a calibration ambiguity into
an inexpensive intervention. Exact source identities and shared compilation
caches shorten execution. Supplied apparatus and candidate action families
remain premises; these changes do not uniquely select physical laws from A1–A3.

## A continuing open observer

The [open-history construction](common_source/OPEN_HISTORY.md), contributed
in [PR 1107](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/1107),
extends the canonical observer program to mixed work/reference states.
Independent discarded phase draws occur at declared boundaries. Every visible
record survives further reads, feedback, checkpoints and native memory recovery.
Hidden draw labels neither enter feedback nor become postselection conditions.

Independent review checks complete channels, reference tensor order, abort
branches, checkpoint continuation and theorem hypotheses. The environment law,
variance, boundary schedule and physical service errors remain declared inputs.

## Controlled occupation removal

The [occupation-control packet](occupation_control/README.md) applies an
existing contractive Lindblad defect theorem to the supplied pulse apparatus.
It encloses the full continuous polynomial-path defect, occupation boundary,
endpoint jumps, thermal initialization, preparation displacement and pointer
readout. Arithmetic is included under its stated IEEE-754 contract. This is
a semantic evaluator certificate, not a certificate for the separate native
compiler's floating execution or laboratory calibration.

Both 32- and 64-level windows are retained. At 64 levels the case-a selected
estimator radius is about `9.91e-8` at 0.4 ms, `0.0163` at 2 ms, `0.211` at
5 ms and `1.68` at 10 ms. The 32-level conditional certificate becomes
unavailable at 5 and 10 ms. The stronger early bound and weaker late bounds
are both meaningful findings. Agreement between two cutoffs is not used as
an occupation certificate. A separately retained unrestricted-versus-exact-
finite radius supports later composition with valid native execution bounds.

## Informative action probes

The [identification-design packet](identification_design/README.md) computes
the exact response rank/nullspace for an explicitly supplied correction
family. Four-level access leaves the audit's `binom(N,4)` term invisible.
Coherent probes involving levels four and five expose the corresponding
blind directions, leaving the unconditional global-energy gauge.

A locked component experiment executes a 41-pulse native preparation,
complete diagonal evolution and phase-sensitive read. Two candidate actions
produce different complete laws; occupation commitment before the read erases
the distinction. Independent reference checks, phase-alias controls and an
ideal independent-trial sampling calculation accompany the result. Its
component sampling guarantee is not transferred to a noncommuting coupled
field or real hardware.

The efficient workflow is to identify invisible directions, select an
intervention with useful separation, evaluate the candidate laws, and then
compile a locked source experiment. Increasing precision on insensitive
probes cannot distinguish those alternatives.

## Shorter words and stable repeated execution

Production now uses exact finite-code identities: controlled occupation
parity takes two native pulses including its scalar phase; number drift takes
one scalar pulse plus one Z pulse per bit. At dimension 64 these replace
2,054- and 838-pulse generic words with 2 and 7 pulses. They do not use
unrestricted oscillator commutators at the cutoff. Live word hashes and
resource receipts are regenerated. Dense basis words still dominate cold
compilation.

Inverse-word arithmetic left small nonunitarity that accumulated until
Windows CI rejected a complete detector law. The final ideal pulse matrix
now receives the fixed-guard polar representation already used by the native
library. Raw, increment and corrected target checks remain mandatory, with
a bound on the total representation change. No state or observation enters
this correction; the evolving density matrix is not renormalized. Correction
norms remain numerical diagnostics rather than hardware-error bounds.

The repository verifier executes both empirical canonical CLIs in one child
process, sharing parameter-keyed source compilation caches. Both still run
their complete predictions, scores and checks. Every declared receipt remains
in the inventory, and failure stops execution. Predictions, fitted parameters
and trajectory states are not reused.

## Remaining attachments

The published measurement normalization and full laboratory timing remain
unauthenticated. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04744) still lists only
its original version. Missing raw counts are not inferred and no rescaling
is selected from residuals. Keep the audit's stronger effective-Hamiltonian
comparator in future empirical protocol designs.

For fixed-band scalar exploration, the existing exact-gradient endpoint is
`GalerkinParameters(grid=None)`. Larger quadrature grids add no field modes.
Useful spatial extension needs more modes, matched observation maps and
controlled omitted-mode effects. The planar cube-graph control still excludes
an inference of physical dimension from adjacency alone.

The new design tool selects informative tests within candidate families.
Source-derivedness additionally needs a source/interface restriction excluding
a competing law, followed by a discriminating observation. The
[source-selection contract](SOURCE_SELECTION.md) owns that obligation.
Reuse universal compilation and finite-instrument dilation while directing
new proofs at selection, clock and metric attachments.

Historical calibration and dated-audit inputs remain byte-identical compressed
snapshots. Live receipts are regenerated from their canonical producers;
calibration is not refreshed using later observed outcomes.
