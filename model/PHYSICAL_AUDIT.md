# Physical interpretation of the executable model

Audit baseline: `34d07c5756618cab969a8c8a5c91072f368a68ae` (18 October 2026).

The construction derives compatible
finite observations from one specified repair process. It has not established
physical 3+1 spacetime, a selected Lorentzian field action, or a measured
native-event correspondence with a laboratory clock. The distinction matters
for deciding what to build next: more carriers do not discharge any of these
three obligations by themselves.

## Are federations necessary?

**No federation is needed for the present local derivations.** One twelve-port
carrier supports overlapping observer regions, the C/E instruments,
conditional histories, local clocks, response geometry, coherence tests and
repair-derived actions. Agreement between regions can be tested inside this
carrier. A proof can quantify over such regions without implementing a
network of larger observers.

A federation becomes useful when a claim concerns larger operational regions,
inter-carrier interventions, scalability, frame compatibility or refinement.
For an extended effective spacetime, some construction must support the
claimed observation domain with controlled approximation errors. A theorem
about an abstract family can provide this if the actual source satisfies its
hypotheses. The present spherical federation is one candidate family; it is
not a prerequisite for every geometry, action or clock theorem.

The [federation evidence](federated_observer/README.md) is valuable as a test
of composition and coarse memory. It also exposes the particular family's
limitations: routing is supplied, coarse blocks retain memory, and existing
ancestry maps do not preserve every seam. Its approximately quadratic native
ball growth over part of the measured range does not establish a
three-dimensional bulk. Building larger copies before specifying the needed
observable correspondence would leave the physical question unchanged.

The separate [OL-A1 signature replication instrument](../code/instrument_specs/OL_A1_SIGNATURE_REPLICATION_SPEC.md)
also remains `FAILED`. Its threshold-sensitive inertia and nondiscriminating
ancestry control do not establish physical signature. That result is not
overturned by successful finite native-carrier calculations or by this audit.

## Contributions of this audit

The [space/time calculation](physical_space_time/README.md) adds an exact
first-arrival construction using the same native carrier and a declared
one-particle preparation. It also checks a signal/clock obstruction in the
original total-two sector: remote repairs can propagate a difference before
a receiver's first local tick. A one-seam-per-attempt dependency bound is
therefore not a one-seam-per-receiver-tick light cone. These results provide
an operational passage-time geometry and expose the extra hypothesis a
metric-time claim would need; they are not a general no-go theorem for
relativistic effective descriptions.

The [action calculation](repair_action_audit/README.md) extends the C/E
analysis to the admissible coherence-table family on the binary sector. It checks the conditions
for a self-adjoint positive repair form and for a Hamiltonian commutator
part. A phase choice in the complete law can supply an interacting
Hamiltonian commutator component in its Hilbert–Schmidt decomposition while
leaving classical current histories unchanged.
This is constructive action information given that law and scheduling
parameter. Classical repair alone does not choose the phase or the physical
action.

The [laboratory-clock contract](laboratory_clock/README.md) solves a finite
affine event/clock calibration problem with explicit interval uncertainty,
then checks held-out observations against the frozen calibration set.
Synthetic controls and retained software timings have separate provenance.
It supplies an executable acceptance test for future paired laboratory
records; it does not manufacture those records or certify traceability from
a metadata label.

The [instrument control](record_laws/INSTRUMENT_CONTRACT.md) corrects an
ambiguity in the record-law exposition. Its four assumptions classify
**unread channels**. A unitary remix of two C Kraus branches preserves that
channel on all 6,084 carrier matrix units while changing the committed
outcomes and mixing currents within a record. Current-history claims also
need the specified definite-current instrument. Phase covariance is an
additional assumption beyond classical conservation. The executed C/E
instruments satisfy that assumption and retain all their existing results.

## What does the empirical work decide?

The [identifiability controls](identifiability_checks/README.md) show why
the oscillator comparisons do not select the native C/E law: the
compiled apparatus uses supplied preparation, Hamiltonian and detector
services, and the scored work-only output is blind to the relevant native
coherence distinction. Compiling it through a consistent repair history
checks implementation without making that distinction observable.

The [preparation diagnostics](empirical_diagnostics/README.md) retain the
125-point comparison's RMSE `0.287643` and unresolved readout normalization.
A constructed alternative preparation reproduces the old read while
predicting different additional probes. Those new probes are predictions,
not collected measurements. This supports experimental design and
limits the physical claim.

The native [closed prediction protocol](closed_prediction_protocol/README.md)
offers a more direct C/E discriminator with complete failures,
finite preparation error and explicit apparatus tolerances. Its apparatus
contract requires a physical implementation and independent validation.
Neither a successful fit using a supplied Hamiltonian nor successful CI is
an OPH-specific experimental confirmation.

## Decision and next acceptance test

Continue the single-carrier route for the immediate physics questions.
Use federations only when the next test explicitly needs a larger domain.
Uniqueness among every possible repair law is not required to test one
candidate; its instrument, state, controls and clocks do need to be fixed
before validation.

The next decisive construction should use one declared preparation and
complete instrument to predict **joint signal, field and clock records**.
Choose the operational spatial and temporal maps in advance, retain hidden
events and failures, and establish their error bounds on the domain claimed.
The native action must predict those same records under the chosen clock
map, including coherent interventions that can distinguish the remaining
record-law freedom. Identify these records with physical detector and clock
observables, calibrate the declared unit/parameter freedoms using existing
measurements, then test unused observation protocols without refitting.
A physical emulator is an optional implementation test; paired microscopic
repair-event timestamps are not a prerequisite for comparing the proposed
Universe with public observations. The
[observed-Universe protocol](../docs/research/NATIVE_CLOCK_EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md)
states this route, and the [public-data audit](../docs/research/PUBLIC_DATA_PREDICTION_AUDIT.md)
separates the conditional ACT result from a native-observer derivation.
Extend the operational domain only with a demonstrated compatibility or
approximation bound.

The audit therefore supports the mathematical direction while narrowing the
next target. The three physical completion claims remain open; their
missing premises are explicit and the new controls make several tempting
but invalid shortcuts testable.
