# Four shared source events for a clock comparison

This [frozen finite campaign](campaign.json) extends the two-attempt
[clock-count readout](../CLOCK_COUNT_INFERENCE.md) to a continuing C/E
history with four common source events. It makes the clock and field outputs
observable on the same retained native prefix, including after three FIFO
exports. The new clock service compiles four transported-core words at modular
parameter **one**. The existing parameter-four words are a control: their
phase wraps before the third checkpoint and their raw contrast ceases to be
monotone. Neither parameter is a duration in seconds.

## One process and complete stopping instruments

The binary total-two carrier is `H66`. Its repair law is the unchanged C or E
endpoint. At each attempted repair the existing observer accepts a supplied
`preferred` seam while retaining its exploration floor `1/2`. The seams are
`(0,7)`, `(6,7)`, `(2,6)`, `(2,3)` in that order. One declared feedback
record at each step continues; **every** other hold, exploration, coin,
current and contrast record stops with its full native output, memory and
unmeasured field. The selected record has weight `1/4` at each step, so its
ideal prefix masses are `1/4`, `1/16`, `1/64`, `1/256`. This intervention
changes the proposal, not C/E's native update. The selected history transports
the core through `(1,7)`, `(1,6)`, `(1,2)`, `(1,3)`. Its one-slot FIFO has
exported zero, one, two and three native records at the four checkpoints.
No selected source or protected field is reset during a run.

The initial source input is a binary basis state rotated by an **executed
1,036-pulse** pair-preparation word into a coherent superposition of native
configurations 55 and 65. Both survive all four selected repairs; their
transported reference-weight ratio is `5/8` at every core. This pure probe is
**not** a preparation of the faithful modular reference. On each selected
repair, a newly compiled, fully certified **2,042-pulse** parameter-one word
acts on the continuing source. Successful export then advances the protected
source-compiled density wave by one tick. A clock export abort retains the
wave at its previous tick. At the chosen terminal checkpoint, a freshly
certified pair-analyzer word and a Ramsey read give four joint outcomes,
plus analyzer export abort. The source preparation abort and every native
stopping outcome remain separate. The processor proper-code complement
outcomes retain the inherited contract and have ideal zero probability on
admitted inputs.

The four terminal horizons are **separate stopping experiments** on copies of
the same prepared protocol. Earlier probes are not measured and discarded in
the runs that continue to later checkpoints. Each source replay enumerates
all semantic native branches at every reached stage, including zero maps,
checks complete mass, and records a digest of all stopped native keys. The
[receipt](results.json) retains both C and E event maps, source diagnostic
probability bands, pulse counts and exact source-error bounds. A separate
reference-bearing test checks that the complete fixed-seam native menu
preserves off-diagonal untouched-reference entries.

## The readouts and the finite theorem premises

For each retained checkpoint event `e_n`, `n=1..4`, hide its native tape from
the readout and use the **conditional ensemble** probability of the first
clock-analyzer code or of Ramsey minus. The same complete native prefix and
FIFO export list is the public event key for both. The target values are:

| Shared event | Clock first-code probability | Field Ramsey minus probability |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.054216677723 | `1/22` |
| 2 | 0.205108918319 | `19/110` |
| 3 | 0.419953217871 | `3529/9900` |
| 4 | 0.652156999780 | `41609/74250` |

The clock entries are outward rational enclosures of
`(1-cos(n log(8/5)))/2`; the field entries come from the original native
arc-walk recurrence. Both sequences are strictly increasing, even after the
conservative source-conditional allowances. They therefore meet the
**order and strict readout** part of
[ClockReadout](../../../Lean/Time/ClockReadout.lean) when a record is a
checkpoint *type with its conditional ensemble expectation*. An individual
coin or Ramsey bit is stochastic and is not itself a monotone clock. Finite
sampling would need an additional accepted-trial confidence certificate;
none is represented as observed here.

The two distinct shared events 1 and 2 determine the unique positive affine
interpolation of these **raw probability readouts**, with rate about
`0.843467674481` and offset about `-0.000275469623`. The predeclared third
comparison event 4 fails the exact cross-multiplication condition in
[ClockComparison](../../../Lean/Time/ClockComparison.lean): its field-minus
probability exceeds the affine prediction by about `0.010592694012`.
Event 3 also fails without refitting. Rational phase enclosures prove a
positive cross product, and the complete source-error/conditioning budget
keeps its lower bound positive. This is a concrete source-backed test of the
finite theorem's **affine-consistency predicate**. It does not show that the
clocks lack a shared event parameter: their detector response functions are
nonlinear. Source-model response inversion identifies the count label from
either disjoint mean band, and the resulting count-to-count affine relation
passes by construction. That lookup is model-conditioned, not an independently
established clock rate.

The full `TwoSharedEventComparison` structure in Lean also asks for supplied
`AffineTimelikeHistory` charts. A one-chart atlas made from each readout would
be a valid *formal diagnostic chart* like the library's three-record control;
this experiment derives no physical event atlas or unit-timelike trajectory.
The executable packet verifies the shared-event map, strict order and
cross-product rather than claiming a new formal Lean model instance or a
proper-time theorem. Host execution time, native attempts, modular
parameter, source wave ticks, ensemble response and calibrated observer time
remain different quantities.

## Bounds, resources and remaining join

[PrivateInner](../../../Lean/Dynamics/PrivateInner.lean) applies to each
specified faithful diagonal transported reference and makes its finite
modular action unitary. The fresh source compiler/certifier then bounds each
actual word and each pair rotation by `10^-10` in operator norm on all 512
address columns, including padding. The complete native Kraus maps and
success/export-abort split meet the finite
[ChoiCPTP](../../../Lean/Dynamics/ChoiCPTP.lean) composition premises;
these are applications with checked model hypotheses, not new Lean proofs.
The field service uses the inherited pulse-product bound. At event 4 the
conservative conditional joint-read total-variation allowance is below
`0.000385`, and interval propagation still puts the raw affine cross product
above `0.00147`. These guards concern executed software arrays and the
specified source routine model; they are not interval proofs of numerical
hardware or physical pulse fidelity.

A four-event successful path uses four new clock words, the pair preparation
and one terminal analyzer: **10,246 clock-source pulses** at horizon four.
Terminal analyzer words vary from 1,042 to 1,048 pulses across checkpoints. The controlled
four-step field service uses **532,322 source pulses**, for **542,568** in
the combined successful path and twice that many coherent processor
load/export calls. There are four native attempted repairs, three exported
records, one retained FIFO slot, a nine-qubit clock-address register and a
sixteen-qubit field/Ramsey/scratch register; the shared source processor has
six levels. Four parameter-one word programs, the pair rotations, controller
stage, program counters, FIFO and exported tape are distinct storage costs.
The overall apparatus is not shown to fit the 256-state repair policy, and
physical transfer, pulse and memory errors remain supplied parameters.
Other transported cores at parameter one lack compiled words: the complete
stopping protocol terminates before requesting them. It does not provide a
full all-core parameter-one continuing service.

The result supplies a continuing, source-executed **shared-event comparison
of finite ensemble readouts**. Regional physical clock compatibility,
proper-time calibration, a whole-3960-arc source compiler and physical
seconds remain open.
