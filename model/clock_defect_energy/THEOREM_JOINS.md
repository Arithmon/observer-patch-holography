## From exchange history to a particle

The positive result is a nontrivial class of collision-free native paths.
Odd exchange survives the declared stutter, backtrack, single-particle face
and disjoint-move square deformations. Its double is an explicit integral
boundary. The computation does not assert the full fundamental group.

Three particle requirements remain distinct:

1. **A live localized excitation.** A completed path class does not define a
   protected sector of the instantaneous source. The tested adjacent pair
   disperses and fails the reference-like exterior comparison.
2. **A coherent exchange response.** The committed C/E records distinguish
   histories. Multiplying one complete history block by a scalar sign leaves
   its channel unchanged. Deriving the sign of a measurable coherent exchange
   requires an actual amplitude/intervention map; `Z/2` alone does not choose
   bosonic or fermionic statistics.
3. **One dynamical energy.** The positive current form and conditional field
   mode provide reusable action and propagation ingredients. They must be
   attached to the same excitation before its rest energy, inertial response
   or gravitational stress can identify a particle.
