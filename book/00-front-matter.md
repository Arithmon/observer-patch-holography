## How to read the claims

This book tells one story about what the world is. A complete self-consistent history and the computation carried by its own local interactions are one reality. No external computer and no extra time play that history. Events, changing local states and records belong to the structure itself. A complete quantum history assigns no simultaneous outcomes to incompatible measurements nobody made, and a closure condition on the whole history says nothing about its inhabitants approaching a final instantaneous state.

Space, durations, fields and matter are readouts of that structure, and the chapters build each of them out of observers repairing their disagreements. Chapter nine derives the move every repair makes and names the interference experiments that read how much quantum coherence a repair keeps. Where a number in these pages was measured before the arithmetic that lands on it, the book says so.

The finite constructions establish conditional charge representations, interaction tensors and common field descriptions. Identifying those representations with the particles in a laboratory requires their dynamics and measured spectral response. The observed mass spectrum and a repair-selected responsive geometry are not derived by the finite identities alone. A condition used by a theorem and an executable example satisfying that condition have different scopes, and the chapters distinguish them.

Locality forbids one thing: information reaching a local update from anywhere but its own neighborhood. Work counters, conventional orderings and global calculations used to check a construction are tools in the hands of whoever does the checking. There is no universal clock. An update count, a causal order, a coordinate, a modular parameter and a clock reading are different things, and moving between any two of them takes a map with a name on it. An observer need not be conscious. The last chapters, on experience and on ethics, take further steps, and they say where each step is taken.

Appendix B gives the working definitions, and the [OPH interpretation and working terms](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/main/docs/INTERPRETATION_AND_TERMS.md) use the same vocabulary alongside the papers.
